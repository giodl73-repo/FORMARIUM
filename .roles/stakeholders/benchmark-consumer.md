# Benchmark Consumer

## Key question

*Can another project reproduce this result without importing FACTOR internals
or accepting its preferred representation?*

## Required evidence

- Portable schema, corpus, split, and result packets.
- One command or documented sequence for validation.
- Strong conventional controls and exact alias records.
- Explicit licenses, non-goals, and interpretation boundaries.
- At least one independent implementation or consumer target.
