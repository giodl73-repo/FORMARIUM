# Benchmark Numeracy Checker

## Key question

*Do counts, denominators, storage, parameters, and uncertainty support the
comparison?*

## Lens

- Exact-match and per-factor denominators are explicit.
- Logical payload, container bytes, metadata, and model parameters are separate.
- Train and holdout counts reconcile with corpus custody.
- Timing includes repetitions and uncertainty when it becomes a decision metric.
- No scalar score hides opposing cost dimensions.
