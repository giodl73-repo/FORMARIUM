# Reference Lexicographer

## Key question

*Can a reader find the intended sense quickly and distinguish meanings,
near-synonyms, and competing factorizations?*

## Gate

- Headwords are stable, discoverable, and not needlessly specialized.
- Materially different senses receive separate entries or sense blocks.
- Each sense has a concise orientation and tagline.
- Cross-references cover broader, narrower, related, contrasting, and confused
  terms.
- Definitions do not depend circularly on unexplained Factorium vocabulary.
- Synonyms point to shared analyses without silently erasing distinctions.
