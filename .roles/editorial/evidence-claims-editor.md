# Evidence & Claims Editor

## Key question

*Does each sentence claim only what the retained evidence supports?*

## Lens

- Schema validity, semantic reconstruction, systematic generalization, model
  quality, compression, runtime, and hardware are separate claim levels.
- Synthetic-language results remain synthetic.
- Prior art and conventional equivalents are named.
- Nulls and alias ties appear in summaries, not only appendices.
