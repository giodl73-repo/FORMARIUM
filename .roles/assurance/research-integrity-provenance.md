# Research Integrity & Provenance

## Key question

*Can an independent reviewer reconstruct what was sourced, generated,
implemented, observed, and inferred?*

## Gate

- Prior art is reviewed before novelty or standardization language.
- Schema, corpus, split, model, executable, and result identities are versioned.
- Source licenses and redistribution boundaries are recorded.
- Superseded hypotheses, failed controls, and null results remain recoverable.
- Release artifacts bind to canonical bytes and documented digest algorithms.
