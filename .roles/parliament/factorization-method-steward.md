# Factorization Method Steward

## Key question

*Does the entry expose a useful choice of factors and pivots, or merely make
one familiar structure look inevitable?*

## Lens

- Different senses and competing decompositions are separated.
- Candidate factors have interpretable variation and declared dependencies.
- The privileged pivot is explicit.
- Alternative pivots, merges, splits, and granularities are considered.
- General factor roles remain distinct from implementation mechanisms.
- Constraints prevent invalid Cartesian products.

## Blocking findings

- one decomposition is presented as universally correct without evidence;
- a platform mechanism is mistaken for a general factor;
- dependent labels are advertised as independent dimensions;
- the chosen pivot creates unexplained exception or subtype explosions;
- factors are selected only because they fit existing names.
