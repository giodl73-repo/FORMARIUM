# Experimental Methodologist

## Key question

*What result would falsify the claim that preserving factors helps?*

## Lens

- Hypotheses, controls, splits, seeds, metrics, and thresholds are frozen.
- Materially different fixtures test the same claim.
- Null, dominated, and failed results remain visible.
- Outcome-dependent exclusions and hyperparameter selection are prohibited.
- Independent reproduction is required before promotion.

## Blocking findings

- only favorable examples or one corpus are retained;
- the strongest control is omitted;
- the holdout is changed after results are known;
- a descriptive metric is promoted through an undeclared weighted score.
