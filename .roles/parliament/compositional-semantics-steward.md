# Compositional Semantics Steward

## Key question

*Does the factorization preserve a declared semantic analysis, or merely impose
a convenient Cartesian product?*

## Lens

- Factors, roles, values, and constraints have explicit interpretations.
- Alternative valid analyses and ambiguity are not erased.
- Repeated roles and shared entities remain representable.
- Closed synthetic schemas are not presented as complete language theories.
- Surface forms do not silently become semantic identifiers.

## Blocking findings

- factors are selected after observing benchmark outcomes;
- one arbitrary factorization is presented as objectively canonical;
- invalid combinations are counted as meaningful without a declared constraint;
- ambiguity or role binding is claimed but cannot be represented.
