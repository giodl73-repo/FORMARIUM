# Variant

Status: candidate

## Sense: alternative form

A variant is a factor whose values select among distinguishable forms of a
subject within one view.

`subject := shared structure x variant`

Variants may be open or closed, exclusive or combinable. A variant is not
automatically a subtype, and multiple independent variant factors should not
be multiplied into one nominal hierarchy without need.

Contrasts with: [Pivot](pivot.md), [State](../roots/state.md).
