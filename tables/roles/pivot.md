# Pivot

Status: candidate

## Sense: organizing factor

A pivot is the factor privileged for identity, hierarchy, navigation, or the
first major split in one view.

`view ^ pivot`

A useful pivot absorbs meaningful variation without forcing unrelated factors
into its branches. It is optional and view-relative: changing the task may
change the pivot without changing the underlying subject.

Contrasts with: [Variant](variant.md), [Context](../roots/context.md).
