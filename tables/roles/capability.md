# Capability

Status: candidate

## Sense: supported behavior

A capability is a factor describing what a subject can do, provide, or
participate in.

`subject := identity x capability set`

Capabilities often vary independently of nominal kind and may be assigned to
interfaces, traits, protocols, permissions, services, or documented
competencies. The mechanism is not the capability itself.
