# Editorial Maturity Scale

Status: candidate Scale Table

Canonical headword:
[Formarium Entry Publication](../entries/formarium-entry-publication.md)

Primary family: Scale Table

Purpose: interpret Formarium entry maturity without converting it into an
automatic score or publication state.

## Scale

| Level | Interpretation | Minimum evidence | Reader posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| `candidate` | useful proposal needing broader review | scoped sources, explicit limits, initial role review | use cautiously; expect revision |
| `supported` | credible in more than one relevant setting | multiple independent sources/examples and closed major findings | use with stated scope |
| `established` | repeatedly used with documented support | durable domain acceptance, repeated application, revision history | rely within declared authority |
| `disputed` | materially competing interpretations remain | named alternatives and evidence on each side | preserve alternatives; do not force consensus |
| `deprecated` | retained for history but no longer recommended | superseding entry or evidence and migration guidance | avoid new use; follow replacement |

## Rules

- Levels are not evenly spaced numbers.
- `disputed` and `deprecated` are not lower numeric scores.
- Publication and maturity are separate dimensions.
- A strong source can establish a formula while Formarium's presentation
  remains `candidate`.
- Automated checks cannot upgrade maturity.
- Every change records rationale, evidence, reviewers, and effective revision.
