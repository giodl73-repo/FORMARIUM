# Constraint

Status: candidate

## Sense: restriction on valid possibility

Constraint limits, requires, or forbids values, combinations, transitions, or
outcomes.

`constraint := scope x condition x force x consequence`

Treat constraint as prime when its rule is already explicit. Factor it further
when source, priority, exceptions, enforcement, or conflict resolution changes
what is permitted.
