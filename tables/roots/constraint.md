# Constraint

Status: candidate

## Sense: restriction on valid possibility

Constraint limits, requires, or forbids values, combinations, transitions, or
outcomes.

`constraint := scope x condition x force x consequence`

Treat constraint as prime when its rule is already explicit. Factor it further
when source, priority, exceptions, enforcement, or conflict resolution changes
what is permitted.

Related: [Possibility](possibility.md), [Purpose](purpose.md),
[Authorization policy](../primes/authorization-policy.md).

## Sense: structural role

As a factor role, constraint removes invalid values, combinations, or
transitions from an otherwise broader view.

`factorization ! constraint`

Constraints differ from policies that rank or select among otherwise valid
possibilities.
