# Identity

Status: candidate

## Sense: distinguishing continuity

Identity answers what something is taken to be and what makes observations
refer to the same thing across contexts or time.

`identity := distinguishing attributes x continuity rule @ context`

Treat identity as prime when a table only needs a stable referent. Factor it
further when aliases, versions, roles, credentials, or changing attributes can
alter sameness.
