# Possibility

Status: candidate

## Sense: open alternative

Possibility represents a state, action, or outcome that remains available or
uncertain within a view.

`possibility := alternative x conditions x likelihood x consequence`

Treat possibility as prime when alternatives only need enumeration. Factor it
further when feasibility, uncertainty, risk, opportunity, dependency, or
decision rights affect what can occur.

[Transformation](transformation.md).
