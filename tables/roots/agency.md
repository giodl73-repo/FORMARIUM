# Agency

Status: candidate

## Sense: capacity to act

Agency identifies a participant treated as able to initiate, choose, or remain
responsible for an action.

`agency := actor x capability x authority @ context`

Treat agency as prime when the actor is already known. Factor it further when
delegation, automation, collective action, accountability, or permission must
be distinguished.
