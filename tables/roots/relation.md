# Relation

Status: candidate

## Sense: typed connection

Relation states how two or more distinguishable participants are connected.

`relation := participants x relation type x direction @ context`

Treat relation as prime when the connection type is sufficient. Factor it
further when cardinality, role, strength, direction, duration, or provenance
changes the consequences.

Related: [Identity](identity.md), [Boundary](boundary.md),
[Subject-object relationship](../primes/subject-object-relationship.md).

## Sense: structural role

As a factor role, relationship carries a typed connection among participants.
It contrasts with component when connection does not imply ownership or
part-whole containment.

`participants x relationship @ context`
