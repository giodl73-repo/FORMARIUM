# Mechanism Path Evidence Table

Status: candidate Evidence Table

Primary family: Evidence Table

Canonical headword: [Association, Causal Effect, Influence, Mechanism, Intervention, and Attribution](../entries/causal-reasoning.md)

Canonical senses: `causal-mechanism`, `causal-effect`, `influence`,
`intervention`, `causal-attribution`

## Governing question

What evidence supports each proposed step in a change-producing pathway, and
which causal claims remain unestablished?

## Evidence table

| Path element | Required claim | Evidence to retain | Competing explanation or gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| initiating condition/action | exact exposure, intervention, version, and timing | assignment/occurrence record and provenance | selection, common cause, or misclassification |
| entity or participant | identity, state, boundary, and role in path | observation/measurement tied to step | wrong entity, proxy, or omitted participant |
| activity or transformation | input-to-output change under conditions | perturbation, trace, process, or domain evidence | association or narrative only |
| intermediate state | ordering and dependence on prior step | repeated observation and alternate-path test | correlated marker rather than producer |
| propagation | direction, channel, lag, and interference | path-specific evidence and negative controls | shared environment or reverse path |
| termination/outcome | outcome measure, horizon, population/case | comparison and uncertainty | endpoint mismatch or transport failure |

## Review procedure

1. Freeze the exact effect, mechanism, influence, intervention, or attribution
   claim; do not let one evidence table answer all five.
2. Record every proposed entity, activity, state, ordering, and boundary.
3. Bind evidence and counterevidence to individual path steps.
4. Test perturbations, alternate paths, common causes, reverse direction, and
   missing intermediates where appropriate.
5. Preserve gaps and distinguish a plausible account from supported mechanism.
6. Keep responsibility, blame, legality, and policy outside the causal result
   unless separately governed.

## Sources and provenance

See the canonical Causal Reasoning entry. This table structures evidence; it
does not identify an effect, estimate a path, or assign responsibility.
