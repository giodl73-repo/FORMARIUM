# Assurance and Conformity Evidence Table

Status: candidate Evidence Table

Primary family: Evidence Table

Canonical headword: [Assurance, Assessment, Audit, Certification, and Accreditation](../entries/assurance-assessment-audit-certification.md)

Canonical senses: `assurance`, `assessment`, `audit`, `attestation`,
`certification`, `accreditation`

## Governing question

What does an assurance, audit, certification, or accreditation artifact support
and what does its scope explicitly leave unestablished?

## Evidence table

| Artifact or claim | Required binding | Evidence/authority | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| assurance case | exact claim/property, subject, use, argument, evidence, defeaters, version | traceable evidence and independent review proportionate to claim | certainty or every future condition |
| assessment result | subject/configuration, criteria/version, method, sample, assessor, date | observations, workpapers, calculations, limitations | criteria outside scope or causal effectiveness |
| audit finding | mandate, criteria, evidence, sampling, finding, response, reviewer | protected audit record and quality controls | root cause, intent, or remediation success by itself |
| certification | scheme, certified subject/scope, certification body, decision, issue/expiry/status | scheme-governed assessment and certificate/status source | universal quality, safety, legality, or accreditation of subject |
| accreditation | accreditation criteria, assessment body, activity scope, accreditation body, status/period | competence/impartiality assessment and public scope | correctness of every certificate or assessment |

## Review procedure

1. Verify issuer and immutable artifact identity through an authoritative source.
2. Match subject, criteria/scheme, scope, locations, versions, and dates.
3. Inspect methods, samples, exclusions, findings, limitations, and status.
4. Separate evidence, finding, decision, attestation, and public claim.
5. Check surveillance, expiry, suspension, withdrawal, appeal, and supersession.
6. Reject any downstream claim broader than the retained scope supports.

## Failure signs

- a logo or certificate number replaces scope verification;
- current status is inferred from an old artifact;
- accreditation and certification are reversed;
- no distinction remains between assessor evidence and decision authority;
- audit completion is called effectiveness, legality, or safety.
