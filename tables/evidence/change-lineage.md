# History, Revision, Provenance, Lineage, and Change Evidence

Status: candidate Evidence Table

Primary family: Evidence Table

Canonical headword: [Identity, Naming, Classification, and Versioning](../entries/identity-naming-classification-versioning.md)

Canonical senses: `entity-identity`, `version`

## Governing question

Which events, sources, activities, agents, rules, and versions explain how this
record or subject reached its current state, and what changed or was lost?

## Evidence table

| Concept | Required record | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| history | ordered relevant events/states, subject identity, time, source | complete past |
| revision | prior/new version, exact change, author/authority, reason, effective time | improvement or correctness |
| provenance | source entities, activities, agents/authorities, derivations, custody | truth or causation |
| lineage | directed source-to-derived chain, transformation, version, loss | same identity or reversible mapping |
| change record | changed field/factor/relation, before/after, trigger, approval, validation, rollback | deployed or effective state |

## Constraints

- Current state, event history, revision intent, provenance, lineage, and
  deployment/effect remain separate.
- Derived artifacts retain source identity, activity/rule, parameters,
  versions, loss, and invalidation.
- Missing history and unresolved custody remain visible.
- Superseded records remain reconstructable rather than overwritten.
