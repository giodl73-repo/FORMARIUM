# Planned Work Procedure

Status: candidate Procedure Table

Primary family: Procedure Table

Canonical headword: [Coordinated Work](../entries/coordinated-work.md)

Canonical senses: `plan`, `task`, `work-milestone`, `workflow`, `orchestration`,
`choreography`, `concurrency`, `compensation`

## Governing question

What is the smallest governed plan that makes intended work, dependencies,
resources, evidence, and change visible without pretending the future is known?

## Procedure table

| Step | Required record | Gate | Failure recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Frame | outcome, scope, owner, authority, assumptions, exclusions | completion condition is observable | return to objective owner |
| 2. Decompose | tasks, deliverables, inputs, outputs, owners, effort/duration bases | all scoped work has an owner and evidence | split vague work or expose gap |
| 3. Connect | dependencies, interfaces, decisions, branches, joins | no hidden predecessor or orphan completion | repair work graph |
| 4. Resource | people/capabilities, materials, facilities, funds, constraints | allocation and contention are visible | replan scope, sequence, or capacity |
| 5. Mark | start/finish and meaningful milestones with criteria | milestones are zero-duration evidence points | reclassify hidden tasks |
| 6. Baseline | plan version, dates, schedule basis, risk/uncertainty | authority approves one recoverable baseline | retain draft and resolve conflicts |
| 7. Execute | actual events, state, outputs, evidence, forecasts | actuals do not overwrite baseline | correct record and preserve history |
| 8. Review | variance, blockers, changed assumptions, outcomes, decision | disposition and change authority recorded | escalate, rebaseline, stop, or recover |

## Constraints

- A plan is a revisable intention, not an assertion that events will occur.
- Task completion evidence is not automatically outcome evidence.
- Resources are typed and time-bounded; names do not prove availability.
- Milestones do not hide work or consume duration.
- Rebaselining preserves the prior baseline and reason.
- Named project methods, ceremonies, tools, and job titles remain examples.
