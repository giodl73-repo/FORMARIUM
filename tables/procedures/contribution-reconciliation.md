# Contribution and Reconciliation Procedure

Status: candidate Procedure Table

Primary family: Procedure Table

Canonical headword: [Decomposition Modes and Combination Contracts](../entries/decomposition-modes-combination-contracts.md)

Canonical senses: `contribution`, `combination-contract`,
`completeness-contract`

## Governing question

How can a bounded result be allocated among declared contributions without
double counting, hiding interaction, or naming the residual as a cause?

## Procedure table

| Step | Required record | Gate | Failure recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Frame | result, subject, boundary, period, model, purpose | one exact quantity/status is being explained | narrow the claim |
| 2. Choose basis | baseline/comparator, unit or scale, sign convention | contributions share an interpretable basis | transform with loss or keep separate |
| 3. Admit contributors | identities, roles, inclusion rule, evidence status | every term has a declared source | remove or mark unresolved |
| 4. Treat overlap | interaction, shared causes, joint ownership, allocation rule | no contribution is silently double counted | retain joint term or range |
| 5. Combine | operator, order, conditions, uncertainty | contract is valid over the declared domain | split regimes or change contract |
| 6. Reconcile | predicted/allocated result, observed/declared result, residual | arithmetic and semantic totals are distinguished | diagnose model, data, timing, or omission |
| 7. Disposition | explained, unresolved, excluded, or out-of-scope remainder | no residual receives an unsupported name | retain neutral residual |
| 8. Review | sensitivity, alternatives, provenance, version | result survives declared checks | revise and preserve superseded record |

## Constraints

- Contribution is relative to a model, rule, comparator, and result.
- Signed contribution, causal effect, organizational responsibility, and moral
  blame remain separate claims.
- Joint contributions remain joint unless an allocation rule is declared.
- Reconciliation equality does not prove every term is true.
- A residual can combine omissions, timing, measurement, model, and processing
  errors; it is not automatically loss, waste, fraud, or creation.
- Open or partial contributor sets do not normalize themselves to a whole.
