## Mechanism mappings

| Source concept | Candidate records or mechanisms | Required condition | Not equivalent to |
|---|---|---|---|
| governance | charter, governing-body mandate, decision-right register, risk strategy, oversight committee, performance/risk report, accountability forum | governed subject, purpose, boundary, stakeholders, authority, direction, oversight, and accountability are stated | management activity, org chart, board meeting, or policy document alone |
| obligation | legal/regulatory register, contract term, policy duty, role obligation, standard requirement, voluntary commitment, XACML obligation | source, obligated subject, required conduct, scope, conditions, jurisdiction, effective time, and precedence are retained | control, task, rule, permission, or responsibility alone |
| compliance | applicability analysis, obligation-to-control map, evidence register, assessment, audit finding, attestation, exception/waiver record, remediation plan | exact obligation version, criteria, evidence, assessor, time, uncertainty, exceptions, and result are retained | control presence, certification label, effectiveness, ethics, or desired outcome |

## Enforcement chain

| Stage | Typical mechanism | Required custody |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | charter, strategy, policy | authority, purpose, scope, version |
| Obligation identification | requirement register, contract inventory | source, subject, applicability, effective time |
| Operationalization | rule, procedure, control, assignment | trace to obligation and owner |
| Decision | policy decision point, reviewer, approval workflow | facts, rule version, result, rationale |
| Enforcement | policy enforcement point, workflow gate, technical control | action, target, failure behavior, evidence |
| Assessment | test, inspection, audit, attestation | criteria, sample, evidence, assessor, limitations |
| Response | remediation, escalation, exception, consequence | owner, authority, due date, verification, supersession |

## Preservation and loss

| Property | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Obligation source and applicability | must be preserved |
| Governance authority | must be preserved |
| Technical enforcement semantics | target-system-specific |
| Compliance result | scoped to obligation, evidence, criteria, and time |
| Effectiveness or ethical quality | not generated by compliance status |
| Reverse inference | invalid from document or tool labels alone |

## Change tests

| Change | Required review |
|---|---|
| Obligation source changes | applicability, controls, owners, evidence, assessments, and supersession |
| Organization boundary changes | governing authority, stakeholders, jurisdiction, and accountability |
| Control changes | obligation coverage, residual gaps, evidence, and effectiveness tests |
| Exception expires | enforcement, remediation, current compliance, and escalation |
| Assessment method changes | comparability, sampling, uncertainty, and historical interpretation |

## Failure signs

- governance is reduced to a board, chart, or policy file;
- an obligation omits its source or obligated subject;
- a control identifier replaces the requirement it supports;
- a policy enforcement point is treated as legitimate authority;
- missing evidence becomes an automatic pass;
- compliance status is reused after obligation or system versions change;
- certification is treated as universal proof of effectiveness or ethics.
