# Agreement, Commitment, Obligation, Guarantee, and Contract Integrity

Status: candidate Constraint Table

Primary family: Constraint Table

Canonical headword: [Governance, Obligation, and Compliance](../entries/governance-obligation-compliance.md)

Canonical senses: `governance`, `obligation`, `compliance`

## Governing question

Which invariants keep an agreement, commitment, obligation, guarantee, or
contract scoped to its parties, authority, terms, conditions, and evidence?

## Constraints

| ID | Requirement | Invalid when |
|---|---|---|
| `AC-01` | identify parties, roles, authority, subject, jurisdiction/domain, and version | labels substitute for party or authority |
| `AC-02` | distinguish mutual agreement, unilateral commitment, imposed obligation, assurance/guarantee, and contract artifact | every promise becomes the same legal relation |
| `AC-03` | state duties, rights, conditions, triggers, exclusions, dependencies, and consideration/basis where applicable | terms are inferred from title |
| `AC-04` | preserve offer/acceptance or admission, effective time, duration, termination, and supersession | draft or expired terms are treated as active |
| `AC-05` | separate promised performance, allocated/reserved capacity, actual performance, evidence, and outcome | commitment becomes delivery |
| `AC-06` | retain breach/nonperformance, exception, waiver, remedy, appeal/dispute, and residual obligation | one failure flag closes every consequence |
| `AC-07` | scope guarantees to exact proposition, provider, beneficiary, conditions, and remedy | guarantee becomes universal certainty |

## Claim boundary

This view is a semantic integrity record, not legal advice. Named contract
types, jurisdictions, clauses, service agreements, warranties, and guarantee
schemes remain external authorities.
