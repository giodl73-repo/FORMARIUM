# Momentum, Torque, Stress, Field, and Density Research Note

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: source-grounded candidate synthesis

## Finding

The reusable mechanics structure separates state, interaction, moment,
distribution, and field levels:

```text
selected mass and velocity in one frame -> linear momentum
net external force accumulated in time -> momentum change

force plus reference geometry -> torque about a point or axis

mass over finite volume -> average density
mass distribution at continuum scale -> local density field -> integrated mass

gravitational force / test mass at a location -> gravitational field

surface orientation at a material point -> stress tensor -> traction vector
```

Momentum is not velocity, force, impulse, or kinetic energy. Torque is not work
despite shared `N m` dimensions. Pressure is a scalar normal-loading quantity;
stress is a tensor that maps oriented planes to traction. Gravitational field
and kinematic acceleration share dimensions but retain different roles. A bulk
density average cannot reconstruct a heterogeneous local field.

Named force families, materials, body shapes, astronomical objects, stress
criteria, and coordinate systems remain examples or scoped authorities rather
than new canonical senses.
