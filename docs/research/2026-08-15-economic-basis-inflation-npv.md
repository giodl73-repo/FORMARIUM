# Economic Basis, Inflation, and Net Present Value Research Note

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: source-grounded candidate synthesis

## Finding

The reusable ladder is:

```text
economic event + authority + recognition/measurement rules -> accounting record
priced population and basket + weights + interval -> price-index change
signed timed cash flows + valuation date + discount model -> net present value
```

These concepts produce comparable-looking monetary numbers at different
descriptive levels. Accounting basis decides when and how an event enters a
particular record; it is not the cash event itself. Inflation is change in a
declared aggregate price index, not one price movement or the index level. Net
present value maps admitted signed flows to one date and sums them; it is not
realized accounting profit or a complete decision policy.

Named standards, index products, asset classes, and appraisal methods remain
scoped authorities or examples. The canon owns authority, purpose, recognition,
measurement, index population and basket, weights, interval, vintage, cash-flow
boundary, timing, sign, discount rate, nominal/real alignment, uncertainty, and
sensitivity.
