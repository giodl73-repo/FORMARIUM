# Operating-Condition and Risk-Treatment Source Boundary

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: bounded editorial research for a two-view custody repair

## Trigger

A read-only audit of the 53 canonical entry paths and 95 canonical view paths
found source or provenance headings on 146 of 148 paths. The two exceptions
are:

- `tables/diagnostics/operating-condition-failures.md`;
- `tables/procedures/risk-treatment-response.md`.

Both are already fixed-point candidate views with stable owners, senses,
paths, and assurance bindings. This repair adds explicit source custody and
boundaries only. It does not admit a concept, sense, relation, view, or new
preview edition.

## Editorial synthesis

The operating-condition diagnostic's seven symptom rows are Factorium's
cross-domain editorial synthesis. They organize discriminating questions but
do not establish cause or diagnose an incident.

The risk-treatment procedure's six roles are likewise Factorium's editorial
routing structure. NIOSH and FEMA demonstrate that treatment location,
timing, authority, and evidence matter, but neither source establishes the
six-role table as a universal taxonomy or treatment hierarchy.

## Admission decision

Repair exactly the two existing views. Preserve their candidate status,
canonical owners and senses, governing questions, tables, procedures,
boundaries, paths, and relation sidecar. Add explicit sources and an editorial
synthesis statement, then refresh only the affected assurance digests.
