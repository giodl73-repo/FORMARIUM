# Stock, Flow, Accumulation, Balance, and Conservation Research

Date: 2026-08-15

Status: bounded source review for a candidate Factorium anchor and Diagnostic
view

## Research question

Can one cross-domain entry separate stored amounts, boundary-crossing or
period-assigned flows, accumulation, accounting balance, and conservation
without universalizing the terminology or turning a generic equality into a
physical law?

## Source findings

The United Nations System of National Accounts records opening and closing
stock levels at points in time and the changes between them over a period.
DOE energy-storage guidance independently separates energy capacity, an
amount, from power capacity, a release rate. These support a general
stock-versus-flow orientation while preserving domain-specific quantity and
unit rules.

USGS water-budget methods define movement into and out of, and storage change
within, a selected control volume. EPA's mass-balance pattern keeps inputs,
outputs, internal generation/consumption, and accumulation separately visible.
The reusable contribution is therefore a contract over a named property,
boundary, interval, terms, sign convention, and uncertainty—not one fixed list
of domain terms.

NASA conservation guidance scopes no-creation/destruction to a named quantity
and domain. A generic accounting balance may include production,
transformation, consumption, reclassification, valuation, or correction
terms. Calling all such closure equations conservation laws would overstate
their authority.

NASA's steady-state mass equation sets accumulation to zero while retaining
balanced through-flow. USGS residual analyses show that nonclosure can combine
unmeasured components, timing mismatch, model assumptions, and measurement or
estimation error. A residual is evidence to diagnose, not a self-interpreting
physical flow.

## Candidate editorial decisions

1. Admit five senses: `stock`, `flow`, `accumulation`, `balance`, and
   `conservation`.
2. Treat storage, inventory, reserve, population, account balance, and state
   quantity as contextual stock mechanisms or near terms rather than silent
   synonyms.
3. Let flow cover both interval totals and rates only when the time basis and
   units make the distinction explicit.
4. Treat sources and sinks as model-relative terms: boundary transfers and
   internal generation/removal must not be interchanged.
5. Make gross versus net, internal versus external, observed versus inferred,
   and rate versus interval-total reporting explicit.
6. Define steady state as zero accumulation under the selected resolution, not
   as equilibrium, emptiness, closure, inactivity, or zero flow.
7. Treat residuals as mixtures requiring disposition against uncertainty and
   omitted-term hypotheses.
8. Reassign the General Accounting Balance Formula view from the provisional
   energy owner to this cross-domain anchor.

## Limits

- The entry does not replace conservation laws, accounting standards,
  hydrologic methods, process engineering, population accounting, or system
  dynamics.
- Stock/flow terminology varies across economics, physics, ecology, software,
  and organizations; the five-sense organization remains candidate.
- Conservation of mass-energy, charge, momentum, or another quantity retains
  its domain assumptions and mathematical authority.
- A closed balance does not prove correct measurement, complete terms, causal
  explanation, sustainability, or good control.
