# F51-F100 Factoring and Evaluation Campaign

Status: source-backed editorial campaign plan; candidate content only

Date: 2026-08-16

## Research question

How should Factorium extend the F50 fixed point so that a reader can identify
not only the concepts in a problem, but also how their factors combine, how a
bounded evaluation uses them, and what is lost when the result is projected
into a readable Factor Guide?

The campaign treats phase numbers as reviewed editorial slices rather than an
entry quota. A slice may add an anchor, deepen an existing owner, add a
specialized view, admit a typed relation, or prove a book-level composition.

## Governing distinction

The current foundations answer two different questions:

- a root coordinate says what a factor concerns;
- a factor role says what job it performs in one selected view.

F51-F100 adds a third, orthogonal question: what decomposition and combination
contract relates the factors in that view? A contract can be compositional,
partitioning, sequential, interacting, conditional, allocative, projective,
or another declared mode. It does not turn arithmetic symbols into universal
semantic operators.

Each ten-phase batch receives a separate `.roles` plan and result review. The
Product Owner supplies a value gate in addition to semantic, editorial,
assurance, practitioner, and schema review. A batch does not pass its boundary
while any P1 or P2 finding remains open.

## Product-value contract

The campaign value hypothesis is that readers need to move from finding a
concept to composing a bounded set of concepts and understanding how those
factors combine in an evaluation. The present friction is not lack of labels;
it is the work required to reconstruct interaction, order, dependence,
evidence status, completeness, and projection loss across separate entries.

Every ten-phase boundary must therefore show:

| Test | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Recognizable job | one plain-language reader question the batch makes newly answerable or materially easier |
| Book improvement | a coherent reading route, contrast, procedure, or guide rather than record-count growth alone |
| Composition improvement | exact concepts, contracts, or relations that reduce hidden local assumptions |
| Neutral rehearsal | tasks cover every admitted path without encoding the desired answer |
| Cost visibility | added anchors, views, relations, navigation, review, and identity-migration burden |
| Honest evidence | source support and internal mechanics remain separate from reader or outcome evidence |
| Portfolio decision | continue, merge, defer, stop, or remove the next proposed slices based on marginal value |

At F60 the reader job is: determine whether factors are independent,
interacting, ordered, projected, or incomplete before combining them. At F70
it is: interpret an evaluation without confusing its scale, baseline,
criterion, aggregation, or sensitivity. At F80 and F90 it is: expose the
operational and contextual conditions that make an option feasible. At F100
it is: reconstruct a bounded concept evaluation from question through book
guide without treating the projection as authority.

## F100 result

The campaign reaches fixed point without adding a canonical owner in
F91-F100. Existing relation, query, closure, evaluation, and projection
contracts carry F91-F98. One integrated guide carries F99's explanation route
and F100's exact 24-record candidate Book One spine. The Product Owner stops
F-number content expansion and selects internal private-preview preparation;
external-reader evidence remains absent.

## Campaign constraints

- Keep books and Factor Tables primary; software remains a projection and
  rehearsal mechanism.
- Reject or merge any slice that does not reduce a recognizable reader's
  lookup, distinction, composition, or evaluation work.
- Preserve factor role, decomposition mode, combination operator, dependency,
  polarity, provenance, granularity, and completeness as separable fields.
- Never infer independence from separate columns or multiplication from a
  relation label.
- Never infer causal contribution from temporal order, association, or model
  decomposition.
- Preserve interaction terms, residuals, unmapped values, projection loss,
  unresolved frontiers, and alternative decompositions.
- Treat named methods, scales, standards, frameworks, hazard catalogs, and
  domain taxonomies as examples or bounded external authorities.
- Keep all new material `candidate` until domain and reader evidence justify a
  different maturity.
- Migrate exact reference and assurance identities only at reviewed batch
  boundaries.

## Rejected strategies

| Strategy | Disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| one entry for every phase label | reject | confuses execution slices with independent semantic owners |
| one universal arithmetic algebra | reject | semantic interaction, sequence, exclusion, and projection are not interchangeable arithmetic operations |
| infer operators automatically from prose | reject | hides sense, authority, evidence, and dependency choices |
| grow named taxonomies for apparent coverage | reject | replaces reusable criteria with open-ended member catalogs |
| use internal simulations as reader evidence | reject | validates mechanics only |
| wait for unavailable external readers | reject | internal editorial and simulation work can proceed while release gates stay open |
