# Oscillation, Spectrum, and Dispersion Research Note

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: source-grounded candidate synthesis

## Finding

The reusable structure is:

```text
repeated variation + reference + excursion measure -> oscillation and amplitude
waveform + sampling/measurement + representation convention -> spectrum
dispersion relation omega(k) -> phase velocity and local group velocity
```

Amplitude is not one context-free number: its reference, measure, represented
quantity, component, and interval matter. A spectrum is a quantity plotted or
represented against a spectral coordinate, not a catalog of named frequency
or wavelength bands. A transform coefficient remains tied to sampling, window,
sign, normalization, and one- or two-sided conventions.

Phase velocity tracks a constant-phase feature of a component. Group velocity
is the local derivative of a dispersion branch and can describe a narrowband
envelope while that approximation remains valid. Neither definition alone
proves material, energy, causal-signal, or information transport speed.

Named bands, named wave families, instruments, and application-specific modes
remain examples or external taxonomies. The canon owns reference, measure,
coordinate, representation, dispersion, branch, bandwidth, and validity scope.
