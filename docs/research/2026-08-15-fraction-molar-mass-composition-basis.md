# Fraction, Molar Mass, and Composition-Basis Research Note

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: source-grounded candidate synthesis

## Finding

The reusable part-whole structure is:

```text
declared whole and compatible quantity kind
  -> constituent or contained part
  -> part / whole fraction
  -> optional per-hundred display
  -> closure only for an exhaustive mutually exclusive partition
```

A fraction is a constrained ratio, not a synonym for every quotient. Its
boundedness follows from nonnegative part-within-whole membership. Summing to
one additionally requires an exhaustive, nonoverlapping component set.

The reusable mass/amount structure is:

```text
specified material or entity definition
  -> mass and amount of that same subject
  -> molar mass = mass / amount
  -> mass/amount conversion
  -> mass-fraction and amount-fraction basis transformation
```

Molar mass has unit `kg mol^-1`. Relative molecular mass has unit one; matching
numerical values under a `g mol^-1` convention do not make the quantities
identical. Composition-basis conversion requires one consistently ordered,
exhaustive component set and a molar mass for each component identity.

Named substances, molecular families, tabulation authorities, and specialist
molar-mass averages remain examples or scoped sources rather than new senses.
