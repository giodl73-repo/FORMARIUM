# Formography Primary Source Packet

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: initial verified core; broader review remains open

## 1. Exact Formography cognate

**Source:** Ingmar Lippert, Ann-Kristin Kühnen, and Susann Wagenknecht,
"Formographic inquiries into practice, accountability, and infra-critique,"
TU Dresden workshop call, 2021.

Institutional source:
https://tu-dresden.de/gsw/phil/iso/technosozial/forschung/forms

DOI-indexed mirror:
https://doi.org/10.58079/n41t

> "In order to inquire into forms, their becomings, their appropriation and
> contestation, we propose formography---the detailed study of forms in (and
> of) practice, an approach that draws upon ethnographic observation and works
> with analytic perspectives from fields such as sociology, social theory,
> anthropology, and STS."

**Use:** Confirms an exact lexical and methodological cognate. It requires
acknowledgment and contrast.

**Publication boundary:** A peer-reviewed article or book chapter using the
exact term as its named method was not confirmed. Status remains
`UNCONFIRMED`.

## 2. Concept mapping

**Source:** Joseph D. Novak and Alberto J. Cañas, "The Origin and Development
of Concept Maps," Institute for Human and Machine Cognition.

https://cmap.ihmc.us/docs/origins.php

> "Concept maps are a representation that shows explicit relationships
> between concepts using linking words between concepts and arranging the
> ideas expressed in a hierarchical form."

**Use:** Establishes explicit concept-relation mapping and hierarchy as prior
art. Formography must show why alternative factorization, evidence, boundary,
and projection custody are substantive additions.

## 3. Analytico-synthetic faceted classification

**Source:** Ferreira, Maculan, and Naves, "Ranganathan and the faceted
classification theory," *Transinformação* 29(3), 2017.

DOAJ record:
https://doaj.org/article/fce7935674da4458a765d043566510cc

**Use:** Establishes Ranganathan's facet analysis and PMEST as central prior
art for decomposing subjects into recombinable facets.

**Quote status:** A direct article fetch was not available during this module.
Do not quote the article until the publisher or archived full text is
retrieved. The bibliographic and conceptual precedent is retained; exact
wording remains `UNCONFIRMED`.

## 4. ISO Topic Maps

**Source:** Lars Marius Garshol and Graham Moore, *Topic Maps - Data Model*,
public working draft of ISO/IEC 13250-2.

https://www.isotopicmaps.org/sam/sam-model/2008-06-03/

> "scope: context within which a statement is valid"

> "Topic Maps is a technology for encoding knowledge and connecting this
> encoded knowledge to relevant information resources."

**Use:** Establishes topics, associations, occurrences, and scoped assertions
as strong representational prior art. Formography must contribute a mapping
method rather than another encoding syntax.

## 5. Caroline Levine's *Forms*

**Source:** Caroline Levine, *Forms: Whole, Rhythm, Hierarchy, Network*,
Princeton University Press, 2015.

https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691173436/forms

> "Borrowing the concept of 'affordances' from design theory, this book
> investigates the specific ways that four major forms---wholes, rhythms,
> hierarchies, and networks---have structured culture, politics, and scholarly
> knowledge across periods."

**Use:** Establishes a major published precedent for forms operating across
domains and interacting through multiple structures. Formography must
distinguish its open factor sets, inspectable records, source custody, and
non-hermeneutic conformance tests.

## Source-quality boundary

This packet contains four directly quoted institutional, standards, or
publisher sources and one verified bibliographic record without a direct
quote. It is not a complete literature review. German, French, Chinese, and
Japanese sources; patent databases; citation indexes; and later STS
publications remain open research.
