# Three-Case Formography Comparison Synthesis

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: authored comparison packet complete; historical pre-independent-coding snapshot

This synthesis records the gate at authored-packet closure. Independent coding
V0 and V1 later completed without passing promotion; the current boundary is
the recruitment-pending practitioner protocol described in the parent
Formography research README.

| Case | Domain | Concept map | Property graph | Formograph | Required failure |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Access Authorization | governance | 4/10 | 10/10 | 10/10 | missing projection loss |
| Amount and Composition | quantitative science | 4/10 | 10/10 | 10/10 | missing denominator basis |
| Choice and Selection | evaluative decision | 4/10 | 10/10 | 10/10 | missing final authority |

## What survived

The same core objects and ten custody features transferred across all three
cases. Quantitative and evaluative semantics were added through named domain
extensions rather than silent redefinition.

## What failed

Every generic scoped property graph represented every core and extension
field. Formography is not a novel graph representation on this evidence.

## Narrowed thesis

Formography may be a governed method profile for creating source-custodied,
context-bounded, alternatively factored, relation-qualified, loss-declared
maps over ordinary representations.

## Falsifier at this snapshot

If independent coders cannot apply the core and distinguish essential domain
extensions from case-specific schema, or if `governed property graph` is
equally precise and cheaper to teach, the proposed field should pivot.
