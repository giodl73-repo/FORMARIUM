# Moderator guide V0

## Before the session

- Assign the next unused anonymous slot from `randomization-v0.csv`.
- Confirm eligibility without recording a name, email address, employer,
  client, or production system.
- Classify experience only as `reference-or-domain`,
  `graph-or-ontology`, or `both`.
- Do not recruit a study author, model agent, synthetic persona, or Factorium
  simulated reader as a participant.
- Do not describe either term as preferred, newer, or more correct.

## During the session

1. Show the method card with `{{METHOD_LABEL}}` replaced by the assigned label.
   Do not alter the body.
2. Show one assigned case at a time and start the timer when the case appears.
3. Ask the participant to identify the boundary, source authority, unresolved
   state, and declared projection loss.
4. Stop the timer after the first complete response.
5. Permit corrections, counting each participant-initiated revision.
6. Ask for a short explain-back in the participant's own words.
7. Code critical misunderstandings only from the frozen protocol list.
8. After all three cases, ask which term they prefer and which felt more
   burdensome to explain.

Apply `scoring-contract-v0.json` before discussing terminology preference.
Enter preference and burden only on the position-3 response row so each
participant contributes once to those denominators.

Do not teach the method after a mistake, compare the labels during a case, or
change the task wording between labels.

## Privacy and evidence boundary

Recording is off by default. Collect only the fields in the response template.
If a participant volunteers identifying, client, or production information,
do not enter it. The empty template and assignment table are protocol
artifacts, not human evidence. Publish no result until actual eligible sessions
have been completed and adjudicated.
