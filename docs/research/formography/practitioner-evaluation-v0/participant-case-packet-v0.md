# Practitioner case packet V0

Complete the cases in the order assigned to your anonymous slot. For each
case, identify the governing boundary, the information that must remain under
source authority, one unresolved state, and one loss that a compact reader
projection must declare.

## Access authorization

A service team needs a compact map explaining who may approve access, what
permission is granted, and which constraints remain binding. Requests,
approvals, grants, and entitlements are related but not interchangeable. Some
requests remain pending or disputed. The compact view must not make itself the
approval authority.

## Amount, concentration, and composition

A laboratory team needs a compact map connecting amounts, concentrations, and
compositions. Numerator and denominator bases, units, conditions, uncertainty,
and closure status affect interpretation. Some values remain provisional. The
compact view must not suppress a denominator basis or imply more certainty
than its source.

## Choice, alternative, and selection

A decision team needs a compact map connecting a question, bounded
alternatives, hard constraints, criteria, preferences, recommendations, and a
final selection. A recommendation may remain unresolved or be rejected. The
compact view must not turn the recommendation into the final decision
authority.
