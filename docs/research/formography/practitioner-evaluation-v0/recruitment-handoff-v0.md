# Practitioner recruitment handoff V0

Status: recruitment may begin; no participant evidence exists

## Neutral invitation

> We are running a short study of terminology used to explain a structured
> mapping method. The session uses three fictional, non-sensitive cases and
> takes approximately 25 minutes. We are seeking people with reference,
> domain-analysis, graph, or ontology experience. The study does not evaluate
> you or your employer, and we will not collect names, employer information,
> client details, or production examples.

Do not name either tested label in recruitment text. Do not describe the study
as validation of a new field or indicate which term the authors prefer.

## Eligibility

An eligible participant:

- is at least 18 years old;
- self-identifies with `reference-or-domain`, `graph-or-ontology`, or `both`;
- is not a study author;
- is not participating as a model agent, synthetic persona, or simulated
  Factorium reader;
- agrees to use only the fictional packet rather than workplace-sensitive
  examples.

Recruit until all 12 slots are filled, including at least four
reference-or-domain practitioners and four graph-or-ontology practitioners.
The `both` category may count toward each minimum.

## Consent script

> Participation is voluntary. We record coded task measures but no name,
> email address, employer, client, or production-system information. Recording
> is off by default. You may stop at any time. Please do not share confidential
> or identifying workplace details. Do you agree to continue?

Do not assign a slot or retain a response when consent is declined.

## Session handoff

1. Assign the next unused slot without connecting it to contact information.
2. Use only the assigned case order and displayed labels.
3. Apply `moderator-guide-v0.md`.
4. Score with `scoring-contract-v0.json` before discussing terminology
   preference.
5. Enter one row per case in `participant-response-form-v0.csv`.
6. Record preference and burden only on the position-3 row.
7. Keep recruitment contacts outside the repository and delete any temporary
   slot-to-contact scheduling link after the session.

Do not calculate or publish the promotion gate from an incomplete sample.
