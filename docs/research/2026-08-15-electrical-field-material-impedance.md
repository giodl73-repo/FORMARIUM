# Electrical Field, Material, Storage, and Impedance Research Note

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: source-grounded candidate synthesis

## Finding

The reusable ladder is:

```text
local force-per-charge or potential gradient -> electric field
material + direction + state -> resistivity -> specimen geometry -> resistance
conductor/dielectric configuration + state -> capacitance
complex voltage/current response + frequency -> impedance
```

These concepts occupy different descriptive levels. Electric field is local
and vector-valued; potential difference compares points. Resistivity is a
material constitutive property; resistance belongs to a specimen or terminal
model with geometry and contacts. Capacitance describes a charge-potential
relation for a configuration, not charge itself. Impedance retains magnitude,
phase, terminals, frequency, and representation; resistance is one narrower
real-response case.

Named component families, dielectric materials, frequency bands, and circuit
topologies remain examples or external catalogs. The canon owns descriptive
level, material/configuration state, geometry, terminals, frequency, complex
convention, equivalent representation, calibration plane, and validity range.
