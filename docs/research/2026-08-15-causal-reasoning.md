# Causal Reasoning Research Note

Status: source-backed candidate synthesis

## Research question

How can Factorium separate association, causal effect, contextual influence,
mechanism, intervention, and actual-case attribution while keeping the
counterfactual contrast, assumptions, evidence, and scope visible?

## Admission rationale

The control anchor contains an unresolved `causal model`; the polysemous force
entry contains unresolved `influence`. Claim/evidence already owns observation,
result, inference, limitation, and confidence, but no entry owns the distinct
contracts for causal effect, mechanism, or attribution. This F8 bridge connects
evidence, control, coordinated work, risk, and system boundaries without
promoting a statistical method into a universal ontology.

## Source basis

The 16-finding campaign is recorded in
[Causal Reasoning Web Evidence](../../signals/discover/websearch/causal-reasoning-websearch-2026-08-15.md).
It uses original work by Rubin, Pearl, and Halpern; the open Hernán and Robins
causal-inference text; primary methodological papers; and WHO evidence guidance.

## Editorial decisions

- `association` describes an observed dependence and is not a weak causal
  effect.
- `causal-effect` owns a specified contrast between outcome possibilities under
  alternatives for a population or units, outcome, and horizon.
- `influence` is retained as a broader contextual contribution claim. A source
  may influence a result without the evidence identifying a stable quantitative
  causal effect or sole cause.
- `causal-mechanism` owns the process or pathway by which change is produced;
  effect evidence and mechanism evidence can support one another but neither
  automatically proves the other.
- `intervention` is an action or assignment, not its effect. The effect belongs
  to a comparison with a stated alternative.
- `causal-attribution` asks about a particular realized outcome and therefore
  needs case facts and a domain-appropriate actual-cause or responsibility
  model in addition to population evidence.
- A causal diagram, randomized label, regression coefficient, mediator, or
  successful action receives no causal credit merely from its mechanism name.

## Candidate factor spine

```text
causal-reasoning-use
  := exact claim and claim type
   x subject or population and unit
   x exposure, action, or candidate cause
   x alternative or counterfactual contrast
   x outcome, measure, and time horizon
   x temporal order and causal boundary
   x causal model, variables, paths, and interference
   x design, assignment, comparison, and data provenance
   x identification assumptions and estimand
   x estimator, uncertainty, sensitivity, and diagnostics
   x mechanism evidence and competing explanations
   x transport scope, heterogeneity, and applicability
   x actual-case facts, attribution rule, and responsibility boundary
```

## Claim limits

This is a candidate reference organization, not a universal theory of
causation. It supplies a vocabulary and evidence contract, not domain-specific
legal attribution, clinical guidance, safety certification, or a guarantee
that causal effects are identifiable from available data.
