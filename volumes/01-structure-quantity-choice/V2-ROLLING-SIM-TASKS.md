# V2 Rolling Reference Simulation Tasks

Status: author-only route prompts for `sim-50`; not reader observations

## Task BL - The solver selected our strategy

A team turns a planning question into an optimization model and reports the
solver's best assignment as the approved strategy. Use the Optimization
Problem Structure Mapping to recover variables, objective, constraints,
feasibility, result status, optimality support, sensitivity, mapping loss, and
the separate decision handoff.

## Task BM - Five users liked the prototype

A team treats a favorable informal walkthrough as proof that its product is
usable, accessible, validated, and ready to ship. Use the Prototype, Test, and
Iteration Procedure to recover the learning question, prototype boundary,
users, tasks, context, method, observations, findings, change, rerun, stopping,
and production boundary.

## Task BN - The famous person deserves sole credit

A biography assigns one person sole credit because their name appears on the
published work. Use the Contribution, Credit, Priority, and Legacy Evidence
Table to reconstruct activity, prior and parallel work, evidence custody,
priority event and rule, credit authority, causal limits, counterclaims,
reception, revision, and an unresolved disposition.

## Rehearsal boundary

The prompts establish route ownership only. They do not establish that readers
can find, understand, trust, apply, or return to the records.
