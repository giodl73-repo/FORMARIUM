# GPC-09 V1 Simulation Tasks

Status: author-only route prompts for `sim-49`; not reader observations

## Task BI - Same word, same meaning

Two documents use the same English word. One uses it as a conventional label,
the other as a disputed translation. The intended referent, speaker purpose,
audience uptake, and practical use differ. Use the Meaning anchor to retain
sense, reference, producer intent, interpreter, use context, ambiguity,
translation direction, alternatives, and loss.

## Task BJ - Published means known

A widely cited report labels a proposition “known” without naming the
epistemic subject, inquiry, warrant, or defeaters. Use the Epistemic Standing
view to reconstruct attitude, method, grounds, testimony, standing,
alternatives, confidence, scope, and limitation without issuing a truth
verdict.

## Task BK - Online means reusable

A transcript is online, so a downstream project assumes it may translate,
train on, republish, and generalize it. The performer, recorder, translator,
archive, represented parties, consent process, restriction, and return path
are collapsed. Use the Custody view to separate every role and retain unknown
authority and intentionally absent content.

## Rehearsal boundary

The prompts establish exact route ownership only. They do not establish that
a reader can find, understand, trust, apply, or return to these records.
