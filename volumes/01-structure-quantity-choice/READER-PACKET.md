# Volume 01 Reader Packet

Status: external session prompts

The moderator presents one task at a time. Participants should not read ahead
and should not receive the evaluator rubric.

## Task A - One word, several meanings

You are reviewing two statements:

1. "The net force on the cart changed."
2. "The policy remains in force until June."

Use the reference to explain whether the same sense and specialized table
should be used for both. Show where you found the distinction.

## Task B - Boundary or interior

A colleague asks for "the size of a circular garden" but has not said whether
they need fencing, planted ground cover, or soil capacity.

Use the reference to identify the missing distinction and the next questions
you would ask before choosing a formula.

## Task C - Percentage without a basis

A report says a mixture contains "5 percent component A."

Use the reference to decide whether the statement is complete. Identify at
least two materially different interpretations and one piece of missing
information.

## Task D - Temperature change

A process specification says to increase a temperature from `20 C` by
`18 F`.

Use the reference to determine whether the instruction can be interpreted by
applying an ordinary point-temperature conversion. Explain the distinction
you found.

## Task E - Missing policy facts

An approval workflow has an applicable policy, but one required fact is
unknown. There is no blocking constraint yet because evaluation cannot finish.

Use the reference to find the appropriate outcome and identify what must be
recorded with the result.

## Task F - Follow a factor deeper

An access-control design lists `subject`, `action`, and `object`, but a review
questions whether the same user should have different rights to documents they
own and documents merely shared with them.

Use the reference to find the factor that owns this distinction, follow it to
its deeper entry, and explain why it is not just another subject attribute.

## Task G - Apply a guide

Choose one:

- You are designing a Rust authorization boundary for a background job and an
  HTTP handler.
- You need to specify `0.100 mol/L` of a component in `500 mL` final solution.

Use the applicable guide to identify the selected factors or relation, one
rejected shortcut, one required control, and one unresolved local decision.

## Debrief prompts

After assigned tasks:

- What did you expect to find but could not?
- Which table or label was hardest to understand?
- Did any link or index send you somewhere surprising?
- When did the amount of detail feel too small or too large?
- What would you use first if returning to this reference?
