# Proof Set Simulation Quickstart

Status: internal `sim-01` rehearsal surface; not a private-preview edition

## What this is

This sheet rehearses the self-guided shape of a future Factorium proof edition.
It accompanies a generated static rendering of *Structure, Quantity, and
Choice*. It does not recruit a participant, collect reader evidence, or admit
`preview-01`.

Factorium is a table-first reference for selecting a concept sense, comparing
credible structures or relations, checking constraints, and following factors
into deeper entries.

## Start here

1. Open the generated proof and use its contents list or browser find.
2. Begin from a title or phrase you would plausibly know; do not jump to a
   target because its internal path is familiar.
3. Identify the sense or lookup question before choosing a formula or view.
4. Follow at least one factor, cross-reference, or guide trace.
5. Record only artifact-level notes with `SIM-*` identifiers.

Do not record fictional time, difficulty, preference, task completion, quote,
or return use. Those require a real reader.

## Prescribed rehearsal

Choose one route without consulting the evaluator rubric:

- **Sense:** determine whether physical force and a policy being in force use
  the same sense and specialized view.
- **Comparison:** determine what is missing from the statement "5 percent
  component A" and find at least two materially different interpretations.
- **Application:** choose one Factor Guide and identify its selected factors or
  relation, one rejected shortcut, one required control, and one unresolved
  local decision.

For the rehearsal result, record:

- first plausible title or phrase;
- pages or contents headings followed;
- whether the required distinction is present;
- alternate plausible landing pages;
- broken links, missing headings, rendering defects, or unclear instructions.

## Self-selected rehearsal

Use a non-sensitive hypothetical problem. Write the concept or decision in
ordinary language, select a candidate entry or guide, and check whether the
reference makes the sense, pivot, constraints, alternatives, and unresolved
choices visible.

This is a coverage exercise. Do not report the hypothetical scenario as a
successful real-world application.
