## Part XIX - Structure a problem, learn through a prototype, and reconstruct credit

### From a real choice to a bounded optimization model

179. [Optimization Problem Structure Mapping](../../tables/mappings/optimization-problem-structure.md)

The Mapping view separates application question, variables, data, objective,
constraints, feasible set, candidate solution, optimality support, sensitivity,
and decision handoff. A solver result is not automatically a recommendation or
authorized choice.

### From a mockup to a traceable learning cycle

180. [Prototype, Test, and Iteration Procedure](../../tables/procedures/prototype-test-iteration.md)

The Procedure view connects learning question, prototype identity and fidelity,
users, tasks, context, evaluation, observations, findings, change, rerun, and
stopping. Internal rehearsal is not reader evidence or production readiness.

### From a famous name to an evidence-bounded contribution record

181. [Contribution, Credit, Priority, and Legacy Evidence](../../tables/evidence/contribution-credit-priority-legacy.md)

The Evidence view reconstructs activity, prior and parallel work, source chain,
priority rule, credit authority, causal boundary, counterclaims, reception,
legacy, and revision. It cannot allocate credit, rank people, or settle history.
