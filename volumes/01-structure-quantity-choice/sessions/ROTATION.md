# The Sieve Task Rotation

Status: ready

Round: `sieve-01`

## Purpose

Rotate four tasks per participant so every session covers:

- sense or nearby-concept selection;
- specialized-view selection;
- graph navigation;
- realistic application.

The rotation gives every reader a manageable session while collecting at
least two planned observations for every task if all five sessions complete.

## Assignment matrix

| Participant | Task 1 | Task 2 | Task 3 | Task 4 | Planned perspective |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `P01` | A | D | F | G | first available reader; broad background recorded at session |
| `P02` | B | C | E | G | quantitative or engineering reader preferred |
| `P03` | A | C | E | F | product, operations, or organizational reader preferred |
| `P04` | B | D | F | G | software or systems reader preferred |
| `P05` | A | B | D | E | accessibility perspective preferred when feasible |

Preferences support coverage but are not participant identities. Reassign a
task when it is irrelevant to a participant, and record the change.

## Rules

- Present prompts only from `../READER-PACKET.md`.
- Keep `../EVALUATOR-RUBRIC.md` moderator-only.
- Do not replace a failed task with an easier task during the same
  observation.
- Record skipped and reassigned tasks.
- Do not pool dry runs with reader observations.
