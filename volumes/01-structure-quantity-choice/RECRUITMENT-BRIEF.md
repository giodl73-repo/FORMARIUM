# The Sieve Recruitment Brief

Status: ready

## Study invitation

Use this text outside the repository when contacting a potential reader:

> Factorium is testing a new table-first reference for distinguishing concepts,
> selecting structured views, and applying them to practical problems. We are
> looking for a 45-60 minute remote or in-person reading session. You will be
> asked to use a prototype and think aloud while completing several tasks. We
> are testing the reference, not your knowledge. Participation is voluntary,
> and you may stop or skip a task at any time.

Do not commit names, contact details, calendar information, or invitation
responses.

## Desired round composition

- 3-5 completed readers;
- two or more broad practitioner backgrounds;
- software/systems perspective;
- scientific, engineering, analytical, or quantitative perspective;
- product, operations, organizational, or general-reference perspective;
- accessibility or assistive-technology perspective when feasible.

One person may satisfy more than one perspective. These are coverage goals,
not demographic classification.

## Screening questions

Ask only what is needed:

1. Which broad practitioner perspective would you use?
2. Are you comfortable reading Markdown or a rendered documentation site?
3. Do you have any reading or access setup you want the moderator to support?
4. Are you available for a 45-60 minute session?

Do not request confidential work examples.

## Consent checkpoint

Before tasks, confirm:

- participation is voluntary;
- the prototype, not the participant, is being evaluated;
- no recording is planned unless separately agreed;
- notes use a participant code;
- only de-identified observations may be committed;
- the participant may stop or skip tasks.

## Scheduling custody

Keep scheduling and contact information outside the repository in an
appropriate private system. The repository stores only:

- participant code;
- broad background;
- voluntary device/access context relevant to the observation;
- task behavior and de-identified quotes.
