# Book One Candidate Simulation Rubric

Status: author-only expected artifact routes; excluded from candidate reading

## SIM-BO-01 expected artifacts

- the homepage exposes the candidate before worked traces, search, or the full
  contents grid;
- the candidate guide and quickstart are directly reachable;
- question, working concepts, decisive constraints, result state, unresolved
  frontier, and next action remain visible;
- the 24-record teaching spine and 151-record searchable-depth boundary are
  explicit and non-authoritative.

## SIM-BO-02 expected artifacts

- the exact ordinary phrase does not rank unrelated mathematical or thermal
  records ahead solely through stopword matches;
- the candidate quickstart or guide and operating-condition material are
  reachable from the first result set;
- scenario, operational resource, queue, interaction, spatial context,
  hazard/exposure, change lineage, and operating-condition diagnostic remain
  separately available;
- no artifact converts feasibility into forecast or operation into one cause.

## SIM-BO-03 expected artifacts

- candidate orientation can hand off to ordinary search without internal IDs;
- Comparative Quantity and Amount, Concentration, and Composition are both
  credible specialized-depth landings;
- percentage basis, numerator/denominator, component set, and amount/mass/
  volume distinctions remain visible;
- no result treats every `5 percent` as one composition sense.

## SIM-BO-04 expected artifacts

- the guide frames the question before composition;
- scenario, demand/capacity, agreement/queue, evidence, hazard, choice,
  evaluation, projection, and reconciliation owners remain distinct;
- incomplete, contradictory, and truncated states cannot be hidden by a
  profile or aggregate;
- the route retains constraints, uncertainty, interaction, loss, authority,
  frontier, and next action without issuing a ranking or final choice.

## Critical failure boundary

The simulation fails critically if the candidate copies canonical authority,
claims reader success, hides specialized depth, makes problem prose create
relations, flattens a conflict/frontier, or admits `preview-01`.
