## Part XVIII - What does this mean, who claims to know, and who may share it?

### From a familiar word to a situated meaning

176. [Meaning, Reference, Interpretation, and Use](../../tables/entries/meaning-reference-interpretation-use.md)

The anchor separates conveyed content, referential target, interpretive act
and result, situated use, ambiguity, and directional translation. Its Book
view keeps context, language, alternatives, standing, restriction, and loss
visible without choosing one theory or exact translation.

### From a citation to a reconstructable warrant

177. [Epistemic Standing, Inquiry, and Warrant](../../tables/evidence/epistemic-standing-inquiry-warrant.md)

The Evidence view records proposition, subject, attitude, inquiry, grounds,
warrant, defeaters, standing, confidence, limitation, provenance, and review.
It records claims of knowing but does not certify truth.

### From public availability to source custody

178. [Source, Performance, and Research Custody](../../tables/evidence/source-performance-research-custody.md)

The Evidence view separates speakers, performers, participants, researchers,
translators, archives, authority to share, consent, access, restriction,
intentional absence, return, correction, and supersession. Availability and
citation do not become permission.
