# Factor Guides

Status: candidate pilot set

Factor Guides narrow canonical entries and views to bounded local evidence.
They do not create new headword authority. See
[Factor Guide Format V0](../specs/FACTOR-GUIDE.md).

## Pilot guides

- [Rust Access-Control Evaluation](rust-access-control-evaluation.md) -
  narrows the access-control request graph, policy views, and cross-paradigm
  role mapping into one multi-tenant service boundary
- [Aqueous Solution Amount Concentration](aqueous-solution-amount-concentration.md)
  - selects amount concentration from nearby quantity senses and preserves
  final-volume, entity, unit, state, and uncertainty contracts
- [Evidence-Informed Intervention Choice](evidence-informed-intervention-choice.md)
  - compares three synthetic alternatives while preserving incomplete
  evidence, failed and unresolved joins, recommendation, and final authority
