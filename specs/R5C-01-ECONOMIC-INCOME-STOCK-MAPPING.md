# R5C-01 Economic Income and Stock Mapping Design

Status: held candidate design pending curriculum comparison

No Factor Table is admitted by this design. The candidate must compete with
the other curriculum-pressure findings under the same evidence and admission
rules; the campaign may strengthen, narrow, merge, or reject it.

## Decision

Create one no-new-sense Mapping Table under `stock-flow-balance`. Reject a
new economic anchor: `stock`, `flow`, `accumulation`, and `balance` already
own the reusable structure, while `cost-price-value-return` owns monetary
basis, price, return, and valuation distinctions.

## Governing question

How do income, consumption, saving, investment, and wealth occupy different
flow, use, balancing, accumulation, and stock roles under a declared economic
accounting boundary?

## Mapping contract

The view must expose subject/institutional unit, resource boundary, period,
point-in-time valuation, income definition, transfer and tax treatment,
consumption definition, saving identity, asset/liability coverage,
investment meaning, price and currency basis, other changes, provenance, and
framework version.

It must distinguish:

- income from one receipt, asset sale, borrowing, and holding gain;
- consumption activity from consumption expenditure;
- saving as a framework-scoped balancing item from cash retained;
- economic investment/capital formation from every financial asset purchase;
- gross from net concepts and saving from change in wealth;
- wealth or net worth from income, liquidity, affordability, and wellbeing.

## Boundary

Named account classifications, institutional sectors, tax rules, welfare
judgments, financial advice, and compilation decisions remain with their
authorities. The view maps source-scoped economic concepts onto Factorium's
generic roles; it does not authorize an accounting treatment or claim exact
equivalence across national, business, household, or personal-finance uses.

## Acceptance

1. Adds no canonical sense or relation.
2. Maps only existing Stock/Flow senses.
3. Names direction, source framework, version, and loss.
4. Retains liabilities, valuation changes, transfers, and other-volume changes.
5. Does not equate saving with wealth change or investment with saving.
6. Keeps wellbeing, fairness, sustainability, and recommendation external.
7. Gives R5C-01-19 and R5C-01-21 one direct route.
