# Decision/Evidence Reading Route

Status: F30 complete reader-facing projection contract

## Goal

Make the admitted evidence-to-evaluation relation legible as a book and
problem-led reading path without adding it to the interactive Composition Lab
or implying that a local evidence record is applicable.

## Reader route

The existing Evidence-Informed Intervention Choice Guide owns the human
reading destination. It must show one compact five-stage route:

1. **Add** the exact Claim/Evidence source factor.
2. **Multiply** through `f27-evidence-qualifies-evaluation` to the exact Choice
   evaluation factor and Decision scope view.
3. **Evaluate** the declared applicability check.
4. **Stop** at `incomplete` while that check is unresolved.
5. **Flatten** only the three loss-declared projection rows.

The route binds the exact canonical query identity and links back to the
admission contract. It does not duplicate the full Factor Tables or turn the
guide into a second graph authority.

## Home and Explorer projection

The proof-site home page adds one problem-led card and one read-only
Composition Explorer panel for the exact canonical query. Both link to the
existing full guide. The card must say that the route is read-only and not
available in Compose.

Technical manifests report six read-only traces and five authored Lab
starters separately. The sixth trace may not appear in the starter payload,
Lab relation controls, palette, closure engine, or Compose deep links.
