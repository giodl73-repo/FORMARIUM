# FACTOR Research

Use this skill before standardizing a semantic contract, changing a benchmark,
or making an adoption decision.

## Workflow

1. State the research question and decision.
2. Inspect local specs, fixtures, controls, and results first.
3. Research feature structures, compositional generalization, semantic parsing,
   role/filler binding, and vector-symbolic controls when relevant.
4. Record citations, implications, confidence, and non-goals.
5. Separate adopt-now, prototype, and defer/reject recommendations.
6. Preserve evidence that the general method predates this repository.

## Output

Write a cited note under `docs/research/` and update the affected wave or
specification.
