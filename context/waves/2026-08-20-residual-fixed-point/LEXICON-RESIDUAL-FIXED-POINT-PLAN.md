# Lexicon Residual Fixed-Point Plan

Status: complete

## Frame

Lexicon now contains 54 canonical entries and 34 complete noncanonical
candidates. Further domain comparison produces many missing labels but very few
missing decomposition contracts.

The bounded slice is:

```text
logic, communication, cognition, social, spatial, and meta-reference audits
+ strict cross-domain and existing-owner filter
+ source review of four surviving governing questions
-> at most four new noncanonical candidate pairs
```

## Surviving research questions

1. **Collective action and shared resources:** when do individual incentives,
   benefit access, and extraction rivalry produce a provision or appropriation
   shortfall?
2. **Collection identity and arrangement:** when is an aggregate a separately
   identified collection, and what membership, arrangement, completeness,
   custody, and lifecycle rules govern it?
3. **Human action error and recovery:** where does an unintended outcome diverge
   from intention, plan, execution, memory, or a deliberately followed rule?
4. **Situational understanding and projection:** what relevant state does an
   agent detect, integrate into a current situation, and project forward, and
   where is that representation deficient?

Each question may still merge, split, or fail after source and owner review.

Final disposition: the first three families produced candidates. Situational
understanding failed the composition gate, and collection custody routes to the
existing organization ownership/stewardship owner.

## Explicit routes and rejections

- argument structure, inference modes, logical consequence, proof, program
  correctness, modality, and computability route to existing evidence,
  mathematical, state, assurance, or specialist views;
- implicature, sign theory, discourse coherence, medium, reception,
  information seeking, and focalization route to meaning, interaction,
  evidence, inquiry, or specialist linguistic views;
- signal detection, cognitive load, workload, memory, learning taxonomies, and
  metacognition route to measurement, evaluation, capacity, competency, or
  specialist human-factors views;
- bureaucracy, polycentric governance, social-network metrics, practice theory,
  public-accountability taxonomies, deliberation, stratification, and contract
  doctrine route to organization, governance, institution, incentive, consent,
  deontic, and network owners;
- CRS, place names, territory, cadastre, terrain, watersheds, landscape ecology,
  geologic time, urban morphology, and biogeochemical cycles remain specialist
  spatial or earth-system views;
- equivalence, classification warrant, supersession, aboutness, SKOS, FRBR,
  Wikidata, Schema.org, OAIS, PROV-O, and archival provenance route to existing
  similarity, identity, classification, meaning, evidence, lifecycle, and
  custody owners.

## Gates

1. A survivor must remain cross-domain after its named source framework is
   removed.
2. Its governing question must not be answerable by composition of current
   owners without semantic loss.
3. Senses must be mutually distinguishable and cannot be validation guards or
   factor fields.
4. Domain authorities retain scope; no social, archival, cognitive, or safety
   framework becomes universal.
5. Every proceed decision receives a complete candidate draft and admission
   brief; every merge or rejection is recorded.
6. Canonical Tables, relations, assurance, search, and publication counts remain
   unchanged.
7. Independent fixed-point review leaves no unresolved actionable finding.

Canonical admission remains separately gated by recurring external-reader need.
