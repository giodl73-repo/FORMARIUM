# Lexicon Residual Fixed Point

Status: complete

## Decision

Six residual audits across logic, communication, cognition, social systems,
space, and knowledge organization produced dozens of apparent gaps. A strict
cross-domain and existing-owner filter reduced them to three coherent families:

- collective action and shared-resource coordination;
- collection identity, membership, and arrangement;
- human action error and recovery.

Situational understanding and projection failed the composition gate. All other
proposals route to existing owners or views, or remain specialist domain
vocabulary.
