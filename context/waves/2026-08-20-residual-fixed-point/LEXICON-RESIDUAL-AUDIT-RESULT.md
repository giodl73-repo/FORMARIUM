# Lexicon Residual Audit Result

Status: complete; three families proceed to noncanonical drafting

## Audit scope

The residual audit compared the 54 canonical entries and 34 completed
noncanonical candidates against six previously underused control sets:

- logic, argumentation, formal methods, and computation;
- semiotics, pragmatics, discourse, information behavior, and media;
- learning science, cognition, memory, attention, and human factors;
- anthropology, sociology, collective action, organizational theory, and
  public administration;
- human geography, GIS, spatial ontology, urban form, and earth systems;
- terminology, knowledge organization, archival description, and
  meta-reference systems.

## Fixed-point filter

An apparent gap survived only when it was cross-domain, had a falsifiable
governing question, could not be composed from current owners without loss, had
stable source distinctions, and was not merely a named theory, standard,
disciplinary catalog, or specialized view.

That filter reduced the audit to three candidate families.

## Proceed

| Family | Surviving senses | Governing distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Collective action and shared-resource coordination | `provision-shortfall`, `appropriation-shortfall` | under-provision of a shared benefit versus over-appropriation of a rival stock or flow |
| Collection identity and arrangement | `named-collection`, `membership-rule`, `collection-arrangement`, `collection-completeness` | collection-level identity and rules that remain distinct from member identity and system composition |
| Human action error and recovery | `action-error`, `error-recovery` | divergence among intention, plan, execution, and result versus detection, correction, and stabilization |

Resource-type grids, free riding, common-pool labels, collection members,
boundaries, lifecycle, custody, slips, lapses, mistakes, and violations remain
factors, examples, or routed concepts rather than senses.

## Merge or reject

Situational awareness does not advance. Its perception, comprehension, and
projection levels are stages in one source model, not independently owned
cross-domain senses. Relevant records compose from perception, claim/evidence,
control/monitoring, model/representation, state, and conditional projection.
A future Factor Guide may show that composition without creating another
authority.

The other major proposals route as follows:

- argument, inference, proof, program correctness, modality, computability, and
  safety cases route to evidence, mathematics, state, assurance, or specialist
  formal-methods views;
- implicature, sign theory, discourse relations, medium, reception,
  information-seeking, and focalization route to meaning, interaction, inquiry,
  evidence, or specialist language views;
- signal detection, cognitive load, workload, memory, learning taxonomies, and
  metacognition route to measurement, evaluation, resource/capacity,
  competency, or human-factors views;
- bureaucracy, nested governance, social-network metrics, practice theory,
  accountability taxonomies, deliberation, stratification, and contract
  doctrine route to organization, governance, institution, incentive, consent,
  deontic, and network owners;
- CRS, place, territory, cadastre, terrain, watershed, landscape ecology,
  geologic time, urban morphology, and biogeochemical cycles remain specialist
  spatial or earth-system views;
- equivalence, classification warrant, supersession, aboutness, SKOS, FRBR,
  Wikidata, Schema.org, OAIS, PROV-O, and archival provenance route to
  similarity, identity, classification, meaning, evidence, lifecycle, and
  custody owners.

## Claim boundary

This audit establishes candidate completeness under the chosen comparison
controls. It does not establish reader demand, canonical admission, empirical
validity of any source framework, or completeness against every discipline.
