# Lexicon Foundational Comparison Result

Status: complete; twelve concept families proceed to noncanonical drafting

## Comparison set

The audit compared the existing 54 canonical entries and 22 noncanonical
candidates against controls that the earlier category panel had not used
systematically:

- BFO, DOLCE, SUMO, UFO, and CIDOC CRM;
- WordNet, Roget, FrameNet, ConceptNet, and library or knowledge
  classifications;
- jurisprudence, deontic logic, and institutional ontology;
- pragmatics and speech-act theory;
- psychology and affect research;
- medicine and public-health classification;
- process aspect, persistence, history, economics, and complexity;
- philosophy and ontology of art.

The controls were applied semantically rather than by label matching. A source
category advances only when it exposes a reusable governing question that no
existing owner answers without loss.

## Dispositions

| Provisional family | Disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Part / component / member / constituent / piece / feature | Merge and reject | Component, membership, composition, partition, and fraction questions already have owners; bare piece and feature do not support a coherent standalone family |
| Disposition / capacity / function / capability | Split and proceed | Engineering capability, operational capacity, mathematical and software function, and competency route out; latent disposition and selected function remain disjoint |
| Right / duty / liberty / power / liability / immunity | Proceed as analytical mapping | Hohfeldian incidents expose correlatives and opposites that governance, access, consent, and authority entries currently assume |
| Permission / obligation / prohibition / waiver / exemption | Proceed as source-relative deontic mapping | Formal normative status differs from domain-specific grants, duties, policy exceptions, and authorization decisions |
| State / activity / accomplishment / achievement | Proceed as process-aspect family | Telicity, duration, culmination, and subinterval entailments are not owned by the engineering lifecycle entry |
| Persistence / continuity / identity through change / replacement | Proceed with competing accounts preserved | The canonical identity owner requires a continuity criterion but does not structure the competing persistence accounts |
| Institution / norm / social object / reified relationship | Proceed | Governance and organization assume, but do not explain, constitutive rules and socially dependent entities |
| Assertion / question / directive / promise / declaration | Proceed as illocutionary-force family | Protocol interaction and propositional meaning do not own the communicative force of an utterance |
| Cardinality / ordinality / rank / index / sequence position | Proceed | Set size, ordered position, ranking, indexing, relational multiplicity, and ordinal scale values have distinct admissible operations |
| Affect / appraisal / emotion / feeling / mood | Split and proceed narrowly | Affect is umbrella vocabulary and mood routes to the stance candidate; appraisal, emotion episode, and phenomenal feeling remain disjoint |
| Sign / symptom / syndrome / diagnosis / differential diagnosis / prognosis | Proceed with clinical scope | Clinical findings, classification, hypothesis management, and prognosis have a source and authority structure absent from generic evidence, evaluation, and prediction owners |
| Path dependence / lock-in / emergence / historical causation | Split and proceed narrowly | Path dependence and lock-in survive; emergence routes to tipping or realization, and generic historical causation routes to causal reasoning |
| Work / performance / aesthetic object | Split and proceed narrowly | Abstract work, notation, performance, and compliance survive; aesthetic object, experience, and interpretation require theory choices or existing owners |

## Surviving candidate queue

The comparison supports twelve new noncanonical entries:

1. disposition, power, and designed function;
2. process aspect;
3. persistence and identity through change;
4. Hohfeldian incidents;
5. deontic operators and normative status;
6. institutions, norms, and social objects;
7. illocutionary force and speech acts;
8. cardinality, ordinality, rank, and index;
9. appraisal, emotion episode, and phenomenal feeling;
10. clinical sign, symptom, diagnosis, and prognosis;
11. path dependence and lock-in;
12. abstract work, notation, and performance.

The queue is candidate-completion work only. None has recurring external-reader
evidence, and none is admitted to canonical Tables, graph identity, catalogs,
assurance, search, or publication counts.

## Relation result

`part-of` and `contrasts-with` do not advance as sidecar relation kinds.
Unqualified parthood collapses incompatible flavors and inference rules.
Editorial contrast is already represented more precisely by decisive
distinction tables and the entry graph.

See the [relation gap assessment](LEXICON-RELATION-GAP-ASSESSMENT.md).

## Falsified proposals

The audit rejects:

- one universal part-whole relation;
- bare piece, feature, affect, emergence, aesthetic object, or aesthetic
  experience as standalone senses;
- universal emotion categories or one mandatory persistence theory;
- jurisdiction-independent legal or clinical claims;
- treating Hohfeld, Standard Deontic Logic, BFO, DOLCE, or another comparator as
  Lexicon's authority;
- using another authored campaign as reader-demand evidence.
