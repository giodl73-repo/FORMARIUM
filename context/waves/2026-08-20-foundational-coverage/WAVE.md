# Lexicon Foundational Coverage

Status: complete

## Decision

The earlier category panel did not systematically compare Lexicon with
foundational ontologies, major lexical systems, or several disciplines that
carry reusable decomposition patterns. This wave audits those controls and
develops noncanonical candidates only where a disjoint governing question
survives existing-owner review.

Canonical admission remains out of scope. The 54 canonical entries, relation
sidecar, catalogs, assurance bindings, search, and publication counts remain
unchanged.
