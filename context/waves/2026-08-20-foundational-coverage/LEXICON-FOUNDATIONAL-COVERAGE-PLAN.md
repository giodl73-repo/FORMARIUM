# Lexicon Foundational Coverage Plan

Status: complete

## Frame

Lexicon already has strong owners for measurement, systems, information,
governance, decisions, evidence, software, physical science, and the twenty-two
completed noncanonical candidate families. What is missing is not another
unbounded word harvest but a comparison against conceptual systems that ask
different foundational questions.

The bounded slice is:

```text
five foundational ontologies
+ five lexical and classification controls
+ neglected disciplinary controls
+ existing-owner and relation-contract audit
-> sourced noncanonical proposals for only the disjoint surviving gaps
```

## Provisional concept families

The following are research questions, not promised entries:

1. part, component, member, constituent, piece, and feature;
2. disposition, capacity, function, and capability;
3. right, duty, liberty, power, liability, immunity, and disability;
4. permission, obligation, prohibition, waiver, and exemption;
5. state, activity, accomplishment, achievement, and process aspect;
6. persistence, continuity, identity through change, and replacement;
7. institution, norm, social object, and reified relationship;
8. assertion, question, directive, promise, and declaration;
9. cardinality, ordinality, rank, index, and sequence position;
10. affect, appraisal, emotion, feeling, and mood;
11. sign, symptom, syndrome, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, and prognosis;
12. path dependence, lock-in, emergence, irreversibility, and historical
    causation.

Broad proposals may split; overlapping proposals must merge or route to an
existing owner. Specialist doctrine, clinical instruction, ontology
conformance, and universal metaphysical claims remain out of scope.

## Relation questions

Two relation kinds receive separate compatibility review:

- `part-of`, including whether physical part, member, constituent, and feature
  require distinct kinds or belong inside an entry contract;
- `contrasts-with`, including whether antonymy, incompatibility, and decisive
  distinction are safe to represent as one symmetric relation.

Parser support, candidate relation-kind design, and canonical edge admission
remain separate states. This wave does not mutate
`reference/lexicon-relations-v2.lexicon`.

## Comparison controls

- BFO, DOLCE, SUMO, UFO, and CIDOC CRM;
- WordNet, Roget, FrameNet, ConceptNet, and a library or knowledge
  classification;
- jurisprudence and deontic logic;
- medicine and public health;
- pragmatics and speech-act theory;
- psychology and affect research;
- process philosophy, history, economics, and complexity;
- philosophy and ontology of art.

These controls inform boundaries. They do not become Lexicon's authority, and
label presence alone is not evidence of a gap.

## Falsification and deletion targets

A provisional family is rejected when:

- an existing canonical or candidate owner answers its governing question
  without semantic loss;
- it is only a specialist vocabulary list rather than a reusable
  decomposition;
- its proposed senses cannot be made mutually distinguishable;
- authoritative sources disagree in ways the entry cannot preserve as explicit
  context;
- it requires Lexicon to assert one universal metaphysics, legal regime,
  diagnostic standard, or aesthetic theory.

The wave should delete convenient duplicate ownership, generic `related-to`
relations, universal part-whole semantics, and simulated claims of reader need.

## Gates

1. Every surviving entry has a sourced admission brief and complete
   noncanonical draft.
2. Every rejected or merged proposal has an explicit disposition.
3. Sense identifiers do not collide with canonical or candidate owners.
4. Existing-owner boundaries are reciprocal where two candidate families meet.
5. Relation proposals define direction, inverse policy, qualifier contract,
   endpoint types, false inferences, and admission boundary.
6. The canonical corpus and relation sidecar remain byte-identical.
7. Independent fixed-point review leaves no unresolved critical, major, or
   actionable finding.

Canonical admission remains separately gated by recurring external-reader need,
domain approval, graph and catalog validation, assurance bindings, and
publication review.
