# Wave: Role and Ambiguity

## Goal

Extend FACTOR from flat one-surface/one-meaning custody to ordered role slots,
shared fillers, explicit alternative analyses, and exact plus approximate
role/filler controls.

## Thesis

Factor preservation is credible only if role identity survives repeated
participants and if ambiguity remains a set of valid analyses rather than
being erased into one label. Any distributed benefit must survive typed
feature structures and exact tensor-product binding.

## Frozen fixture families

### Ordered transfer

Factors: giver, theme, recipient-primary, recipient-secondary, tense, and
polarity. Recipient slots are semantically ordered and may contain the same
filler.

### Attachment ambiguity

Factors distinguish observer, predicate, patient, instrument, and
patient-associated object. Ambiguous surfaces retain both complete meanings;
unambiguous paraphrases identify each reading.

## Success criteria

- ambiguity is represented as explicit candidate meanings, never `unknown`;
- repeated fillers and repeated role slots remain distinct;
- analysis families are split without hidden paraphrase or candidate leakage;
- exact records and TPR remain authoritative strong controls;
- HRR dimensions, seed, binding, unbinding, and cleanup are frozen in advance;
- all metrics retain explicit denominators and separate cost fields;
- no broad semantic-role-labeling, ambiguity-resolution, or NLP claim appears;
- `.roles` has no unresolved critical or major finding.
