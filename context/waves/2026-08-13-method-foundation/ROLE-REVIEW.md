## Fixed point

The repaired method-foundation structure is admitted:

```text
foundation
  -> schema specification
  -> prior art and custody
  -> parser and conformance
  -> compositional fixtures
  -> strong-control bakeoff
  -> portable packet and onboarding
```

There are no unresolved critical or major findings. FACTOR is correctly
structured as a research method and benchmark contract, not yet as a general
NLP framework.
