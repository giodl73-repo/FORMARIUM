# Wave: Method Foundation

## Goal

Establish FACTOR as the public, hardware-neutral home for
Factor-Preserving Semantic Encoding and freeze the first reproducible method
contract.

## Thesis

Semantic factors should remain explicit enough to recombine, edit, audit, and
evaluate independently. Any benefit must survive comparison with ordinary
packed fields and strong conventional factored representations.
