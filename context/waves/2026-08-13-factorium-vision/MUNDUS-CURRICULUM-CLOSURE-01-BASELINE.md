## Recommended research

### Optimization problem structure

Six packets across mathematics, software, AI/inference, and control contribute
optimization terms or exact session headings. The recurring lookup question is
not “which solver?” but how variables, domain, objective, constraints, feasible
set, optimality claim, method, diagnostics, and sensitivity differ from a
substantive decision.

Research a no-new-anchor Mapping or Constraint view across the existing Choice,
Constraint, and Mathematical Relation owners. Do not import a catalog of named
algorithms or optimization classes.

### Prototype, test, and iteration

Two materially different courses—human-computer interaction and games for
social change—repeat prototyping pressure. The HCI record also supplies
accessibility, learnability, usability, and user-testing vocabulary; the games
record supplies assessment and intended social outcome.

Research a no-new-anchor Procedure view across Requirement/Validation,
Coordinated Work, and Evaluation. Its likely question is how purpose,
hypothesis, fidelity, participant/task boundary, observation, criterion,
revision, and stopping rule form a traceable iteration without calling an
internal rehearsal reader evidence.

## No admission

Admit zero anchors, zero views, zero relations, and zero discovery repairs in
this baseline. The course campaign authorizes established-source research for
two bounded view questions only. Content design, source review, role review,
and a separate admission decision remain required.

## Product decision

The campaign earns the next small investment because two questions recur
across materially different reader contexts and appear capable of reducing
reconstruction while reusing existing owners. It does not earn a broad
course-derived content batch. Stop all domain-label, named-method, tool, and
single-course-specialization branches unless later independent pressure changes
their evidence.

## Claims boundary

This is internal source-led coverage evidence over a frozen registry snapshot.
It does not establish curriculum consensus, completeness, pedagogical value,
reader comprehension, professional guidance, or market demand.
