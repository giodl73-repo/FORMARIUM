## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAMP-001 | major | Population, frame, observed sample, and analytic sample could collapse into one data label. | Closed: each has a sense and every transition is preserved. |
| SAMP-002 | major | Estimand, estimator, estimate, statistic, and parameter could collapse into one metric label. | Closed: target, rule, realized result, data function, and modeled property are contrasted. |
| SAMP-003 | major | Random sampling, assignment, and splitting could be treated as interchangeable. | Closed: each mechanism has a distinct purpose and licensed claim. |
| SAMP-004 | major | Large sample size or a narrow interval could substitute for total-error evidence. | Closed: coverage, response, measurement, processing, linkage, dependence, and model error remain separate. |
| SAMP-005 | major | `Representative` could become an unsupported binary property. | Closed: it must be replaced by an exact result, target, criterion, and evidence. |
| SAMP-006 | major | Weighting could be presented as a universal repair. | Closed: target, source probabilities/model, balance, overlap, extreme weights, uncertainty, and residual limits are required. |
| SAMP-007 | major | A participant or benchmark result could automatically generalize to deployment. | Closed: source-target differences, modifiers, versions, support, and bridge evidence remain explicit. |
| SAMP-008 | major | A random test split could prove future-domain or semantic coverage. | Closed: grouped, temporal, cross-environment, and leakage-controlled tests are tied to the target claim. |
| SAMP-009 | major | Final row counts could hide exclusions, missingness, linkage, and preprocessing. | Closed: a stage-by-stage inclusion flow and denominators are required. |
| SAMP-010 | major | Unsupported target groups could disappear from the conclusion. | Closed: exclusions and unsupported regions remain visible. |
| SAMP-011 | major | Candidate organization could become universal statistical doctrine. | Closed: the entry remains candidate and alternative disciplinary terminology is retained. |
| SAMP-012 | major | The diagnostic could be read as statistical or high-stakes certification. | Closed: it explicitly organizes failure isolation and denies certification. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate publication.
