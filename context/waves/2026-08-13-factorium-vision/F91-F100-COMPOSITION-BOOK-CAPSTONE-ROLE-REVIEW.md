Status: plan reviewed; implementation pending

## Scope and value

The reader has a bounded question spanning several concepts. Their job is not
to find one entry; it is to form the needed closure, combine or exclude
concepts under typed contracts, evaluate the result, see unresolved frontier
and projection loss, and retain an explanation another person can inspect.

The thirteen-role review is
`signals/roles/check/f91-f100-composition-book-capstone-roles-check-2026-08-16.md`.
It found zero P1 blockers, thirteen P2 conditions, and five P3 notes.

## Product Owner plan decision

Decision: **proceed with no new canonical owner and one reader-facing
capstone artifact**.

The product value is navigation reduction and a coherent teaching route, not
more content. Existing schemas, relations, fixtures, guides, and browser
profiles must carry F91-F98. F99-F100 may add one integrated candidate guide
that names an exact Book One spine. Stop before a second query language, new
relation kinds, automated judgment, external-reader claims, or publication.
