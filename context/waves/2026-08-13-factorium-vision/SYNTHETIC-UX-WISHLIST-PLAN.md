# Synthetic UX Wishlist Plan

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: complete; see `SYNTHETIC-UX-WISHLIST-RESULT.md`

Campaign: `SUJ-03`

Evidence class: authored synthetic product-design rehearsal only

## Objective

Reuse the 25 frozen SUJ-01 profile descriptors and their SUJ-02 term-blind
tasks to ask what experience would plausibly help before, during, and after a
Factorium route. Produce UX hypotheses and rejection constraints without
representing authored answers as participant preferences.

## Questions asked of every proxy

1. Which task-shaped entrance would be easiest to recognize?
2. What should happen immediately after choosing it?
3. What result shape would help decide where to open?
4. When should Compact, Abbreviated, Book, or Full appear?
5. What would make Compose approachable without pretending prose is parsed?
6. Which trust cue must remain visible?
7. How should a dead end or uncertain result recover?
8. What should be easy to copy, print, or hand to another person?
9. What nearby exploration could make the reference enjoyable?
10. What keyboard, mobile, visual, or reading need should the design respect?
11. Which tempting feature would add more confusion or risk than value?
12. Which incumbent resource should remain part of the route?

## Frozen design candidates

The campaign compares these bounded experience candidates:

- task-shaped intent router: term lookup, question composition, or learning;
- result preview that exposes owner, kind, concise distinction, and depth;
- authored Compose starters with explicit controls and no automatic execution;
- visible stop, unresolved frontier, and outward handoff;
- situation-shaped related routes for voluntary exploration;
- copy/print handoff containing route and source links.

Accounts, saved histories, social editing, inferred personalization, open
natural-language execution, and autonomous recommendations are rejection
probes, not admitted candidates.

## Admission rule

Synthetic counts never constitute demand, preference, usability, or reader
recurrence. SUJ-03 alone cannot admit a feature. A change may be implemented
only when it is already supported by deterministic product mechanics or a
prior cross-campaign structural result; SUJ-03 may shape its wording and
disclosure. All other ideas remain external-reader questions.

## Current smallest candidate batch

The SUJ-01 and SUJ-02 results already support three distinct jobs. If the UX
rehearsal exposes no blocking contradiction, add one homepage intent router
above the existing two-book explanation:

- **I know the term** -> Search the Tables;
- **I have a question** -> Compose from explicit concepts and controls;
- **I want to learn or explore** -> Open the Reader.

The router changes navigation copy only. It does not alter search ranking,
canonical content, graph closure, reader profiles, persistence, or authority.
Preserve `sim-42`; any rendered implementation must be a new simulation.

## Boundaries

- Profile metadata does not determine a response.
- Responses are authored from the task and current interface, never claimed
  as quotes, inner states, accessibility outcomes, or preferences.
- No demographic comparison is permitted.
- No external-reader gate closes.
- The sole SUJ-02 status-language miss remains deferred.
