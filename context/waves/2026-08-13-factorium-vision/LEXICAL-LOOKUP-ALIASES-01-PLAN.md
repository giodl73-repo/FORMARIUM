## Frozen behavior

`organisation`, `frame of reference`, and `electric power` each expose one
canonical Table owner. `field of force` and `force field` each expose both
Electrical Quantity and Matter and Load Measure. Neither field phrase may pick
one meaning, reorder the underlying literal results, or imply that electric and
gravitational fields are equivalent.

Activation is exact after Unicode normalization, lowercasing, trimming, and
internal whitespace collapse. Partial, extended, fuzzy, morphological, and
generated variants do not activate. The panel is labelled `Lookup alias` and
states that it routes language to existing Tables without creating concepts,
senses, equivalence, classification, or semantic closure.

edition-local product metadata because the two field terms legitimately have
multiple owners and the current canonical alias grammar is entry-local.
Reference V0, Tables, Reader, Guides, relations, search index records, literal
ranking, and all prior editions remain unchanged.

Render `sim-47` only after the implementation commit is clean. Require exact
reproduction of `sim-46`, five aliases, seven target routes, zero missing
targets, unchanged full rankings for all five queries and near misses, and a
