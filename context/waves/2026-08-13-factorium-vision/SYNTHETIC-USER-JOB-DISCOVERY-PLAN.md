## Question

Does Factorium merely improve definition and synonym lookup, or does it offer
a recognizable new reason to visit: tracing distinctions, factors,
constraints, and combinations after the relevant words are already known?

## Portfolio

Freeze 25 adult proxy personas before executing search. The portfolio crosses:

- six age bands from 18–24 through 65–74;
- education, science, engineering, software, operations, policy, media,
  creative, household, community, and retirement backgrounds;
- ten curiosity/fun situations and fifteen practical-purpose situations;
- dictionary, thesaurus, encyclopedia, web search, handbook/textbook, and
  workplace-document alternatives;
- five simple lookup controls, ten contrasts, and ten composition/evaluation
  tasks.

Age, profession, and digital comfort vary the authored setup; they must not be
used to infer ability, preference, comprehension, or behavior. Personas have
IDs rather than names and contain no protected, private, medical, financial,
or other sensitive personal histories.

## Frozen result grammar

For each proxy journey retain:

1. exact two-query lexical results against `sim-42`;
2. exact selected destinations and owner count;
3. the authored starting expectation and first plausible landing;
4. a bounded proxy reaction: useful moment, friction, and reason to stop;
5. relationship to the declared alternative:
   - `replacement` — Factorium performs substantially the same lookup job;
   - `bridge` — the alternative names or orients, then Factorium continues;
   - `new-reason` — the useful output is a trace, comparison, composition, or
     evaluation not reducible to definitions or synonyms;
   - `not-served` — the task remains better owned by an external or local
     authority;
6. projection losses, missing owner, and change disposition;
7. an explicit statement that every reaction is authored, not observed.

Do not record simulated completion rates, timings, quotations, satisfaction,
preference, comprehension, accessibility success, adoption, or return intent.

## Controls against designing the answer

- Five simple lookup controls remain in the denominator even when Factorium is
  plainly excessive.
- Prior tools are frozen before execution and are not scored as inferior.
- `replacement`, `bridge`, `new-reason`, and `not-served` are all valid.
- A new reason requires exact reference support and an output beyond a
  definition or synonym list; page count alone is insufficient.
- External authorities and local workplace rules remain valid endpoints.
- No persona is resampled, rewritten, or removed after search.
- The portfolio reports every null, failure, and unresolved route.

## Decision

The campaign may recommend a product-positioning hypothesis or a bounded
navigation/content investigation. It cannot close R4E, admit `preview-01`, or
support reader, market, demographic, accessibility, or adoption claims.
