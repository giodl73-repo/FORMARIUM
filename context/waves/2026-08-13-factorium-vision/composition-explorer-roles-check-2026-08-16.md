## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 8

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 0 | P3 notes: 24

Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: the explorer must remain a deterministic projection of exact
trace custody, not an alternate closure implementation.

Cross-role consensus: useful comparison requires both compact staging and a
visible escape to the complete guide. Exact context, direction, admitted
nodes, boundaries, loss, and identity prevent the compact surface from
overclaiming.

## Amendments

1. Bind all five panels to exact trace bytes, canonical source hashes,
   worksheet digests, work accounting, and manifest records.
2. Preserve semantic distinctions across Add, Multiply, Evaluate, Stop, and
   Flatten; show exact admitted nodes and boundary types.
3. Keep the surface read-only, book-subordinate, responsive, independently
   checked, and edition-gated with prior-edition regression.

All amendments are applied. No critical or major finding remains open.
