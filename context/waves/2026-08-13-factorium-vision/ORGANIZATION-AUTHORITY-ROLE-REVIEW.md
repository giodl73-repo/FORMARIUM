## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORG-001 | critical | Role could collapse into person, job title, position, or permission set. | Closed: the role sense is an assignee-independent function bundle; target mechanisms are explicitly non-equivalent. |
| ORG-002 | critical | Responsibility could be mistaken for authority. | Closed: duties/results and legitimate decision/action rights have separate senses, sources, and required fields. |
| ORG-003 | critical | Technical action traceability could be presented as complete organizational accountability. | Closed: `accountability-answerability` and `action-accountability` are distinct senses and mapping rows. |
| ORG-004 | critical | Delegation could erase the delegator's retained obligations or become permanent silently. | Closed: source, recipient, transferred scope, retained duties, limits, expiry, substitution, and revocation are mandatory. |
| ORG-005 | major | Ownership could imply legal title, physical possession, or performance of every task. | Closed: the admitted sense is scoped ownership/stewardship under a governing scheme; legal ownership remains unresolved. |
| ORG-006 | critical | A responsibility matrix or org chart could be treated as the canonical model. | Closed: mappings are contextual, partial, many-to-many, and invalid in reverse without source semantics. |
| ORG-007 | major | Authority could exist without a legitimate granting source or effective limits. | Closed: source, permitted decisions/actions, scope, constraints, controls, time, and revocation are required. |
| ORG-008 | major | Responsibility could be assigned without resources or sufficient decision rights. | Closed: the entry requires conditions/resources and makes responsibility-without-authority an escalation failure sign. |
| ORG-009 | critical | Combined assignments could violate least privilege or separation of duty. | Closed: conflict, least-privilege, separation, vacancy, substitution, and succession checks are mandatory. |
| ORG-010 | major | Federal security and HR guidance could be universalized as one organization design. | Closed: target domains remain cited and bounded; operating-model prescription is a non-goal. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate publication.
