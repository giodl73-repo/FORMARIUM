## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| RIV-001 | critical | A generated format could replace readable Markdown and make the software the product. | Closed: V0 owns metadata only; linked Markdown remains the publication body. |
| RIV-002 | critical | A shared schema could flatten eleven family-specific contracts. | Closed: V0 stores primary family identity but leaves typed payload semantics behind versioned family boundaries. |
| RIV-003 | major | One-sense ownership would misrepresent formula views that compare several senses. | Closed: views carry one or more explicit sense IDs in stable order. |
| RIV-004 | critical | Three early formula pilots lack canonical headword owners. | Closed: migration records visible unresolved owners rather than guessing a taxonomy. |
| RIV-005 | major | File paths could accidentally become canonical identity. | Closed: stable IDs are separate; paths are validated source metadata. |
| RIV-006 | critical | A checked-in generated catalog could silently become stale. | Closed: `reference-check` compares exact generated bytes and fails closed. |
| RIV-007 | major | Hand-maintained `tables/INDEX.md` could remain a second authority. | Closed: it now links generated anchor/view catalogs and curates only nonduplicated supporting navigation. |
| RIV-008 | major | Unresolved reports could collect explanatory prose rather than candidate declarations. | Closed: only bullet declarations carrying the exact marker are projected. |
| RIV-009 | major | Canonical ordering could erase meaningful factor or sense order. | Closed: entries/views/aliases sort by identity; sense and factor declaration order is preserved. |
| RIV-010 | major | A custom V0 could be mistaken for an ecosystem interchange standard. | Closed: the spec calls it an internal canonical format and places standard exports behind a compatibility boundary. |
| RIV-011 | major | Projection generation could rewrite files on every run. | Closed: writes occur only when bytes differ; validation is read-only. |
| RIV-012 | critical | Source and manifest metadata could drift independently. | Closed: exact headings, maturity, sense markers, factor phrases, family declarations, and owner links are checked. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate V0.
