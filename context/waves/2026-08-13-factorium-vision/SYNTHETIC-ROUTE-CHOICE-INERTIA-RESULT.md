## Product interpretation

The combined rehearsal sharpens the earlier “second reference move” model:

> Start with the ordinary question. Use Search when one structural landing can
> orient it; use Compose when several concept owners must be joined. Stop when
> the distinctions, conditions, missing evidence, and next authoritative source
> are explicit.

Tables A–Z remains the dictionary-like browse after a term or family is known.
Reader remains the guided-learning and wandering surface. The term-blind
portfolio supplies no synthetic reason to make either the dominant entrance
for problem-shaped questions.

Factorium's rehearsed advantage is not replacing the incumbent's speed,
breadth, examples, local facts, or authority. It is preserving a structural
route across concepts and making the stopping rule and outward handoff part of
the answer.

## Trust, memory, and enjoyment hypotheses

Trust cues cluster around visible sources, evidence state, scope and authority
boundaries, unresolved questions, and explicit units or bases. Blockers cluster
around missing local evidence, candidate synthesis, dense multi-owner routes,
and absent domain authority.

Possible memory cues are situation-shaped rather than headword-shaped:
“percent of what,” “green but unusable,” “same average but different,” and
“passed the threshold but not decided.” Possible fun continuations follow a
nearby distinction or relation. Both are future test prompts, not measured
memory or enjoyment.

## Decision

Proceed to prepare a real-reader, term-blind observation around Search,
Compose, stopping, and outward handoff when external readers become available.
Do not modify search aliases, ranking, content, profiles, navigation, or the
edition from this dependent synthetic campaign. Keep the green-dot status miss
as a deferred observation target.

No external-reader gate closes. No reader participated, and this campaign
supplies no evidence about behavior, comprehension, accessibility, preference,
trust, task success, demand, adoption, or return use.
