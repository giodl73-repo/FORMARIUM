# Global Philosophy Counter-Sample Blueprint 01

Date: 2026-08-17

Campaign: `GPC-01`

Decision: proceed as nine staged campaigns over twelve expandable lanes. Do
not run one omnibus “Eastern philosophy” or “all cultures” comparison.

## Why this program exists

PFC-01 deliberately exposed the limitation of its Socrates-to-Rawls sample.
Leaving that sample alone would let one Western sequence decide which
Factorium gaps appear universal. Merely appending famous representatives from
other places would reproduce the same canon method at larger scale.

GPC-01 instead samples philosophical positions, questions, practices,
arguments, texts, periods, languages, and social locations. People, schools,
cultures, nations, religions, and transliterated terms remain source custody
and navigation—not automatic Factorium concepts.

## Twelve lanes

| Lane | What it prevents |
|---|---|
| South Asian | “Indian” does not silently mean only one Hindu or Sanskrit lineage; Brahmanical, Hindu, Jain, Sikh, and materialist positions remain distinguishable. |
| Buddhist, transregional | Buddhism is not reduced to India or one school; South, Central, East, and Southeast Asian transmissions retain lineage and location. |
| Chinese | Confucian, Daoist, Mohist, Legalist, Buddhist, and later traditions remain internally differentiated. |
| Korean and Japanese | Neither becomes a footnote to China; transmission, adaptation, local traditions, and modern philosophy remain visible. |
| Arabic-language, Islamic, and Persianate | Language, religion, region, ethnicity, philosophy, theology, and mysticism are not treated as synonyms. |
| Jewish | Ancient, medieval Islamicate, Hebrew, diasporic, and modern settings remain connected without disappearing into Christian or Islamic containers. |
| Russian and Eastern European | Russian philosophy receives a specific historical lane but does not stand for Eastern Europe or Eurasia. |
| African | Region, language, oral/literate form, continental setting, and diaspora replace the fiction of one African system. |
| Latin American and Caribbean | Scholastic, literary, liberation, decolonial, Indigenous, diasporic, and academic forms remain contestable rather than one essence. |
| Indigenous and First Peoples | Each people and community retains authority and context; public availability is not permission or adequate custody. |
| Central and Southeast Asian | Traditions not captured by Buddhist or Islamic histories remain independently discoverable. |
| Oceanic and Pacific | Community-led and regionally grounded sources prevent a residual continental bucket. |

These lanes are work partitions, not natural civilizations. Mixed, diasporic,
multilingual, and border-crossing packets may belong to several lanes.

## Packet contract

Every packet records source custody, the tradition's self-identification and
context, governing question, reusable concepts, decisive distinctions,
practice or argument context, translation terms and losses, internal
disagreement, existing Factorium owners, coverage, and mapping loss.

Revision 2 additionally requires every source-supported factoring encountered
along the way to receive a stable `factoring_id`. Its record preserves source
anchors, governing sense or question, pivot, factors, dependencies,
constraints, alternative factorings, excluded combinations, and interpretive
status. Each factor retains its source term, transliteration, working gloss,
role, variation, and source support.

These source-local records are immutable. A later interpretation may supersede
one, but it cannot rewrite or delete it. Incompatible factorizations and
factorings with no Factorium mapping remain addressable.

Six sampling axes prevent name-only coverage: historical period, genre or
practice, school or lineage, language and translation, social location, and
governing question.

## Admission boundary

A cross-tradition concept-owner candidate must recur in at least three
independently sourced packets across two lanes, lack an adequate current owner,
and pass source, taxonomy, mapping, and product review. Recurrence is screening
pressure, never automatic admission.

Important structures limited to one tradition may become scoped candidate
views or routes. Conversely, the same English gloss in several lanes may still
be a false equivalence. Every translation declares direction, context, and
loss.

## Execution order

1. `GPC-01`: this source and method blueprint.
2. `GPC-02`: South Asian and transregional Buddhist comparison.
3. `GPC-03`: Chinese, Korean, and Japanese comparison.
4. `GPC-04`: Arabic-language, Islamic, Persianate, and Jewish comparison.
5. `GPC-05`: Russian and Eastern European comparison.
6. `GPC-06`: African comparison.
7. `GPC-07`: Latin American and Caribbean comparison.
8. `GPC-08`: Indigenous, Central/Southeast Asian, and Oceanic custody-led comparison.
9. `GPC-09`: one-pass global factoring alignment, conflict retention,
   recurrence, and Factorium disposition.

Each execution campaign freezes its own sources and packets. A lane may split
when source review shows that one label conceals materially different contexts.
No campaign can declare world-philosophy closure.

## One-pass alignment contract

Each lane also appends to a separate alignment ledger. An alignment record
names the source factoring IDs, mapping direction, relation kind, shared and
preserved structure, losses, conflicts, candidate owner, and review status.
Allowed relation kinds are exact, structural overlap, analogy, broader,
narrower, conflict, and unmapped. A shared English gloss is never sufficient
for alignment.

Before GPC-09 begins, the complete ordered inventory and digests of all
PFC-01/GPC-02–GPC-08 source-local factorings are frozen. GPC-09 then consumes
that inventory once and emits alignment groups, conflict groups, unmapped
factorings, owner candidates, scoped-view candidates, rejected/deferred
mappings, and a complete source-to-output ledger.

The alignment pass may add mappings and dispositions; it may not edit, delete,
or silently merge the local records. “No defensible alignment” is a valid
result. This gives us consistent final reconciliation without making every
tradition conform to the eventual global frame.

## Product decision

The recognizable reader job is: start with a question or distinction from a
philosophical tradition, locate the nearest Factorium owner, and see exactly
where translation or structure stops carrying.

The smallest useful next investment is `GPC-02`, because South Asian and
Buddhist sources directly pressure all three leading PFC-01 design candidates:
epistemic standing, normative/practical structure, and human agency. It also
tests person/no-self, liberation, testimony, inference, nonviolence,
many-sidedness, dependent structure, and conventional/ultimate truth.

Do not add Tables, biographies, school catalogs, search aliases, or reader
claims from this blueprint. The packet floors prevent token representation;
they are not coverage percentages or phase-value evidence. External readers
and qualified tradition/domain review remain later gates.

## Evidence boundary

Eighteen scholarly and research-ethics sources support the staged frame,
terminology rules, translation fields, and custody gates. They do not establish
the eventual packet selections, an exhaustive map of any tradition, global
coverage, reader value, or comparative superiority.
