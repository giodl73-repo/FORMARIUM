## Why this test exists

CAD-01 showed that “Factorium has contrast and thesauri do not” is false.
Merriam-Webster's Synonym Chooser supplied genuine distinctions on six of the
twelve terms. `CAD-02` therefore treats **schematized thesaurus** as the null
description and tests the narrower proposed difference: whether a Factorium
route carries contrast into consequence-bearing inspection.

The exact packets, five binary dimensions, interaction metric, thresholds, and

## Scoring contract

Score each dimension separately. Metadata volume, headings, implied domain
knowledge, and generic links earn no credit. `task_scope_coverage` requires the
materially opposed meanings or layers named by the packet. `governing_inputs`
requires at least two explicit packet-relevant inputs. Each remaining dimension
requires one explicit, packet-relevant passage.

The direct pass records product identity. The second pass uses shuffled packet
and product labels. Both are internal procedural audits, not independent
readers. Reconcile all disagreements against immutable capture text and retain
the disagreement register.

Apply every threshold conjunctively. Do not replace the dimensions with a
weighted scalar. A surviving result supports only a bounded artifact claim:
on these packets Factorium exposes consequential structure beyond the retained
thesaurus output. It does not establish uniqueness, general superiority,
reader value, comprehension, preference, trust, adoption, or return use.

No repair is admitted during execution. Classify every miss. Any later change
requires a separate smallest-batch decision and a clean rerun of the exact
portfolio.
