## Synthesis

Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: prose must remain graph-inert; the selected reviewed relation
records, not suggestive wording, determine closure.

Cross-role consensus: the simulation is trustworthy when every admitted edge,
derived node, scope, conflict, unresolved check, bound, and projection loss is
visible and bound into a deterministic identity.

## Amendments

1. Restrict closure to explicit seeds and the six exact reviewed F1-F6
   relations; normalize ordering and include every input and result in identity.
2. Preserve node classes, qualifiers, exclusions, conflicts, frontiers,
   capacity requirements, unresolved-only evaluation, and flattening loss.
3. Keep the lab local and disposable, subordinate to the book/reference work,
   with no persistence, network, publication, reader evidence, or V1 claim.

All amendments are applied. No critical or major finding remains open.
