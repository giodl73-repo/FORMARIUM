# Formography Practitioner Protocol Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: pre-registered protocol plan

## Objective

Test whether the Formography heading improves practitioner comprehension or
reduces explanation burden relative to Governed Property Graph when the method
body and cases are otherwise identical.

## Frame

```text
working Factorium reference method and ordinary property-graph representation
+ unresolved human terminology and comprehension question
-> a blinded practitioner comparison without changing source authority
```

Factorium retains canonical semantics, review policy, publication authority,
and source custody. The study tests terminology and comprehension only.

## Design

- 12 anonymous participant slots;
- three cases per participant;
- complementary within-participant label assignments;
- each case shown six times under each label;
- each case shown four times in every position;
- identical method body and task text under both labels;
- at least four reference or domain practitioners and four graph or ontology
  practitioners, with overlap allowed.

## Measures

Record comprehension correctness, completion time, corrections, explain-back
word count, critical misunderstanding, terminology preference, and terminology
burden.

## Boundaries and falsifier

Do not collect names, email addresses, employers, client data, or production
details. Recording is off by default. Model agents, synthetic personas,
Factorium simulated readers, and study authors cannot fill participant slots.

Formography promotion requires comprehension no lower, median time at least
10% lower, corrections no higher, at least two-thirds participant preference,
and no more than 20% critical misunderstandings under the Formography label.
Null or adverse results are retained. No result exists until eligible sessions
are actually completed.
