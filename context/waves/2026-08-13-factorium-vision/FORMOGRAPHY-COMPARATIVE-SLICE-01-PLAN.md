# Formography Comparative Slice 01 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: executed bounded research slice

## Objective

Encode one governed Factorium case as a concept map, a generic scoped property
graph, and a candidate formograph to test whether the proposed method retains
consequential information beyond established representations.

## Frozen case

`Access, Permission, Authorization, and Entitlement` is selected because the
current reference already owns:

- five canonical senses;
- nine source factors;
- one admitted integrity view with eight constraints;
- exact entry and view SHA-256 assurance bindings;
- explicit authority, maturity, and non-certification boundaries.

The canonical reference, Table, constraint view, and assurance records remain
authoritative. Research artifacts are projections only.

## Predeclared features

1. question frame;
2. primary factorization;
3. alternative factorizations;
4. typed relations;
5. scoped relation qualifiers;
6. source-digest custody;
7. review binding;
8. explicit boundary;
9. projection loss;
10. unresolved state.

## Decision rule

- **Pivot:** the formograph retains nothing beyond the graph and imposes no
  consequential conformance requirement.
- **Survive narrowed:** the graph can encode the complete information, but the
  formograph requires consequential custody and loss invariants.
- **Survive strong:** the graph cannot encode consequential information without
  changing its representation model.

## Structured failure

The accepted formograph must declare selected and omitted projection content,
a loss note, and retained authority. A fixture without omitted content must
fail with `FG-PROJECTION-LOSS-MISSING`.

## Boundary

This one governance-domain case cannot establish a field, cross-domain
repeatability, independent coding, or reader value.
