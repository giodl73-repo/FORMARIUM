## Product answer

The synthetic evidence supports this positioning hypothesis:

> A dictionary tells you what a word means. A thesaurus shows neighboring
> words. Factorium helps determine which concept applies, how several concepts
> fit together, what conditions change the answer, and where inference must
> stop.

Factorium therefore appears strongest as a **second reference move** and as a
**problem-led structural reference**, not as a universal replacement for
dictionaries, thesauri, encyclopedias, handbooks, web search, or local
documentation.

## Decision

Carry the two complementary entrances—**look up a concept** and **work through
a question**—into future real-reader observation. Admit no content, alias,
search, persona-conditioned UI, or `sim-43` change from this synthetic round.
The known available/unusable scenario was intentionally selected after QLD-02
and does not count as independent recurrence.
