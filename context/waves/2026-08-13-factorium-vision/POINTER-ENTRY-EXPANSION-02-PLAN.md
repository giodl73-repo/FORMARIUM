# Pointer Entry Expansion 02 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: active internal simulation plan

## Objective

Continue the explicit Pointer Entry concordance with a third bounded registry.
Preserve `sim-52` at 20 labels and `sim-53` at 40 labels; add 20 labels in
`sim-54` for a total of 60.

## Selection

The added labels are exact reusable terms present in at least 10 owning Tables:

`assumptions`, `behavior`, `capacity`, `claim`, `contract`, `criteria`,
`domain`, `interval`, `measure`, `measurement`, `observation`, `population`,
`quantity`, `representation`, `resource`, `result`, `review`, `revision`,
`scenario`, and `value`.

The batch avoids plural variants of already admitted concepts such as
`conditions`, `constraints`, `resources`, `roles`, and `states`. It also keeps
the prior exclusion of `reference`, whose token appears in repository paths.

## Product value

This batch connects recurring evidence, evaluation, quantity, representation,
and review vocabulary to exact owning Tables. It does not collapse nearby
terms: result remains distinct from outcome, measure from measurement, and
criteria from any later singular criterion review.

## Scope and stop

Require exactly 60 rows in the successor registry, at least one occurrence per
row, source-custodied owner grouping, and unchanged canonical search. Stop
before morphological normalization, automatic synonym handling, generated
orientation text, phrase mining, canonical promotion, or reader-value claims.
