# R5P - Proof Set

Status: internal simulation preflight active; external preview deferred

Parent milestone: R5 - First substantial volume

Required predecessor for evidence-bearing distribution: R4E - The Sieve

Parallel editorial track: R5A - Factor Forge

## Why this codename

A proof set is a bounded edition prepared for inspection before publication.
For Factorium it also evokes a mathematical set: the preview contains a fixed,
enumerable selection whose identity and review state can be checked without
claiming that it is the whole reference.

## Goal

Determine whether invited practitioners can independently navigate,
understand, and apply a frozen Factorium proof edition to real, non-sensitive
problems—including problems that require several concepts to be composed—then
choose to return for another use, without repeated unresolved critical
blockers.

## Evidence boundary

Proof Set follows rather than replaces The Sieve. The Sieve supplies moderated
task evidence for the frozen R4 prototype. Proof Set evaluates a repaired,
self-guided release candidate that may include selected reviewed R5 work.
Neither round grants evidence coverage to content it did not contain.

The cohort is deliberately small. Counts and observations support a release
decision for the previewed edition; they are not statistical validation,
market demand evidence, proof of domain authority, or a broad effectiveness
claim.

Unless separate confidential distribution terms are established, `private`
means invited and non-promoted. Do not promise secrecy for repository content.
Do not solicit confidential participant work examples.

## Entry gate

Do not tag or distribute `preview-01` until all of the following are true:

- R4E has an observed gate decision from 3-5 target-reader sessions;
- relevant Sieve findings are deliberately forward-applied and dispositioned;
- one coherent selected volume is curated from reviewed content;
- the candidate has deterministic identity and an immutable manifest;
- every included entry, view, relation, guide, and publication surface passes
  its applicable validation and review-assurance checks;
- local links, generated navigation, sources, maturity, and revision custody
  are complete for the candidate;
- no applicable `.roles` review has an open critical or major finding;
- one rendered proof edition and one-page quickstart are available;
- invitation, consent, feedback, accessibility, and disposition materials are
  ready.

The gate is coherence- and evidence-driven. It does not require an arbitrary
intermediate entry count, the approximately 250-entry R5 target, or Workbench.

## Internal simulation preflight

Simulation may proceed before R4E closes because it does not admit or
distribute the preview candidate. It may:

- walk synthetic scenarios through every prescribed task route;
- verify that volume, catalog, entry, specialized-view, prime, and guide links
  reach the expected material;
- compare plausible first selections with the evaluator contract;
- predict terminology, navigation, layout, trust, and accessibility risks;
- repair defects visible from the artifacts themselves;
- rehearse manifest, rendering, quickstart, and feedback mechanics.
- rehearse a manual Composition Query worksheet whose seed concepts, sense
  choices, admitted relations, closure steps, exclusions, unresolved frontier,

observation files. They MUST NOT invent participant identity, behavior,

### Composition-query rehearsal boundary

Proof Set may test whether a fixed edition contains the material needed for a
declared multi-concept problem and whether one author-specified closure trace
can be rendered as a readable worksheet. The rehearsal may exercise these
candidate operations:

- add a seed concept;
- join selected senses through one admitted typed relation;
- record an exclusion or required-node contradiction;
- run an explicitly named Constraint, Diagnostic, Procedure, Formula-scope, or
  completeness check;
- flatten the resulting working graph into a traceable provisional Factor
  Guide.

The worksheet declares `complete`, `incomplete`, `contradictory`, or
`truncated`; records finite traversal budgets; and carries a manifest of every
included, excluded, unresolved, and projected node and edge. Operator labels
such as add, multiply, subtract, evaluate, and flatten are reading aids only,
not mathematical or semantic equations.

The rehearsal MUST NOT infer semantic edges from prose, silently choose among
senses or Context Profiles, present all reachable links as required closure,
generate domain conclusions, or claim that the author-declared trace predicts
reader behavior. Automated graph expansion, interactive construction,
recommendation, persistence, and publishing remain R6 Workbench scope.

## Candidate artifacts

- tag `preview-01` and a manifest identifying exact source revisions;
- a rendered static HTML or PDF proof edition;
- a concise reader quickstart;
- when admitted, one bounded composition worksheet with its exact closure
  trace and flattened Factor Guide projection;
- an invitation and consent brief adapted from The Sieve materials;
- a de-identified feedback and use log;
- a finding and disposition ledger;
- a final preview gate audit and role review.

Only one rendered format is required for the first round. Accessibility
findings must be recorded and dispositioned; format multiplication is not a
substitute for testing the chosen edition.

## Cohort and round

Invite 8-12 readers across at least three broad practitioner backgrounds:

- software or systems;
- scientific, engineering, analytical, or quantitative;
- product, operations, organizational, or general reference;
- accessibility or assistive-technology perspective when feasible.

One reader may satisfy more than one perspective. These are coverage goals,
not demographic classifications. Keep names, contact details, schedules, and
invitation responses outside the repository. Commit only participant codes,
broad relevant background, voluntary access context, task outcomes, and short
de-identified observations.

Run the round self-guided over a bounded period. A reader should:

1. use the quickstart without live teaching;
2. complete one prescribed lookup, comparison, or application task;
3. apply Factorium to one self-selected, non-sensitive scenario;
4. record blockers, uncertainty, and what they expected to find;
5. receive a later invitation to return for a second use.

Assistance is allowed for access failures but must be recorded. Assisted work
does not count as independent completion for the affected task.

## Exit gate

The first private-preview round may close only when:

- at least 8 invited readers receive the frozen edition;
- at least 6 independently complete onboarding and the prescribed task;
- at least 5 complete a self-selected real application;
- at least 4 return for a second use without moderator prompting;
- every reported failure or assisted task is classified and dispositioned;
- accessibility observations have an owner and disposition;
- no repeated critical trust, navigation, accessibility, or semantic blocker
  remains open;
- revisions introduce no open critical or major `.roles` finding;
- the gate audit records one decision: `proceed`, `repair and rerun`, or
  `narrow`.

These counts are minimum release evidence, not population estimates. A
`proceed` decision permits continued preparation for the separate R5 public
publication gate; it does not itself publish or promote the volume.

## Failure ownership

Factor preview failures before repairing them. Candidate owners include:

- canonical sense or factor content;
- specialized-view selection or scope;
- volume selection and chapter order;
- index, cross-reference, or rendered navigation;
- quickstart or task wording;
- visual scanning or accessibility;
- source, maturity, or trust presentation;
- mismatch between the preview audience and the reference promise.

Preserve original observations. Record revisions and return-use outcomes as
new evidence rather than rewriting the initial record.

## Non-goals

- public launch or general availability;
- completion of the approximately 250-entry R5 volume;
- statistically representative usability or market validation;
- Workbench, chat, or application implementation;
- automated semantic-edge inference, unrestricted graph traversal, or
  generated domain conclusions;
- monetization or pricing experiments;
- broad semantic, scientific, or domain-authority claims;
- confidential participant data or work products;
- retroactive evidence coverage for unpreviewed content.

## Execution sequence

1. Run internal simulation preflights and repair author-detectable defects.
2. Close The Sieve from real reader evidence when readers are available.
3. Forward-apply and review relevant findings.
4. Select the book-shaped preview candidate.
5. Validate, review, render, manifest, freeze, and tag `preview-01`.
6. Recruit and distribute to the invited cohort.
7. Collect prescribed, self-selected, and return-use evidence.
8. Factor and disposition every failure.
9. Rerun when critical or major revisions invalidate prior evidence.
10. Publish the de-identified gate audit and decision.
