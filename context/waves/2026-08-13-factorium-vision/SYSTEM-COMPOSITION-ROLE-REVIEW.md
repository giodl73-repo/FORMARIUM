## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCD-001 | critical | `system` could be reduced to a collection of components. | Closed: orientation, factorization, and constraints require interacting elements, a selected whole, and relevant behavior, meaning, or purpose. |
| SCD-002 | critical | Purpose could be universalized to natural and conceptual systems. | Closed: purpose is scoped to engineered and organizational systems; the general sense also admits behavior or meaning. |
| SCD-003 | critical | System-element membership could silently imply component ownership. | Closed: membership is boundary- and view-relative; component claims require part-whole, replacement, and lifecycle semantics. |
| SCD-004 | critical | Capability could be equated with a component, interface, or named mechanism. | Closed: capability requires beneficiary, outcome, conditions, and evaluation basis. |
| SCD-005 | major | Boundary and interface could collapse into one line on a diagram. | Closed: boundary owns inclusion; interface owns participants, exchange, contract, direction, and failure behavior. |
| SCD-006 | critical | Dependency could become an undirected synonym for relationship. | Closed: source, target, required property, condition, consequence, and time scope are mandatory. |
| SCD-007 | major | External dependencies could be redrawn as internal components without an explicit viewpoint change. | Closed: environment and boundary rules require a recorded revision when assignments change. |
| SCD-008 | major | NASA and NIST engineering definitions could be presented as a universal ontology. | Closed: research limits source authority, retains SEBoK's broader physical/conceptual distinction, and keeps Factorium synthesis at candidate maturity. |
| SCD-009 | major | V0 could imply machine-queryable typed relations it does not encode. | Closed: direction and relation semantics remain in linked Markdown; richer payloads are deferred to a versioned successor. |
| SCD-010 | major | Whole-level behavior could be attributed to one component without evidence. | Closed: the Constraint view requires a behavior model, observation, or test for such attribution. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate publication.
