## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| RDL-001 | critical | "Unique" differentiation could become an uncited marketing claim. | Closed: the schema permits scoped contribution language and requires separate competitive evidence for uniqueness claims. |
| RDL-002 | critical | A Reference Delta could caricature or rank other reference forms instead of complementing them. | Closed: comparator ownership, non-duplication, source identity, scope, and confidence are required. |
| RDL-003 | major | Repeating generic comparison prose in every entry would break size budgets and create copy drift. | Closed: only substantial and family-defining entries require deltas; brief and prime entries may inherit. |
| RDL-004 | critical | The physical Formula Table could continue to masquerade as the canonical headword for every meaning of force. | Closed: `tables/entries/force.md` owns the headword and the Formula Table is a linked physical-sense view. |
| RDL-005 | critical | Lexical proximity among physical force, coercion, organization, influence, and validity could create false joins. | Closed: five separate senses have distinct taglines, factors, contexts, and source gates. |
| RDL-006 | major | The force sense inventory could be presented as lexically exhaustive. | Closed: the entry explicitly labels the five senses a pilot inventory. |
| RDL-007 | major | Reference comparison could duplicate copyrighted definitions, synonym lists, or encyclopedia prose. | Closed: the schema requires summary of reader-task ownership and linking rather than copying. |
| RDL-008 | major | Reference Delta could be mistaken for an Evidence Table and duplicate provenance authority. | Closed: it is mandatory comparative metadata; Evidence Tables remain views for evidence-comparison tasks. |
| RDL-009 | major | Non-physical force factorizations could look professionally endorsed. | Closed: all remain `candidate` and explicitly require specialist legal, ethical, organizational, and social review. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate publication.
