## Portfolio verdict

Factorium is shaping into a coherent structured reference rather than a loose
table collection. Its strongest property is **custody of distinctions**:
sense, factor, role, mechanism, constraint, method, result, inference,
authority, control, and outcome are separately named and reviewed. The
canonical interchange and generated projections make that discipline
reproducible.

The current corpus is credible at `candidate` maturity. It is not yet a
publishable 250-entry volume because external lookup evidence, broader
qualified domain review, balanced family growth, unresolved-candidate triage,
and scaling automation remain gates.

## Shape assessment

### What is differentiating

- **Polysemy plus structure:** one headword can own several materially
  different senses without sharing one false decomposition.
- **Reader-question families:** Factor, Formula, Mapping, Decision,
  Transition, Constraint, Procedure, Diagnostic, Scale, Reference Value, and
  Evidence views answer distinct lookup questions.
- **Canonical metadata with readable publication bodies:** software validates
  identity and projections without becoming editorial authority.
- **Non-equivalence discipline:** roles, mechanisms, records, formulas, and
  organizational assignments remain contextual rather than exact by naming.
- **Visible epistemic stages:** observation, measurement, result, inference,
  claim, evidence implication, confidence, limitation, and provenance retain
  separate custody.
- **Versioned evidence honesty:** candidate maturity, unresolved terms,
  historical snapshots, and external gates remain visible.

### What most needs protection during growth

- concise entries rather than encyclopedic sprawl;
- source scope and qualified review;
- selection procedures and failure signs;
- graph coherence without admitting every unresolved word;
- family balance driven by reader questions;
- stable IDs and explicit migrations;
- separation of author confidence from reader evidence.
