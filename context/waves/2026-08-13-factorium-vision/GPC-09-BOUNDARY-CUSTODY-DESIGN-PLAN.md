# GPC-09 Boundary and Custody Design Batch Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: design complete; source/admission review is next

## Scope

Execute only the three designs earned by the 100-record GPC-09
reconciliation:

1. one candidate anchor boundary for semantic meaning, reference,
   interpretation, and use;
2. one epistemic-standing, inquiry, and warrant Evidence Table candidate;
3. one source, performance, and research-custody Evidence Table candidate.

This is the first reopened Factor Forge slice after F100 and therefore remains
capped at one anchor and two views. It adds no philosophy tradition, thinker,
school, community, regional lane, UI mechanic, reader claim, normative
guidance, or canonical reference record.

## Owner test

Audit `information-data-signal-noise`, `claim-evidence`,
`decomposition-modes-combination-contracts`, `organization-role-authority`,
and `identity-naming-classification-versioning` before proposing ownership.
Reuse an owner when its governing question is adequate. A familiar word or
cross-tradition recurrence does not earn a new anchor.

## Product test

The three designs must make these reader tasks materially clearer:

- “What is being claimed to be known, by whom, and on what warrant?”
- “What does this expression mean here, and what fixes or changes that
  interpretation?”
- “Who may share or interpret this source, performance, or research record,
  for which audience and purpose?”

The Tables remain the authority. The Reader, website, search, and Composition
Query may route to these records later but cannot supply their semantics.

## Exit gate

The batch exits only when a specification declares sense boundaries, factors,
dependencies, invalid combinations, source obligations, book projection,
compatibility effects, role findings, repository checks, and an explicit
admit/defer/reject decision for each of the three items.
