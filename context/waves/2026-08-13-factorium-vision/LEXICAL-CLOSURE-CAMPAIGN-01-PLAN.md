# Lexical Closure Campaign 01 Plan

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: approved for freeze; execution not yet started

Evidence class: external lexical-source audit, not reader evidence

## Product question

Across all 53 Factorium entries and 412 declared senses, which immediate
dictionary or thesaurus neighbors repeatedly point toward a distinction that
the present Tables cannot own without distortion?

This is a source-backed reopening test. It is not permission to mirror a
dictionary, fill every vocabulary branch, or turn common and proper nouns into
Factorium concepts.

## Frozen method

Use only Open English WordNet 2025 common words. Exclude Plus and Namenet. Match
each Factorium sense label to noun synset members by exact lowercase,
space-normalized spelling. Do not stem, fuzz, or select among ambiguous synsets.

For exact matches, inspect one hop of synonyms, hypernyms, inverse hyponyms,
and member, part, and substance meronym/holonym relations. Every unmatched
Factorium sense remains in the denominator and is reported as not audited by
this lexical source.

A review candidate must be absent as an exact Factorium sense and recur from
at least three Factorium senses across at least two entries. Recurrence ranks
manual review; it does not admit content.

## Disposition and admission

Every gated candidate receives one disposition from the frozen vocabulary.
The preferred outcome is an existing owner, Guide-local term, or external
taxonomy. A new sense, alias, cross-reference, or view needs a precise current
owner and a book-native reader job. A new anchor additionally requires the
same reusable governing distinction to recur and no current entry able to own
it without distortion.

The maximum downstream batch is one anchor and two views. Any admitted batch
must be frozen and researched separately before content changes. This campaign
itself changes no Table, reference row, schema, relation, search rank, book,
or maintained `sim-46` artifact.

## Validation and stop

The checker must bind the exact reference and source archive hashes, process
53/53 entries and 412/412 senses, retain ambiguity, enumerate every gated
candidate, and verify every disposition. Stop with a null when lexical pressure
is local, generic, already owned, external, or below recurrence. Claim no
findability, comprehension, usefulness, preference, adoption, or reader need.
