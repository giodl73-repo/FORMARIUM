## Reopening triggers

Reopen internal work only when at least one of these is concrete and recorded:

1. an author- or tool-observable broken route, rendering defect, accessibility
   mechanic, inconsistent identity, or failed regression;
2. a repeated unresolved owner or repeated manual composition need;
3. a source-backed content gap or demonstrably overloaded canonical owner;
4. a real R4E or later reader observation with an assigned failure owner;
5. a dependency, browser, security, or format change that breaks the maintained
   artifact.

Every reopening names the reader job, smallest repair, evidence level,
compatibility boundary, and stop condition. Counts, roadmap slots, visual
novelty, and domain symmetry are not triggers.

## Next action when readers exist

Run the matching prepared R4E worksheet for the first real participant. Do not
create participant data, a `preview-01` tag, or an evidence claim before that
session. After 3–5 completed R4E sessions, disposition the findings and decide
whether a separate Proof Set candidate should be selected and frozen.

Until then, the correct repository state is maintenance without speculative
edition growth.
