## Disposition

Every gap was classified. Route loss is concentrated in two different product
owners: Compose lacks an ordinary-language-to-controls bridge for seven
questions, while Reader is an editorial teaching sequence rather than a
task-specific route. Combining those into one feature would fail the
single-owner and smallest-batch rules, so both are deferred.

The missing outward handoff affects all 25 assignments across all three
entrances and has one owner, the publication shell. It passes the frozen owner
test. The admitted repair is one static, reversible **ephemeral handoff note**
on every generated page. It contains:

1. a user-entered safe question or situation;
2. the generated current Factorium page identity;
3. user-entered unresolved work; and
4. a user-entered next authoritative source.

Copy, Print, and Clear are available. The note has no network access, account,
cookie, URL task state, local or session storage, canonical write, verification,
or history. Reload clears it. User text is explicitly unverified and not
stored. The component does not recover the original task automatically.

## Product decision

Keep `sim-44` as the current internal artifact and stop this synthetic slice.
Do not add broad natural-language routing, Reader personalization, task
history, accounts, or persistence from these authored proxies. When external
readers become available, ask whether they recognize the three entrances,
whether re-entering a safe task is acceptable, and whether the handoff note is
worth carrying to an authoritative source. R4E and `preview-01` remain open.
