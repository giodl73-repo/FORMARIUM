# MUNDUS Curriculum Closure 01 Plan

Status: inventory review in progress; no concept admission authorized

## Purpose

Use the courseware registered through MUNDUS to test whether the current V1
concept graph omits reusable governing concepts. MUNDUS is the discovery
bridge. FONTES owns the detailed course records, source surfaces, rights
metadata, and custody used by this campaign.

The campaign is a curriculum-pressure test, not a claim that a curriculum is
complete, authoritative, consensual, or evidence of reader demand.

## Custody baseline

- Read MUNDUS and FONTES without modifying either repository.
- Freeze the exact MUNDUS and FONTES commits, the MUNDUS-to-FONTES registry
  bridge, every included FONTES source file, and normalized canonical URLs.
- Commit only derived inventory, analysis, and admitted Factorium artifacts to
  Factorium.
- Treat assigned textbooks and third-party links as metadata-only unless their
  own source record explicitly permits more.

## Two-pass review

### Pass A: breadth census

Normalize every registered course surface. Individual course work records are
kept as course records. Grouped Stanford and Berkeley work records are not
counted as courses; their row-grain source-surface tables are expanded instead.
The census measures domain pressure and selects the depth portfolio. A title or
homepage identity cannot by itself justify a concept admission.

### Pass B: depth portfolio

Freeze 48 courses, six in each lane:

1. mathematics;
2. physical science;
3. life and health science;
4. computing and software;
5. AI, data, and inference;
6. engineering, systems, and control;
7. social science and humanities; and
8. economics and organizations.

Within each lane prefer a mix of introductory and advanced material, more than
one source family where available, one cross-disciplinary edge, and one
expected no-gap control. Course selection happens before comparison with the
Factorium closure.

## Evidence ladder

1. Homepage or title metadata: domain and curriculum pressure only.
2. Rights-compatible syllabus, session, or lecture headings: candidate
   vocabulary and instructional sequence.
3. Source-owned explanatory material with recorded custody: bounded evidence
   for a reusable distinction.
4. Assigned textbook or third-party material: metadata-only until separately
   cleared and promoted in FONTES.

Named people, named methods, institutions, products, software, standards,
course codes, and historically particular labels remain examples or external
references unless a separate boundary review establishes a reusable concept.

## Comparison and disposition

For every candidate, compare against the V1 closure and record exactly one:

- covered directly;
- covered compositionally;
- wording or discovery repair;
- missing reusable concept;
- proper noun or instance;
- domain-specific specialization;
- insufficient source evidence; or
- duplicate candidate.

The held economic income/consumption/saving/investment/wealth mapping competes
under these rules rather than receiving automatic priority.

## Admission ceiling

Across the whole campaign, admit at most one anchor, two views, zero relation
mechanics, and one discovery repair. Zero change is valid. Admit the smallest
batch that resolves repeated, cross-course pressure; do not turn course lists
into a domain encyclopedia.

## Required outputs

- frozen normalized course inventory and integrity checker;
- frozen 48-course depth portfolio;
- candidate ledger with evidence tier and disposition for every candidate;
- exact before/after closure rerun;
- role plan and result reviews in this wave;
- final portfolio decision with claims boundaries.

## Claims boundary

This synthetic, source-led campaign can identify repeated curriculum pressure
against the current Factorium structure. It cannot show curriculum consensus,
pedagogical effectiveness, reader comprehension, reader demand, or real-world
usefulness.
