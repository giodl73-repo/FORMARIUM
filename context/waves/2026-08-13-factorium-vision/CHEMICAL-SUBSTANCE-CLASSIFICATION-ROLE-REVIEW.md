## Dispositions

| Review concern | Result | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Reusable factorization | pass after findings | Level, composition, charge, structure, criterion, constituent role, and reporting basis are independent pivots with declared dependencies. |
| Concept-taxonomy boundary | pass after rewrite | Named families are examples; the canon owns the rule that generates and distinguishes them. |
| Chemical terminology | pass for candidate | IUPAC supports the entity, species, substance, ion, mixture, and solution spine within source scope. |
| Quantities and formulas | pass after findings | Charge balance and `MX_n` illustrate a constraint without becoming identification proof. |
| Claims and provenance | pass after findings | Classification does not certify identity, purity, safety, nutrition, health, or regulation. |
| Reader use | pass | Halide and sugar examples teach multiple concepts while remaining removable and swappable. |

## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| CSC-001 | major | Initial named-family senses crossed from conceptual reference into taxonomy. | Closed: replaced them with `compound-class` and `classification-criterion`. |
| CSC-002 | major | “Find all things at this layer” supplied no defensible stopping boundary. | Closed: rejected the atlas and added an explicit inclusion rule. |
| CSC-003 | major | Existing roles only implied this boundary. | Closed: added the Concept & Taxonomy Boundary Editor and indexed it. |
| CSC-004 | major | Molecule and compound could become synonyms. | Closed: entity kind and material/composition kind remain distinct. |
| CSC-005 | major | Ionic material could be described as discrete molecules. | Closed: added formula-unit and material-organization distinctions. |
| CSC-006 | major | Charge balance could be treated as identification. | Closed: constrained it to a teaching and consistency check. |
| CSC-007 | major | Chemical and reporting classes could appear equivalent. | Closed: classification criterion, authority, and version remain explicit. |
| CSC-008 | major | Generic classes could imply safety or nutritional conclusions. | Closed: explicit claim boundary and authority escalation added. |

No critical or major actionable finding remains open for candidate publication.
