# Tabula Facta Identity Preview Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: active reversible preview; name explicitly not locked

## Objective

Render one successor internal website edition that lets the author experience
`Tabula Facta` as the public name of the Tables while using the existing Reader.
The preview must make the candidate status visible instead of quietly behaving
like a completed repository rename.

## Reader job

Reader: someone deciding whether the names and two-book relationship feel
clear while browsing, searching, and following the Reader route.

Job: understand that Tabula Facta is the primary dictionary/thesaurus-like
reference, that The Factorium Reader is its selected teaching companion, and
that both still draw from one Factor Table authority.

Current friction: the Factorium project name and Factorium Tables book name
occupy the same visual layer, making it harder to feel the candidate publishing
identity independently from the internal system.

## Smallest complete change

1. add `sim-51` without changing `sim-50`;
2. show `Tabula Facta` on the home, Tables, Reader, chapter, and entry chrome;
3. keep `The Factorium Reader` unchanged;
4. show `candidate name` and `identity preview - not locked` disclosures;
5. retain Factor Tables as canonical authority and Factorium as the internal
   project/interchange identity; and
6. add focused static and browser checks for the identity and authority split.

## Stop and evidence rules

Stop after the reversible website projection is validated. Do not rename files,
schemas, `.factorium` formats, reference identities, source headings, repository
documentation, or historical editions. Do not infer reader preference, brand
fit, market distinctiveness, usability, or release readiness from this author
preview.
