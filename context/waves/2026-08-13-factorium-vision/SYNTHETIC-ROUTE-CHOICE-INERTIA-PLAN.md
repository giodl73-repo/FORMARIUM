## Objective

Test whether ordinary, term-blind prompts can expose the right Factorium
entrance and a bounded reason to leave or supplement the familiar
search-and-stitch chain.

## Frozen portfolio

Each of the 25 prior profile IDs receives one new ordinary-language prompt,
two literal queries, and one desired takeaway class. The five takeaway classes
appear exactly five times each:

- orient;
- distinguish;
- explain;
- decide-or-check;
- trace-or-compose.

The prompt may contain ordinary words that overlap Factorium, but it must not
name a destination, entry title, table family, profile, route, or expected
relationship outcome.

## Result questions

For every authored proxy journey record:

1. the exact incumbent tool sequence and its genuine advantage;
2. the exact first-five Factorium search results;
3. whether a selected destination appears without added canonical vocabulary;
4. the first plausible entrance: Search, Tables A–Z, Reader, Compose, or none;
5. the condition that would justify switching or supplementing;
6. the useful structural moment and the remaining friction;
7. a mechanically suitable view profile hypothesis, not a preference claim;
8. trust-supporting cues and trust blockers;
9. the stopping rule and outward handoff;
10. a possible future memory trigger and fun continuation, both authored;
11. an inertia relationship: stay-with-current, supplement, switch-for-
    structure, or not-served;
12. projection losses and change disposition.

## Boundaries

- Profiles never condition search ranking.
- Age, profession, and digital comfort never mechanically determine entrance,
  density, trust, inertia, or relationship.
- “Reaction,” “memory,” “trust,” and “preference” are authored hypotheses, not
  observations or internal states of a person.
- No completion, time, satisfaction, preference, comprehension, confidence,
  accessibility success, adoption, or return-use metric is permitted.
- No content or product repair is admitted from one authored proxy route.
- Known QLD and SUJ-01 scenarios do not become independent recurrence merely
  by appearing in this intentionally linked follow-up.
- No R4E or Proof Set gate can close from this campaign.
