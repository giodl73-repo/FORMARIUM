## Interpretation

No recurrent lexical neighbor demonstrates a missing Factorium owner or
governing distinction. The screen therefore admits zero anchors and zero views.
It also demonstrates why dictionary closure cannot stand in for conceptual
closure: common words are highly polysemous, technical multiword senses are
often absent, and lexical hierarchy frequently follows a meaning Factorium did
not intend.

The 150 unmatched senses remain an explicit source-coverage frontier. They are
not Factorium gaps. A later initiative should sample that frontier against
bounded domain glossaries only when a product or query signal recurs; it should
not expand this campaign by tuning or fuzzy matching.
