## Claim boundary

The strategies inspect artifacts, not simulated people. Results MUST NOT name
participants or report task success, completion rate, time, difficulty,
preference, quote, accessibility success, real application, or return use.
They cannot close R4, admit `preview-01`, or satisfy R5P reader gates.
