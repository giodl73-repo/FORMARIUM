## Decision

Do not invent F101 or add anchors for numerical balance. The current corpus has
no internally demonstrated owner gap. Factor Forge intake remains open, but a
new batch must be triggered by evidence rather than phase momentum.

## Evidence inspected

- 53 canonical entries and 95 specialized views;
- 11 typed relations, 21 endpoints, and 5 cross-entry links;
- 210 reviewed editorial pairs, one connected component, and coverage of all
  53 entries;
- zero generated unresolved candidates;
- exact Reader/proof routes with zero missing targets;
- the F91-F100 Product Owner stop on automatic content expansion.

The entry distribution is uneven—reference 16, systems 11, science 10,
governance 4, quantities 4, software 3, and six domains with one entry each.
That is descriptive, not an admission test. Equalizing counts would reward
taxonomy filling and navigation cost rather than reader value.

## Why no candidate passes now

No declared unresolved term lacks an owner; the closure graph has no disconnected
canonical island; current internal tasks and Guides record no repeated manual
concept requiring a new owner; and no observed reader failure, recurring
practitioner question, or qualified source campaign demonstrates that an
existing owner is overloaded.

## Reopening triggers

- **Repeated unresolved owner:** the same missing reusable concept occurs in at
  least two independently authored entries, Guides, or source campaigns.
- **Repeated composition need:** two bounded questions require the same manual
  node or distinction that no current entry can own without distortion.
- **Observed lookup failure:** reader evidence repeatedly lands on the wrong
  owner or cannot distinguish a materially different sense.
- **Source-backed domain gap:** authoritative sources identify a recurring
  governing distinction that current owners cannot represent.
- **Maintenance failure:** an existing entry is demonstrably overloaded and a
  split reduces ambiguity and maintenance more than it adds navigation cost.

Domain counts, a familiar term, an F-number target, or the desire to reach 250
entries do not reopen intake.

## Smallest admitted batch when triggered

One reader job and governing question; at most one new anchor and two separately
justified views; exact source basis, owner test, merge/defer list, cross-entry
consequences, assurance, neutral task, and fixed-point review. Every candidate
must also be considered as a sense, example, alias, cross-reference, Guide-local
concept, or external catalog instead of a new anchor.

## Product Owner disposition

**Stop now.** Maintain `sim-39`. Resume content only from a reopening trigger,
and resume external preview only when real readers are available. This does not
claim that Factorium is complete, balanced, optimal, or publication-ready.
