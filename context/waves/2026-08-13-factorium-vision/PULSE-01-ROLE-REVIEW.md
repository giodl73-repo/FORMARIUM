## Finding ledger

| ID | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| FAV-001 | critical | Software and experiments could remain the perceived product despite the new name. | Closed: the deliverable hierarchy makes books and Factor Tables primary and all software auxiliary. |
| FAV-002 | critical | A broad encyclopedia could present plausible AI decompositions as authoritative facts. | Closed: multiple candidates, counterexamples, maturity, provenance, and review are mandatory. |
| FAV-003 | major | Long explanatory essays could destroy dictionary-like lookup speed. | Closed: entries are table-first with a 120-word orientation budget and one-line sense taglines. |
| FAV-004 | major | Different meanings of a word could be mixed with alternative analyses of one meaning. | Closed: sense blocks and candidate factorizations are separate structural levels. |
| FAV-005 | major | One favored pivot could be presented as the natural hierarchy. | Closed: pivots are explicit, alternatives are expected, and subtype or exception explosions are failure signs. |
| FAV-006 | major | OO inheritance, Rust traits, or another mechanism could define the supposedly general vocabulary. | Closed: general factor roles precede cross-paradigm assignment tables. |
| FAV-007 | major | Unconstrained factor lists could imply every Cartesian combination is valid. | Closed: dependencies, exclusions, derivations, and contextual interpretations are required entry content. |
| FAV-008 | major | Existing synthetic semantic evidence could be stretched to justify the larger encyclopedia. | Closed: it is retained as one tested domain and methodological foundation, not proof of the grand vision. |
| FAV-009 | major | A premature symbol system could make entries opaque and difficult to revise. | Closed: textual factor-role vocabulary stabilizes before compact Factorium Marks. |
| FAV-010 | major | A collection of independently authored tables could drift into duplicate and contradictory authorities. | Closed: canonical entry identities, revisions, cross-references, relation types, and reference-architecture review are required. |
| FAV-011 | major | Readers might need to understand the Rust repository before using the reference. | Closed: the practitioner path begins with headwords, senses, tables, and examples; evidence is downstream. |

## Fixed point

Pulse 01 is complete. Factorium may proceed to a general factor-role
vocabulary. No critical or major finding remains open.
