# GPC-08 Indigenous, Central/Southeast Asian, and Oceanic Comparison Plan

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: inventory frozen; source review in progress

Freeze eighteen named packets before source interpretation: six
community-scoped Indigenous/First Peoples packets, six Central/Southeast Asian
packets not adequately represented by prior Buddhist or Islamic lanes, and six
community-scoped Oceanic/Pacific packets. Every packet must retain language,
place, speaker, genre/performance, audience, authority to share, researcher or
editor, archive, consent, translation, colonial/state relation, and internal
disagreement where sources expose them.

Use community-led, community-authorized, primary, or established regionally
grounded sources. Published access never authorizes extraction of restricted,
ceremonial, land-sensitive, or decontextualized knowledge. Each packet must
produce one immutable source-local factoring and typed candidate alignment.
No community, region, national synthesis, customary law, sovereignty claim,
spiritual practice, or decolonial program becomes Factorium guidance.

Stop after lane custody and freeze all PFC-01/GPC-02–08 inventories for the
one-pass GPC-09 reconciliation. Content decisions remain prohibited until that
global pass.
