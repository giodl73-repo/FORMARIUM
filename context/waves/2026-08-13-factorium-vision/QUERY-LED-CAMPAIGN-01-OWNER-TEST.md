## Existing-owner inspection

`tables/diagnostics/dependency-critical-path.md` already asks whether a
dependency, prerequisite, blocker, enabler, bottleneck, or critical path
actually explains the selected limit. It already distinguishes:

- aggregate spare capacity from a local bottleneck;
- a critical or near-critical path from general delay;
- a claimed blocker whose removal changes nothing;
- bottleneck as limiting capacity or constraint under conditions;
- critical path as a result-relative property of an exact graph and model.

Literal `bottleneck` search ranks this view first in `sim-41`. Therefore the
baseline's preliminary QG-3 classification does not admit another view, and
the compound-query result does not admit an alias or ranking change.

The repeated remaining work is narrower: readers must still manually decide
when the limiting condition is instead a service-order policy, a binding
feasibility or policy constraint, or an evidence limitation that prevents a
claim rather than constraining operational flow.
