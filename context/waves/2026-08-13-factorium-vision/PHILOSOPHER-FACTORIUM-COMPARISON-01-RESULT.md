# Philosopher-to-Factorium Comparison 01 Result

Date: 2026-08-17

Campaign: `PFC-01`

Decision: the comparison reveals eight recurrent gap families, but admits no
new canonical entry yet. Proceed only to boundary design for the three
highest-value families.

## What was compared

Sixteen thinkers were read as an initial longitudinal Western sample from
Socrates through Rawls. This is neither a ranked canon nor a representative
sample of philosophy. Each row records a governing question, reusable concepts,
decisive distinctions, present Factorium owners, mapping loss, and one of four
coverage states.

The comparison removes proper names from the admission test. “Categorical
imperative,” “veil of ignorance,” and “Forms,” for example, can remain source
provenance or examples; only recurrent underlying structure can become a
Factorium owner.

## Coverage result

| Coverage | Rows | Thinkers |

“Gap” at row level does not mean “create an entry for this philosopher.” It
means the row's governing distinctions cannot be reconstructed adequately from
current owners. A concept-family gap additionally requires recurrence in at
least three rows.

## What Factorium already does well

The current Tables can already carry important parts of these arguments:
claim/evidence, causation, choice, control, scenarios, obligation, authority,
models, time, lifecycle, force, and perspective. Epictetus's control distinction,
Hume's causal problem, Kant's obligation, Arendt's power/violence contrast, and
Rawls's choice scenario all find useful footholds.

The loss is equally important. Evidence support is not knowledge; preference
is not the good; compliance is not moral duty; economic value is not normative
value; information is not meaning; a lifecycle is not historical
interpretation; physical matter is not metaphysical substance; and operational
control is not human freedom.

## Portfolio decision

The smallest next batch is design, not content:

1. define the boundary and internal pivots for epistemic standing;
2. split the normative/practical family so value, virtue, duty, justice, and
   judgment do not become one overloaded Table;
3. determine whether the existing `Agency` root can be elaborated into a useful
   human-action owner without universalizing one philosophical school.

Defer canonical authoring, philosopher headwords, historical-completeness
claims, and reader-value claims. Freeze a later counter-sample across excluded
women philosophers and non-Western traditions before treating this result as a
portfolio-wide philosophy closure.

## Evidence boundary

The result is a source-grounded internal editorial comparison. It establishes
repeatable mapping pressure and explicit losses only. It does not establish
consensus readings of the philosophers, completeness, reader usefulness,
market differentiation, or superiority to dictionaries, thesauri, or
encyclopedias.
