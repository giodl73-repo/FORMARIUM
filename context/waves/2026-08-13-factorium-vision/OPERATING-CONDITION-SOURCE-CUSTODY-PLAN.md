# Operating-Condition Source-Custody Plan

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: approved for bounded execution

## Trigger and scope

After the `sim-42` internal preview rehearsal stopped without minting a new
edition, a read-only canonical audit found that 146 of 148 entry/view paths
had explicit source or provenance headings. The two missing paths are the
operating-condition failure diagnostic and the risk-treatment response
procedure.

The plan is a maintenance repair, not a new content wave:

1. preserve both canonical owners, senses, paths, statuses, and substantive
   tables;
2. add primary-source provenance with explicit domain limits and identify
   Factorium's cross-domain structures as editorial synthesis;
3. update the two exact assurance digests and review custody;
4. add a deterministic check that all 148 canonical entry/view paths expose a
   source, provenance, or references heading;
5. run repository, reference, sidecar, role, and source-custody validation;
6. do not mutate `sim-42`, mint `sim-43`, or claim reader or field evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop if the repair requires a new owner, sense, relation, treatment hierarchy,
safety prescription, or generated preview. Domain authority and current
regulation remain outside Factorium and control real-world practice.

## Plan review

The standard role packet is
`signals/roles/check/operating-condition-source-custody-roles-check-2026-08-17.md`.
It approves the two-view repair with three conditions: bind every source to a
bounded use, label the cross-domain structures as editorial synthesis, and
prove that the canonical inventory and relation sidecar do not expand.
