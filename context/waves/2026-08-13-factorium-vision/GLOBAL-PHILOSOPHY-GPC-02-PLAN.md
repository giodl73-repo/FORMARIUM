# GPC-02 South Asian and Transregional Buddhist Comparison Plan

Date: 2026-08-17  
Status: executed

## Decision to execute

Freeze 18 disputed position-spaces across eight South Asian and ten
transregional Buddhist packets. Complete a standard literature review before
factoring. For every packet, preserve the source question, pivot, source terms,
factors, dependencies, constraints, alternatives, exclusions, and interpretive
status. Only then append typed, directional candidate alignments to current

## Value and boundary

The future reader job is to tell whether familiar English labels such as
`self`, `knowledge`, `practice`, `dependence`, or `liberation` denote the same
structure before using them together. This campaign can establish source-
supported structural pressure and mapping loss. It cannot establish reader
value, exhaustive cultural coverage, canonical equivalence, or content
admission.
