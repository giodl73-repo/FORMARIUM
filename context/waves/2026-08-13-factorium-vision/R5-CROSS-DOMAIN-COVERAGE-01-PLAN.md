# R5 Cross-Domain Coverage Campaign 01 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18

Status: frozen before corpus comparison

Evidence class: authoritative-source pressure plus internal authored routes;
not reader evidence or qualified domain guidance

## Objective

Test whether the current V1 reference can reconstruct 24 governing questions
drawn from authoritative organization, operations, information, and economics
sources. Classify every pressure point without treating a domain count,
familiar term, standard, or source taxonomy as an entitlement to canon.

## Product contract

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Reader | A practitioner distinguishing, composing, diagnosing, tracing, or teaching a recurring domain concept. |
| Job | Reach a reusable governing distinction and its boundary without reconstructing it from unrelated owners. |
| Current friction | V1 is broad but uneven; source frameworks may require distinctions that its 54 anchors and 97 views express only indirectly. |
| Product change | At most one anchor and two views, or a smaller existing-owner/search repair, earned by exact source and route evidence. |
| Evidence now | Source wording, V1 lookup/closure mechanics, owner pressure, nulls, and maintenance cost. |
| Evidence later | Reader findability, comprehension, practical correctness, usefulness, and return use. |
| Cost and displacement | Every addition increases book navigation, assurance, migration, and review burden; Workbench and external gates remain deferred. |
| Continue/merge/stop | Stop after one exact portfolio and its smallest admitted batch; zero change is valid. |

## Frozen portfolio

The fixture `fixtures/coverage/r5-cross-domain-coverage-01.json` contains six
questions per domain and one question for each established reader job:
find/distinguish, compare/choose, diagnose/explain, compose/design,
audit/trace, and teach/transfer. Every packet declares a forbidden inference,
stop condition, candidate lookup hypotheses, and authoritative source
pressure before current-corpus scoring.

The source inventory is bounded to OECD and ISO for organizations, NIST and
NASA for operations, W3C and NIST for information, and UN Statistics and IMF
for economics. These sources supply pressure, not a universal ontology.

## Classification and owner test

Reuse QG-0 through QG-9 from the query-led blueprint. Test apparent content
gaps in this order: current sense/factor, existing view, wording or alias,
cross-reference, existing-owner revision, new view, Guide-local treatment,
external authority, then new anchor. A source taxonomy remains external unless
its governing distinction recurs and cannot be represented without distortion.

Admission requires both:

1. support from at least two independently governed packets or two materially
   independent authoritative frameworks; and
2. a demonstrated reduction in reader reconstruction that exceeds navigation,
   assurance, and migration cost.

Ceilings are one anchor, two views, zero new relations, and one discovery
repair. They are ceilings, not targets. Proper nouns, named standards,
institution types, economic classifications, operational methods, and data
formats remain external or illustrative.

## Execution

1. Validate and commit the frozen fixture against V1 and `sim-49` custody.
2. Run exact ordinary-language search and owner inspection for all 24 packets.
3. Record selected current owners, manual distinctions, route state, QG owner,
   and disposition without rewriting packet questions.
4. Cluster by governing distinction, not source vocabulary or domain count.
5. Research only surviving candidates and implement the smallest admitted
   batch, including a valid zero-change result.
6. Rerun the exact portfolio, pass role/repository checks, and record stop,
   defer, merge, or continue.

## Claim boundary

This campaign may show source-backed conceptual pressure and internal route
coverage. It cannot show that a reader would find, understand, trust, apply,
or return to Factorium, and it cannot establish that the four domains or the
reference are complete.
