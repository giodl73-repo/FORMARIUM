## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAF-001 | critical | Names could own entity identity. | Closed: continuity, namespace identifier, and mutable names are separate. |
| SAF-002 | major | Classification could be treated as intrinsic identity. | Closed: scheme, purpose, authority, and effective period are required. |
| SAF-003 | critical | SemVer could become a universal version model. | Closed: it is a scoped Mapping view requiring a public API. |
| SAF-004 | critical | State, event, and transition could collapse into status strings. | Closed: condition, occurrence, guarded relation, and projection are separate. |
| SAF-005 | major | Lifecycle could be treated as one universal sequence. | Closed: the Transition pilot is explicitly illustrative and purpose-relative. |
| SAF-006 | critical | Policy prose could be assumed executable. | Closed: intent, rule, constraint, decision, and enforcement roles are separate. |
| SAF-007 | critical | A derived decision could be counted as an input. | Closed: decision outputs and original facts have separate contracts. |
| SAF-008 | critical | Exceptions could silently rewrite policy. | Closed: scope, authority, controls, expiry, review, and underlying policy remain visible. |
| SAF-009 | major | Specialized table families could overlap ambiguously. | Closed: each view declares one family and governing reader question. |
| SAF-010 | major | Standards notation could displace canonical Factorium meaning. | Closed: SCXML, BPMN, DMN, URI, and SemVer remain scoped sources/mappings. |

No critical or major finding remains open.
