# R4E - The Sieve

Status: next milestone; ready to recruit external readers

Parent milestone: R4 - Volume prototype

## Why this codename

A mathematical sieve filters candidates by repeated tests. Factorium's Sieve
does the editorial equivalent: realistic reader tasks expose distinctions,
navigation paths, and applications that survive use, while weak wording,
hidden constraints, and misleading routes become visible for repair.

The Sieve is not a popularity test and does not discard difficult concepts
merely because they require learning. It tests whether the reference provides
the structure needed to learn and apply them.

## Work packages

### S1 - Recruit the round

Recruit 3-5 readers:

- at least one software or systems practitioner;
- at least one scientific, engineering, analytical, or quantitative reader;
- at least two practitioner backgrounds overall;
- an accessibility or assistive-technology perspective when feasible.

Use participant codes only. Do not commit names or contact information.

### S2 - Dry-run the session mechanics

With a colleague who is not counted as evidence:

- verify repository or rendered-volume access;
- time the introduction and task transitions;
- confirm that prompts do not reveal expected terminology;
- confirm that the moderator can record paths and assistance consistently;
- correct protocol defects before the first counted session.

The dry run validates mechanics, not reader usability.

### S3 - Run observed sessions

For each reader:

- use the scripted introduction;
- assign 4-5 rotated tasks;
- encourage think-aloud without teaching;
- record task start/end, path, outcome, assistance, errors, and difficulty;
- capture only short de-identified quotes;
- stop or skip any task the participant declines.

### S4 - Factor the failures

Classify each issue by source:

- canonical content;
- terminology or sense boundary;
- specialized-view selection;
- navigation or link placement;
- chapter order;
- guide narrowing;
- layout or scanning;
- accessibility;
- protocol or task wording.

Do not treat every failure as an entry defect. A task, index, guide, or volume
projection may own the repair.

### S5 - Repair and rerun

- close every repeated critical blocker;
- disposition every failed or assisted task;
- rerun affected tasks after critical or major changes;
- regenerate reference projections when canonical metadata changes;
- preserve original observations and add revision outcomes rather than
  rewriting history.

### S6 - Gate R4

Publish the observed gate decision:

- participant and background counts;
- task completion and timing summaries;
- repeated blockers;
- issue dispositions;
- revisions made;
- rerun results;
- remaining limitations.

Only then may R4 complete and **R5 - Factor Forge** begin.

## Exit gate

- 3-5 completed target-reader sessions;
- at least two practitioner backgrounds;
- median successful sense-location time no more than 3 minutes;
- at least 80% unassisted core-task completion as a directional heuristic;
- no repeated open critical blocker;
- every failed or assisted task dispositioned;
- accessibility observations assigned and dispositioned;
- no open critical or major `.roles` finding caused by the revisions.

These thresholds guide release decisions; they are not statistical population
claims.
