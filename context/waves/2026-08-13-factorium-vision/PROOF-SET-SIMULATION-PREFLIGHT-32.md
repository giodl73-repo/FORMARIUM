## Product decision and claim boundary

Continue the two-book model and stop mixed-book naming. This simulation proves
architecture, routing, deterministic custody, responsive layout, and
compatibility. It does not establish dictionary completeness, thesaurus
quality, lookup success, teaching effectiveness, comprehension, preference,
accessibility success, return use, external-preview readiness, or publication
readiness.
