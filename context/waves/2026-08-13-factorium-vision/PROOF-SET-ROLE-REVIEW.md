# Proof Set Milestone Role Review

Status: fixed point for milestone specification

Date: 2026-08-14

## Plan review

Review the proposed private-preview bridge before admitting it to the roadmap.
The review must protect these boundaries:

- observed Sieve evidence precedes the preview;
- the preview evaluates a frozen book edition, not Workbench;
- editorial quality and coherence outrank an intermediate entry count;
- small-cohort observations do not become broad validation claims;
- participant identity and confidential work remain outside the repository;
- preview completion does not waive the R5 public-publication gate.

## Findings and closure

| PSP-006 | major | An intermediate entry target could reward bulk growth instead of a coherent book. | Closed: candidate admission is coherence- and review-driven without an arbitrary count. |
| PSP-007 | major | Receipt or one moderated task could masquerade as recurring product use. | Closed: independent prescribed use, self-selected application, and later return have separate minimum counts. |

| PSP-009 | major | Participant data or confidential work could leak into version control. | Closed: identity, contacts, schedules, responses, and confidential examples stay outside the repository; committed observations are de-identified. |

No critical or major finding remains open in the milestone specification.

## Result review

The roadmap now has a bounded release bridge between moderated prototype
testing and the first substantial public volume. Its predecessor, artifact,
audience, evidence types, minimum completion counts, claim boundary, failure
ownership, and decision outcomes are explicit. Books and Factor Tables remain
the product; Workbench remains downstream.

This review approves the specification, not preview execution. R5P remains
blocked from candidate admission until The Sieve closes from real reader
observations and its relevant findings are forward-applied.
