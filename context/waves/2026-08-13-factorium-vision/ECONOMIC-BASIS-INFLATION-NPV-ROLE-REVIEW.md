## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| EBI-001 | major | Accounting basis could collapse into cash timing. | Closed: authority-owned recognition and measurement remain explicit. |
| EBI-002 | major | Cash/accrual examples could be presented as universal exhaustive senses. | Closed: they remain scoped examples under one reusable basis sense. |
| EBI-003 | major | One item price change could be called general inflation. | Closed: population, basket, weights, and scope are required. |
| EBI-004 | major | Index level could be confused with inflation rate or spatial cost level. | Closed: level, interval change, and spatial comparison remain distinct. |
| EBI-005 | major | Index-based conversions could claim universal purchasing power. | Closed: series scope and authority remain part of the mapping. |
| EBI-006 | major | NPV could omit or double-count the initial flow. | Closed: time-zero treatment and exact flow reconciliation are required. |
| EBI-007 | major | Nominal flows and real rates could mix. | Closed: price and rate bases must align. |
| EBI-008 | major | A positive NPV could be called realized profit or mandatory approval. | Closed: model output, accounting result, and decision policy remain separate. |
| EBI-009 | major | Uncertain rates, flows, inflation, and terminal values could disappear. | Closed: provenance and sensitivity ranges are required. |
| EBI-010 | major | Named standards, indexes, and valuation methods could expand without bound. | Closed: they remain authorities or examples. |

All 13 selected roles pass after amendments; no critical or major actionable
finding remains open for candidate publication.
