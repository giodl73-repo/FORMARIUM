# MUNDUS Curriculum Closure 01 Plan Role Review

Date: 2026-08-18
Decision: approve the source census; hold the depth portfolio and all concept
admission until the next frozen stage

## Reviewed artifact

`MUNDUS-CURRICULUM-CLOSURE-01-PLAN.md` and the generated 338-surface FONTES
inventory.

## Role findings

### Reference lexicographer

Approve with the evidence ladder. Course vocabulary is candidate language,
not an entry list. The ordered owner test and the proper-noun/instance filter
must run before any new anchor is proposed.

### Taxonomy boundary reviewer

Approve the admission ceiling and the explicit compositional-coverage
disposition. Repeated courses teaching a specialization do not establish a
universal category.

### Reference architect

Approve the two-pass shape. The broad census is a selection layer; the frozen
48-course portfolio and candidate ledger must remain derived artifacts. No
course mechanics belong in the Factor Table schema or relation model.

### Evidence and claims reviewer

Approve after separating homepage metadata from evidence-bearing instructional
surfaces. A metadata-only row may establish that a course surface exists and
nothing more. The final report must retain the synthetic/source-led claims
boundary.

### Domain source reviewer

Approve MUNDUS as discovery bridge and FONTES as source owner. The inventory
records both commits, the registry bridge digest, source-file digests, rights
status, and fetch policy. Third-party and assigned-textbook material stays
metadata-only.

### Research integrity reviewer

Approve with course selection frozen before Factorium comparison. Record
covered and no-gap results as carefully as missing candidates; zero admission
is a valid campaign outcome.

### Mapping integrity reviewer

Approve holding the economic mapping. It must compete with all other repeated
pressure and cannot enter merely because it was discovered first.

### Practitioner reviewer

Approve the eight-lane portfolio target, but flag a material source imbalance:
the 54 individual course records lean heavily toward computation, AI,
engineering, physics, chemistry, and games. The 284 broader Stanford/Berkeley
surfaces restore discovery breadth but remain metadata-only until reviewed.

### Product owner

Approve the inventory as a bounded, useful next investment. The product value
is finding high-leverage missing distinctions across multiple learning jobs,
not maximizing course or concept counts. Stop if the campaign yields only
domain specializations or weaker duplicates of existing entries.

## Closed findings

1. **Custody ambiguity — closed.** The plan now distinguishes the MUNDUS
   registry bridge from FONTES source ownership.
2. **Metadata inflation — closed.** The inventory distinguishes 54 individual
   work records from 284 homepage-only rows and prohibits title-only admission.
3. **Grouped-record double counting — closed.** Stanford and Berkeley grouped
   works are represented only by their row-grain surfaces; all 338 normalized
   canonical URLs are unique.
4. **Premature economic admission — closed.** The draft mapping is held and its
   Table body removed pending the common comparison.

No P1 or P2 finding remains for freezing the census. Approval does not extend
to depth-course selection, candidate extraction, or Factor Table admission.
