# Biography and Thought Closure 01 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Campaign: `BTC-01`
Status: portfolio frozen before current-reference comparison

## Reader and product job

The intended reader is trying to evaluate a compact historical or intellectual
claim such as “this leader caused that change,” “this thinker originated that
idea,” or “this work proved that people behave this way.” Today that reader
must reconstruct person, office, institution, action, evidence, outcome,
credit, and later interpretation from several entries.

The smallest useful product outcome is a reusable Table view or discovery
repair that reduces that reconstruction without making people, offices,
nations, schools, books, or events canonical Factorium concepts.

## Frozen portfolio

Freeze 24 packets before comparison with the V1 reference:

- 8 leadership and world-history cases;
- 6 intellectual-biography cases;
- 6 mind, language, and society cases; and
- 4 adversarial boundary cases.

The portfolio varies formal and informal leadership, individual and collective
action, intended and unintended outcomes, scientific and public argument,
collaboration and priority, favorable and contested legacy, and primary versus
later source custody. Named people are source-local case identities, not
candidate headwords.

## Packet contract

Every packet freezes its reader question, source surfaces, source role,
selection reason, and required distinctions before owner comparison. A source
supports the existence and framing of the case; it does not make every
biographical claim true or settle disputed attribution.

The required distinctions are query terms, not a preselected factorization.
The later screen must retain ambiguity, competing analyses, collective actors,
and incomplete evidence.

## Comparison and disposition

Each distinct required term receives exactly one disposition:

- covered directly;
- covered compositionally;
- wording or discovery repair;
- missing reusable concept;
- proper noun or instance;
- historically specific specialization;
- insufficient source evidence; or
- duplicate candidate.

Repeated words do not establish one meaning. In particular, `power`, `action`,
`credit`, `legacy`, `learning`, and `influence` require context-sensitive owner
review.

## Admission ceiling

The entire campaign may admit at most one anchor, two views, zero new relation
mechanics, and one discovery repair. Zero admission is valid. A candidate must
recur across at least three independently sourced packets in at least two
lanes, lack an adequate current owner or view, and make the reader job
materially easier.

## Stop and defer rules

- Stop named-person, named-work, officeholder, nation, event, movement, and
  school catalogs.
- Defer domain theories that do not recur outside their source discipline.
- Defer disputed historical or scientific conclusions to scoped evidence
  records and qualified source review.
- Do not turn author simulation into reader-value, historical-truth, or causal
  validation evidence.

## Outputs and claims boundary

Produce the frozen portfolio and checker, a complete term/owner disposition,
an exact before/after closure report, plan and result role reviews, and a final
continue/hold/stop decision.

This campaign can establish source-led structural pressure against the current
reference. It cannot establish historical truth, settle scholarly disputes,
measure reader comprehension, prove causal attribution, endorse a thinker or
leader, or establish biographical completeness.
