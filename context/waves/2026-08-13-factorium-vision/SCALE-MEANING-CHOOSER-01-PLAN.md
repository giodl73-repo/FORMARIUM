Score; Quantity Value, Numerical Value, Unit, Dimension, and Conversion; and
Geometric Reference Structure. The geometric route corrects future owner
interpretation without rewriting the original THS-01 expectation or result.

normalization. The chooser must disappear for `scales`, `scale factor`,
`temperature scale`, and other extended queries because ordinary literal
search already retains their context. It must not change the 185-record index
or any literal ranking.

Render clean `sim-48` only after a clean implementation commit. Require three
exact routes, no missing targets, byte-identical search index, live 390-pixel
presentation, and preserved `sim-47` custody. Claim no reader or competitive
evidence.
