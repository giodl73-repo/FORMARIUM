## Product hypothesis

Factorium's dictionary/thesaurus value depends on helping a reader distinguish
near-neighbors, overloaded ordinary words, and structures that look alike but
have different governing relations. If a new frozen portfolio concentrates on
those contrasts, repeated manual distinctions should expose overloaded owners,
missing senses, or missing compact views more directly than another broad
coverage pass.

The hypothesis weakens when the contrasts route cleanly through existing
senses and authored cross-references, or when an apparent gap is only a local
example, named mechanism, external catalog, or missing evidence.

QLD-02 will freeze 24 new questions before inspecting their results. It may
reuse the six reader jobs and four context classes to preserve portfolio
balance, but it must not reuse a QLD-01 question or reinterpret QLD-01's final
dispositions. Its primary pressure is contrast and boundary lookup rather than
general content coverage.

## Portfolio contract

Use the same 6-by-4 job/context matrix as QLD-01, with exactly one packet per
cell. Every packet must:

- ask for at least one explicit contrast or boundary;
- name a recognizable practitioner or learner;
- declare context, forbidden inference, candidate seeds, stress, and stop;
- keep named products, methods, regulations, and open taxonomies external;
- distinguish structural closure from substantive evidence;
- preserve incomplete, contradictory, and no-gap outcomes.

At least four packets each must exercise a no-gap control, a competing sense,
subtraction or contradiction, and a designed incomplete stop. Design flags
balance the frozen portfolio; they are not expected outcomes.

## Admission and execution

Use the QG-0 through QG-9 taxonomy and ordered owner test from the original
blueprint. A repair requires recurrence in two independently frozen QLD-02
packets or a bounded authoritative source campaign. The batch ceilings remain
one anchor, two views, one relation proposal, and two product-mechanic repairs;
zero is valid.

Execution order:

1. freeze the 24 packets, result contract, baseline identity, and plan;
2. commit the freeze before running lookup or authoring dispositions;
3. execute exact lexical lookups and bounded manual route analysis;
4. classify every gap and cluster by governing distinction;
5. research and implement only an admitted smallest batch;
6. rerun the exact portfolio against a separately identified candidate only
   when bytes change;
7. record role, repository, baseline-custody, and Product Owner results.

## Stop conditions

Stop with no batch when pressure does not recur, current owners can represent
the distinction without distortion, or the candidate would become a proper-
noun/member catalog. Do not mint another preview edition merely to advance a
simulation number. QLD-02 cannot produce reader-success, comprehension,
findability, usefulness, preference, adoption, or return-use evidence.
