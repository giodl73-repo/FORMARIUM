## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAUS-001 | major | Association or prediction could be relabeled cause. | Closed: both are contrasted with a defined causal target and assumptions. |
| CAUS-002 | major | One formal framework could be presented as the universal theory. | Closed: potential-outcome, structural, mechanistic, influence, and attribution lenses remain alternatives. |
| CAUS-003 | major | A causal diagram could be treated as evidence for its own arrows. | Closed: models are explicit assumption-bearing proposals with competitors. |
| CAUS-004 | major | A randomized label could replace executed design evidence. | Closed: assignment, versions, adherence, attrition, interference, measurement, and scope remain required. |
| CAUS-005 | major | A mediator could be credited as a mechanism. | Closed: pathway ordering, perturbation, mediator-outcome assumptions, and alternate paths remain required. |
| CAUS-006 | major | An intervention could be confused with its effect. | Closed: action and comparative outcome are separate senses and records. |
| CAUS-007 | major | Influence could swallow every causal relation. | Closed: it is a scoped contribution claim and does not imply identified quantitative effect or sole cause. |
| CAUS-008 | major | Average effects could determine one realized case automatically. | Closed: attribution adds case facts, contingencies, governing rule, and threshold. |
| CAUS-009 | major | Actual cause could automatically assign blame or liability. | Closed: causal attribution and normative responsibility remain distinct. |
| CAUS-010 | major | Nulls, contradictions, and competing explanations could disappear. | Closed: the Evidence contract requires all three plus sensitivity and negative evidence. |
| CAUS-011 | major | Results could transport beyond supported settings. | Closed: population, setting, version, time, heterogeneity, and exclusions remain explicit. |
| CAUS-012 | major | Candidate synthesis could become clinical, legal, or safety guidance. | Closed: high-stakes methods and attribution require qualified domain authority. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate publication.
