# Pointer Entry Expansion 03 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: active internal simulation plan

## Objective

Add a fourth bounded Pointer Entry registry in `sim-55`. Preserve predecessor
editions at 20, 40, and 60 labels; expand the successor to 80.

## Selection

Admit 20 exact reusable labels present in 8-11 owning Tables:

`applicability`, `comparison`, `component`, `dependency`, `failure`, `horizon`,
`interaction`, `kind`, `limitations`, `material`, `owner`, `path`, `phase`,
`question`, `residual`, `role`, `sample`, `sensitivity`, `transformation`, and
`validity`.

The batch excludes `point` despite similar reuse because its exact occurrences
are too generic to provide a stable orientation cue. Existing exclusions for
path-colliding `reference` and morphological duplicates remain in force.

## Product value

The batch strengthens navigation across composition, dependency, evaluation,
materials, sampling, ownership, and validity questions. Exact labels remain
independent; no relationship is inferred merely because they co-occur.

## Scope and stop

Require exactly 80 registry rows, a nonzero occurrence for every row, source
custody, unchanged canonical search, and independently reconstructable
predecessor editions. Stop before phrase extraction, morphological
normalization, automatic admission, canonical promotion, or reader claims.
