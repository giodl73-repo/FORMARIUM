## Reader and product value

The reader is a practitioner encountering an unfamiliar score, rating,
comparison, or pass/fail judgment. Their job is to determine what was
evaluated, against what, on which scale, by which criteria and combination
rule, and whether the result changes under credible variation. Today that
requires reconstructing definitions across Measurement Quality, Control,
Choice, Statistical Summary, and decomposition material.

The smallest valuable product change is one compact evaluation grammar plus a
book route that exposes the source measure, scale, comparator, criteria,
aggregation, thresholds, uncertainty, sensitivity, provenance, and
disposition. Internal review can establish source support, structural
coverage, exact custody, and rehearsal mechanics. It cannot establish reader
comprehension, usefulness, market value, or decision outcomes.

## Plan findings

The review found zero P1 blockers, thirty-one P2 conditions, and eight P3
notes. The P2 closure contract is:

- at most one new anchor rather than ten phase-labelled owners;
- explicit distinctions among measure, metric, indicator, score, scale,
  rating, rank, grade, classification, criterion, threshold, and target;
- exact subject, population, denominator/statistic, comparator, unit/scale,
  direction, rule, missingness, uncertainty, and provenance records;
- transformation and aggregation retain source values, component dimensions,
  interactions, noncompensation, vetoes, loss, and inverse limits;
- computed results do not become observations, and pass/fail does not become
  validity, safety, quality, success, or authority;
- named metrics, scales, methods, and rating systems remain examples or
  bounded external authorities;
- a question-led book route and neutral rehearsal make the ten slices one
  recognizable product improvement;
- the Product Owner closes F70 with an explicit cost and
  continue/merge/defer/stop decision.

No P1 or P2 plan finding remains without a declared closure test.

## Product Owner plan decision

Decision: **proceed with F61-F70 under a merge/defer budget**.

The batch earns implementation because it targets a frequent, recognizable
reader problem rather than phase completion: interpreting a score or judgment
without mistaking its display value for its meaning. The portfolio budget is
one anchor, no more than eight distinct views, one book chapter, and a small
task route. Scale transformation and ranking should share one mapping;
applicability belongs with evidence; evaluation review and disposition belong
in one procedure. Stop before a universal scoring algebra, automated metric
selection, named-metric catalogs, a new relation vocabulary, or any reader
value claim.

Result findings, exact identities, evidence exclusions, and the next
portfolio decision will be added here only after the F70 fixed point passes.
