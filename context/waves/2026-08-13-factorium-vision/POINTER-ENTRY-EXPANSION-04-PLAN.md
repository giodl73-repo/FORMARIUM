# Pointer Entry Expansion 04 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: active internal simulation plan

## Objective

Add a fifth bounded successor registry in `sim-56`. Preserve the 20-, 40-,
60-, and 80-entry editions and expand the concordance to 100 labels.

## Selection

Admit 20 exact labels present in 6-9 owning Tables:

`alternative`, `case`, `change`, `data`, `definition`, `demand`, `duration`,
`event`, `inference`, `mass`, `mechanism`, `normalization`, `rate`, `relation`,
`resolution`, `risk`, `sense`, `sign`, `volume`, and `work`.

The batch defers `type` and continues to defer `point` because their exact
occurrences are too generic for stable orientation. Morphological duplicates
and the path-colliding `reference` token remain excluded.

## Product value

The batch adds cross-Table navigation for change, evidence interpretation,
relations, physical quantities, risk, and work while retaining exact owner
contexts and independent labels.

## Scope and stop

Require exactly 100 rows, nonzero exact use, source custody, unchanged search,
and reconstructable predecessors. Stop before automatic extraction,
morphological normalization, synonym expansion, canonical promotion, or
reader-value claims.
