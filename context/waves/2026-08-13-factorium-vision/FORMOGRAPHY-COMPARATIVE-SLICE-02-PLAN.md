# Formography Comparative Slice 02 Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: executed quantitative research slice

## Objective

Test whether the ten-feature Formography core transfers from governance to a
quantitative scientific case without silently changing its object meanings.

## Frozen case

`Amount, Concentration, and Composition` supplies:

- eight canonical quantity senses;
- fourteen source factors;
- an admitted Formula view;
- SI and IUPAC authority boundaries;
- exact entry and view assurance digests;
- explicit failures for basis, unit, closure, state, and uncertainty.

Canonical Factorium records and scientific source authorities remain owners.

## Domain extension

The unchanged core receives one explicit `quantity-contract-v0` extension:

1. quantity identity;
2. numerator basis;
3. denominator basis;
4. units;
5. conditions;
6. uncertainty;
7. closure.

The extension applies to quantitative factors and relations. It does not
redefine form, factor, relation, frame, projection, or custody.

## Structured failure

A quantitative formograph that names a concentration numerator but omits its
denominator basis must fail with:

`FG-QUANTITY-BASIS-MISSING: quantity_contract.denominator_basis`

## Boundary

This is the second authored case. It does not establish independent
repeatability, measurement validity, scientific authority, reader value, or a
field.
