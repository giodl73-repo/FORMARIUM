## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| MQ-001 | major | Quality terms could collapse into one scalar axis. | Closed: eight senses use different governing comparisons. |
| MQ-002 | major | Error and uncertainty could be treated as synonyms. | Closed: signed reference difference and attributed dispersion remain separate. |
| MQ-003 | major | Accuracy, trueness, and precision could collapse. | Closed: value, limiting mean, and replicates are contrasted. |
| MQ-004 | major | Precision could be interpreted without replicate conditions. | Closed: repeatability or reproducibility conditions are mandatory. |
| MQ-005 | major | Display digits or resolution could imply accuracy. | Closed: resolution, sensitivity, precision, uncertainty, and accuracy remain separate. |
| MQ-006 | major | Calibration could mean adjustment or verification. | Closed: the exclusions and subsequent fitness test are explicit. |
| MQ-007 | major | Metrological and probability calibration could share one sense. | Closed: two senses, factor lenses, and diagnostics are separate. |
| MQ-008 | major | Probability calibration could imply discrimination. | Closed: frequency reliability and ranking are contrasted. |
| MQ-009 | major | Aggregate reliability could hide unsupported groups or regimes. | Closed: target, bins, support, horizons, groups, time, and intervals are required. |
| MQ-010 | major | Calibration could imply indefinite or out-of-range validity. | Closed: range, environment, time, validity interval, traceability, and revision are retained. |
| MQ-011 | major | Corrections could overwrite raw evidence. | Closed: raw indications, corrections, results, references, and uncertainty remain distinct. |
| MQ-012 | major | Candidate guidance could become certification. | Closed: explicit claim limits and diagnostic denial retain domain authority. |

No critical or major actionable finding remains open for candidate publication.
