# Synthetic First-Click to Handoff Plan

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: proposed freeze; execution not started

Campaign: `SUJ-04`

Evidence class: deterministic interface rehearsal plus authored synthetic
handoff hypotheses

## Objective

Start each of the 25 existing term-blind tasks at the `sim-43` homepage and
trace an exact route through its frozen task-shaped entrance to the first
intended destination, a task-fit density projection, related concepts, an
explicit stopping condition, and an outward handoff.

## Exact sequence

Every assignment records:

```text
entrance
-> first action
-> intended destination
-> density transition
-> related concepts
-> stop condition
-> outward handoff
```

Browser-observable fields and authored fields remain separate. The browser may
establish links, page targets, visible labels, query state, controls, content,
and overflow. It cannot establish recognition, comprehension, usefulness,
trust, satisfaction, memory, accessibility success, or preference.

## Frozen route policy

- `know-term` uses the first already-frozen ordinary query only and seeks the
  first result owned by the intended canonical family.
- `have-question` opens Compose and may load only an exact existing authored
  starter declared before execution. It may not infer controls from prose.
- `learn-or-explore` opens the Reader and seeks the first selected canonical
  destination through the fixed teaching route.
- The initial profile is Book. A task-fit profile transition is an authored
  projection from SUJ-02, not a preference.
- No query retry, new synonym, inferred seed, or post-result target change is
  allowed.

## Frozen hypotheses

1. At least 20 of 25 assignments can reach an intended canonical family from
   their frozen entrance without an invented semantic step.
2. At most 5 of 25 destination pages retain the original task after leaving
   the entrance surface.
3. At most 5 of 25 routes expose one explicit, bounded handoff package that
   combines task, selected route, unresolved work, and source links.

Hypotheses 2 and 3 deliberately test suspected product gaps. Their failure is
not reader evidence.

## Smallest-batch owner test

A repair may be admitted only if the same mechanical loss affects at least
eight assignments across at least two entrances, has one product owner, keeps
the Tables canonical, avoids sensitive persistence, and can be expressed as a
reversible static simulation feature. Otherwise record the gap and stop.

## Custody and boundaries

- Preserve `sim-42` and `sim-43`; any accepted repair mints a new simulation.
- SUJ-04 is dependent on SUJ-01 through SUJ-03 and is not replication.
- No external-reader gate closes.
- No demographic comparison or persona-conditioned routing is permitted.
