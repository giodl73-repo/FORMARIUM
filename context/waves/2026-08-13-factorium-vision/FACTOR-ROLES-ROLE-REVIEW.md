## Findings and closure

| ID | Severity | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| FRV-001 | critical | Root coordinates and structural roles could collapse into one supposedly universal type system. | Closed: the role table explicitly separates subject coordinate from view-relative job. |
| FRV-002 | critical | Programming mechanisms could define the general roles they merely implement. | Closed: assignments are many-to-many examples and explicitly not definitions or equivalences. |
| FRV-003 | major | State, context, boundary, relation, and constraint already had canonical root entries. New role files would create duplicate authorities. | Closed: those entries gained separate structural-role senses instead of duplicate headwords. |
| FRV-004 | major | Pivot could be read as mandatory hierarchy. | Closed: pivot is optional and may change between views. |
| FRV-005 | major | Policy and constraint could be silently conflated. | Closed: policy governs selection; constraint limits validity, with overlap declared explicitly. |
| FRV-006 | major | Derived labels could masquerade as observed factors. | Closed: derived views use `=` and retain source-factor dependencies. |
| FRV-007 | major | A factor might be forced into exactly one role. | Closed: role assignments are explicitly many-to-many and view-relative. |

No critical or major finding remains open for candidate publication.
