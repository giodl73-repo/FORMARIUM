# Formography Foundation Plan

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: initial research module complete; targeted narrowing active

## Objective

Adapt the source-custody and falsification mechanics of the RMM research
modules to test Formography as a proposed field before any Factorium or
Formarium naming change.

## Frame

The existing Factorium reference method already separates senses, compares
factorizations, records pivots and constraints, maintains typed relations, and
preserves provenance. The missing capability is a stable field-level account
that can be compared, criticized, taught, and independently implemented.

Working definition:

> Formography is the systematic, source-custodied mapping of context-bounded
> forms through alternative factorization, typed relations, and loss-declared
> projections without treating any one map as a universal ontology.

## Outputs

- source-backed foundation research module;
- claim taxonomy;
- neighboring-field comparison matrix;
- falsification and evidence tests;
- ranked research queue;
- five-paper research program;
- manifest with explicit novelty and rename gates.

## Boundaries

- `proposed field`, not established field;
- no product rename;
- no novelty claim from terminology;
- no claim that Factorium proves the method;
- no universal ontology;
- no promotion before comparative encoding and independent coding;
- the 2021 STS use of `formography` must be cited and distinguished.

## Stop condition

Stop or pivot if an established field owns the complete method, the term has a
dominant incompatible meaning, or the method cannot be distinguished in
representative artifacts from existing concept maps, knowledge graphs,
ontologies, or reference systems.

## Initial decision

`PROCEED_WITH_TARGETED_NARROWING`.

The exact word is occupied by a cognate 2021 STS workshop proposal, but the
reviewed source does not own the complete Factorium candidate method. Strong
prior art exists for every major primitive. No novelty or rename claim is
authorized.

## Next evidence slice

Freeze one representative case and encode it as:

1. a concept map;
2. an ISO Topic Map or generic property graph;
3. a candidate formograph.

Predeclare the required evidence, context, alternative-factorization,
relation-custody, and projection-loss fields. Pivot if the candidate formograph
