## Reader and product value

The reader is a practitioner asking whether a proposed option can work under
declared conditions. Their job is to distinguish assumptions from evidence,
compare scenarios, test eligibility/admissibility/feasibility/sufficiency,
reconcile capacity with demand, and expose blockers, shortages, and
substitution limits. Today that requires reconstructing a route across Choice,
Risk, Evidence, Coordinated Work, System Composition, and evaluation material.

The smallest valuable change is two independent owners plus seven views that
compose into one scenario-to-feasibility route. Internal work can establish
source support, distinction coverage, exact custody, and rehearsal mechanics;
it cannot establish real-world feasibility or reader value.

- explicit contrasts among resource, capacity, demand, load, utilization,

  compatibility catalogs remain examples or external authorities;

  Product Owner portfolio decision.

## Product Owner plan decision

Decision: **proceed under a two-owner, seven-view ceiling**.

The two anchors pass independent owner tests: scenario framing is not a
resource ledger, and cross-domain operational resources are not software
resources or merely plan annotations. The route earns work only if it answers
the reader's feasibility question without claiming a real-world outcome.
Merge or defer overlapping comparison/evidence and dependency/compatibility
work. Stop before automated feasibility judgment, scenario/resource catalogs,
new typed relations, or reader-value claims.
