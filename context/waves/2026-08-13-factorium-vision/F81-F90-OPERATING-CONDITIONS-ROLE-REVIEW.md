Status: plan reviewed; implementation pending

## Scope and value

The reader is a practitioner whose option looked feasible in a static model
but behaves differently in operation. Their job is to reconstruct who
exchanged what, under which commitment and queue state, where it occurred,
which hazard/exposure pathway mattered, what response was active, and which
history/version governs the record.

The thirteen-role review is
`signals/roles/check/f81-f90-operating-conditions-roles-check-2026-08-16.md`.
It found zero P1 blockers, thirty P2 closure conditions, and seven P3
notes. It admits three independent anchors and six views:

| Phase | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| F81 | request, response, acknowledgment, notification, and correlation anchor |
| F82 | agreement, commitment, obligation, guarantee, and contract constraint view |
| F83 | queue, backlog, priority, scheduling, and service-order procedure |
| F84 | location, region, containment, proximity, route, and network anchor |
| F85 | hazard, exposure, harm, vulnerability, and safety anchor |
| F86 | prevention, protection, detection, mitigation, response, and recovery procedure |
| F87 | actor, action, object, instrument, recipient, and outcome mapping |
| F88 | history, revision, provenance, lineage, and change evidence view |
| F89 | integrated operating-condition failure diagnostic and rehearsal |
| F90 | fixed-point review |

The closure contract preserves message/action/completion boundaries, agreement
authority and state, queue stock/flow, spatial reference and topology,
hazard/exposure/harm pathways, treatment timing and evidence, semantic roles,
lineage loss, and unresolved residuals. Named protocols, agreement schemes,
places, hazards, controls, and safety practices remain examples or external
authorities.

## Product Owner plan decision

Decision: **proceed under a three-anchor, six-view ceiling**.

The batch earns work only as one operating-condition route. Existing owners
must absorb agreement, queue, response, semantic-role, and lineage work.
Stop before operational automation, professional safety guidance, member
catalogs, new typed relations, or reader/safety claims.
