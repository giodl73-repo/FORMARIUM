## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 8

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 0 | P3 notes: 24

Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: relation readiness must remain a transparent predecessor check,
not a recommendation, evaluation, or promise of budget admission.

Cross-role consensus: the palette improves the reference experience when it
uses exact labels, preserves all identifiers and controls, and makes
progressive disclosure reversible without changing query state.

## Amendments

1. Group the original 12 controls into six exact owner groups, selected group
   first, with all identifiers retained.
2. Compute direction-sensitive fixed-point readiness from explicit inputs,
   name missing predecessors, disable nothing, and disclose budget loss.
3. Verify disclosure, control preservation, direction changes, closure result,
   responsive behavior, non-persistence, and exact `sim-18` regression.

All amendments are applied. No critical or major finding remains open.
