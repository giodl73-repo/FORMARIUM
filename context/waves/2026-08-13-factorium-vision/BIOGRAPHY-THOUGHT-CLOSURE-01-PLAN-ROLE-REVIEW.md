# Biography and Thought Closure 01 Plan Role Review

Date: 2026-08-18
Decision: approve freezing the 24-packet portfolio; no content admission yet

## Product value frame

| Field | Required statement |
|---|---|
| Reader | A general reader, student, analyst, or writer evaluating a claim about what a person believed, did, enabled, originated, or caused. |
| Job | Separate person, role, authority, action, institution, evidence, outcome, contribution, attribution, and later interpretation. |
| Current friction | The reader must reconstruct a cross-entry frame and is easily pulled toward a proper-name article or a single heroic narrative. |
| Product change | At most one anchor, two reusable views, or one discovery repair, only if repeated packets show that current composition is materially insufficient. |
| Evidence now | Frozen selection, source custody, owner coverage, recurrence, and internal structural rehearsal. |
| Evidence later | Reader findability and usefulness, qualified historical review, and any substantive causal conclusion. |
| Cost and displacement | New navigation and maintenance must beat composition from existing entries; named-case catalogs are stopped. |
| Continue/merge/stop | Continue through one frozen screen; merge adequate concepts into current owners; stop proper-name and single-domain branches. |

## Selected roles and findings

- **Compositional Semantics Steward:** approve because required distinctions are
  query terms rather than a claimed universal biography schema. Retain
  ambiguity and collective actors.
- **Experimental Methodologist:** approve the precomparison freeze, four
  adversarial packets, explicit no-gap outcomes, and zero-admission policy.
- **Factorization Method Steward:** approve only if individual, institutional,
  structural, and collective pivots remain alternatives rather than one chain.
- **Evidence & Claims Editor:** approve with explicit separation of source
  framing, historical claim, causal attribution, reputation, and reader value.
- **Reference Lexicographer:** named people and works remain case labels; only
  reusable distinctions may become headwords or views.
- **Reference Architecture Editor:** prefer a view under an existing owner over
  a new anchor when the job is cross-entry assembly.
- **Concept & Taxonomy Boundary Editor:** approve the hard stop on people,
  leaders, presidents, books, movements, events, and theory catalogs.
- **Research Integrity & Provenance:** source URLs and roles are frozen, nulls
  remain visible, and disputed packets cannot be silently harmonized.
- **Domain Source Reviewer:** institutional and scholarly sources frame packet
  questions but do not settle contested history or scientific generalization.
- **Reference Practitioner:** the campaign is useful only if it shortens the
  recurring “what did this person actually cause or contribute?” lookup.
- **Product Owner:** approve one bounded screen; stop if the result is only
  proper names, domain theories, or concepts already composed adequately.

## Fixed point

Three P2 conditions were closed in the plan: named-person admission was
prohibited; individual agency was made only one competing pivot; and source
framing was separated from substantive truth. No P1 or P2 finding remains for
freezing the portfolio. Approval does not extend to a Table admission or a
reader-value claim.
