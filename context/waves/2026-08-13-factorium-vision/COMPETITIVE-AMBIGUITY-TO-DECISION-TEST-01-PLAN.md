Date: 2026-08-17

## Product value contract

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Reader | A future reader beginning with an overloaded ordinary concept word |
| Job | Move from ambiguous wording to inspectable governing distinctions and a bounded next route |
| Current friction | Search, thesaurus, lexical graph, encyclopedia, and Factorium outputs have not been compared on the same portfolio |
| Product change | None during execution |
| Evidence now | Visible output structure, route availability, interaction count, source custody, and comparative internal mechanics |
| Evidence later | Recognition, preference, comprehension, usefulness, trust, adoption, and return use with real readers |
| Cost and displacement | Sixty frozen query-product captures plus Factorium; no content or UI work unless a repeated gap later earns a separate batch |
| Continue/merge/stop | Execute once, disposition every failure, and do not tune in place |

The exact twelve queries and five comparator classes are frozen in
`fixtures/competitive-reference/campaign-01.json`. Wolfram|Alpha is excluded
from scored denominators because the portfolio is not a declared quantitative
subset. A generic web-search capture substitutes for Google because a stable,
non-personalized Google first screen is not reproducibly available in the
execution environment; this substitution is frozen before capture.

Each product receives its first visible output and at most two result opens.
Score nine separate binary observations and a plain interaction count; do not
collapse them into a weighted quality score. “Decision structure” means at
least one visible contrast/exclusion, governing factor, or stopping boundary.

Two internal passes will operate on immutable captures: a direct observation
pass and a shuffled replay audit. They are procedural cross-checks by the same
internal evaluator, not independent readers. Any disagreement remains visible
and is resolved against the capture without changing the query, budget,
product, or candidate.

Apply every threshold conjunctively. A failed threshold falsifies the internal
differentiation hypothesis. Classify all Factorium failures using exactly one
frozen disposition. Make no reader, market, uniqueness, or superiority claim.
