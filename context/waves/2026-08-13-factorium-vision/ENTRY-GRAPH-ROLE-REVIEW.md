## Finding ledger

| ID | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| FEG-001 | critical | “Eight-level factorization” could conflate the width of one decomposition with recursive abstraction depth. | Closed: access control is explicitly one composite level with eight factors; depth is followed separately through entry links. |
| FEG-002 | critical | A displayed hierarchy could imply one universal tree even though factors are shared across many composites. | Closed: Factorium is a typed graph; tree views are generated projections. |
| FEG-003 | major | Calling an entry prime could claim permanent indivisibility. | Closed: prime and composite are roles relative to a particular view. |
| FEG-004 | major | Words in a factorization could remain unexplained labels. | Closed: every published factor resolves to a canonical entry, external versioned reference, or unresolved-candidate marker. |
| FEG-005 | major | Web search or lexical similarity could create false semantic joins. | Closed: programs may propose joins, but publication requires an approved typed edge. |
| FEG-006 | major | Symbols could become an opaque second language. | Closed: textual relationship names are canonical; marks are display and search aliases. |
| FEG-007 | major | The access decision could be counted as an independent ninth input. | Closed: it is explicitly `DERIVED-FROM` the eight-factor request. |
| FEG-008 | major | Identity, authentication, authorization, and enforcement could collapse into one “security” factor. | Closed: the access-control composite separates subject reference, evidence, policy, request, and enforcement concerns. |
| FEG-009 | major | Repeating full definitions in every parent entry could cause drift. | Closed: composite tables link canonical factor entries; indices and books are projections of one authority. |
| FEG-010 | major | Fixed abstraction tier numbers could imply every domain decomposes to the same depth. | Closed: field, sense, composite, recursive factor, mechanism, and instance are navigational views rather than mandatory numbered ontology levels. |
| FEG-011 | major | Requiring every linked prime to meet ordinary brief-entry length would encourage filler prose. | Closed: a 50–150-word prime class supports graph resolution and can expand when evidence warrants. |

## Fixed point

The entry graph and progressive-disclosure model may guide Factorium's
factor-role vocabulary and pilot tables. No critical or major finding remains
open.
