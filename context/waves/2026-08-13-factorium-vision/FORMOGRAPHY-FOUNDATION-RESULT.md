# Formography Foundation Result

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: source-reviewed initial module; proposed field not established

## Result

Factorium now has a falsifiable foundation research module for Formography:

> Formography is the systematic, source-custodied mapping of context-bounded
> forms through alternative factorization, typed relations, and loss-declared
> projections without treating any one map as a universal ontology.

The prior-art verdict is `PROCEED_WITH_TARGETED_NARROWING`.

An exact academic cognate is confirmed. A 2021 TU Dresden workshop by Ingmar
Lippert, Ann-Kristin Kühnen, and Susann Wagenknecht proposed formography as
detailed ethnographic study of forms in and of practice. The Factorium
proposal therefore cannot claim an unoccupied term and must distinguish its
cross-domain mapping contract from that STS method.

Concept mapping, Ranganathan's faceted classification, ISO Topic Maps,
Caroline Levine's strategic formalism, knowledge graphs, ontologies,
lexicography, systems modeling, and cartography remain required precedents.
No one reviewed source has been shown to own the complete candidate contract,
but every major primitive has prior art.
