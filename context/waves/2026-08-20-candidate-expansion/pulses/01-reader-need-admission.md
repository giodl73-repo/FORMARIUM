# Pulse 01: Recurring Reader Need and Candidate Admission Selection

Status: complete; defer all

## Goal

Select at most one completed candidate family for canonical admission work using
recurring reader need rather than conceptual completeness alone.

## Inputs

- seventeen fixed-point candidate entries and admission briefs;
- query-led and synthetic reader-job evidence already held under
  `signals/discover/hypothesis/`;
- new external reader evidence when available;
- current canonical owner and graph boundaries.

## Method

1. Normalize observed lookup questions without converting proposed headwords
   into evidence.
2. Count repeated questions by governing contract, reader type, and context.
3. Test whether an existing canonical entry already answers each question.
4. Rank candidates by repeated unmet need, consequence, source readiness, and
   maintenance burden.
5. Advance no more than one candidate; explicitly defer the rest.

## Result

No candidate advanced. The repository contains no external reader observation,
and authored synthetic or query-led recurrence has zero admission weight.

## Exit gates

- evidence distinguishes observed reader language from synthetic prompts;
- the selected family has repeated unmet lookup need;
- its nearest canonical owner cannot answer that need without semantic loss;
- source, graph, catalog, assurance, and publication work is bounded;
- a role review records admit or defer with severity and closure conditions.
