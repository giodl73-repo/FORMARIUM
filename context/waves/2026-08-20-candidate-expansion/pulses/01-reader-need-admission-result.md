# Pulse 01 Result: Recurring Reader Need and Candidate Admission

Status: complete; defer all

## Decision

**DEFER ALL.** None of the seventeen candidate families advances to canonical
admission. The repository contains no external reader observation and therefore
no repeated unmet reader lookup need.

Authored synthetic jobs and query-led packets remain useful routing tests, but
they have zero admission weight under this pulse. Their own custody records
prohibit interpreting them as reader demand, behavior, usefulness, adoption, or
return use.

## Evidence classes

| Evidence class | Scope | Admission weight |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic user jobs | Authored tasks against frozen editions | Zero; capability evidence only |
| Query-led discovery | Authored packets and owner dispositions | Zero; internal recurrence only |
| External reader observation | Completed sessions with observed lookup language | Zero observations available |

The external-reader protocol states that no external reader observation exists
and that internal simulations must not report participant behavior. The
synthetic-user synthesis likewise says all selected routes resolved existing
content and recommends real observation before content expansion. Both
query-led campaigns classify themselves as internal authored work rather than
reader-outcome evidence.

same governing lookup need at least twice and preserve enough context to test
whether a canonical owner answers it. Use the prepared R4E worksheets and
observation custody rules; do not substitute another authored campaign.
