# Lexicon Candidate Expansion Plan

Status: implemented

## Frame

The 54 canonical Tables remain coherent but underrepresent agency, social
relations, future claims, living systems, literary language, and service
failure vocabulary.

The bounded slice is:

```text
thirteen proposed missing families
+ authoritative source and owner audit
+ one admission brief and full candidate draft per coherent governing question
+ independent fixed-point review
-> a noncanonical expansion queue with no duplicate ownership
```

Broad proposals may split when they contain incompatible governing questions.
Terms already owned must route to their owner; unresolved terms must remain
explicitly unowned rather than receive a convenient false owner.

## Gates

1. Every candidate has a falsifiable thesis, senses, factorization, boundaries,
   source matrix, failure signs, and stopping condition.
2. Sense identifiers do not collide with canonical or candidate owners.
3. Relative links and reciprocal candidate boundaries resolve.
4. Historical Factorium and Formarium provenance remains readable.
5. The canonical 54-entry corpus, contracts, search, and publication counts do
   not change.
6. Independent rereview leaves no unresolved critical, major, or actionable
   finding.

Canonical admission is out of scope. It still requires recurring reader need,
domain approval, graph and catalog validation, assurance bindings, and
publication review.
