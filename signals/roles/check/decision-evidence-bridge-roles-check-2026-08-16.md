## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 10

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 13 | P3 notes: 17

Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: F27 needs a separate choice owner, but neither the entry nor the
candidate joins may imply that a comparison method, score, or structural pass
selects the correct alternative.

Cross-role consensus: preserve alternative status, unknown outcome cells,
incomplete preferences, recommendation/final-selection separation, and exact
join loss in both book and machine-facing artifacts.

## Amendments

1. Make alternative status, partial outcome maps, incomplete preference, and
   noncompensatory constraints first-class in the entry and Decision view.
2. Define candidate type-level versus guide-instance cardinality, bind final
   endpoints to exact digests, and close join outcomes to
   `pass`/`fail`/`unresolved` with no omitted admitted check.
3. Author a non-winning synthetic guide with status quo, limited trial, and
   broad adoption; keep recommendation and authority-owned selection separate.
