## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 13

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 30 | P3 notes: 7

Verdict: APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS

Top finding: operating behavior becomes interpretable only when exchange,
commitment, queue state, spatial relation, hazard/exposure, treatment, actor
roles, and change history remain separately reconstructable.

## Amendments

1. Admit three anchors—interaction exchange, spatial operating context, and
   hazard/safety—and no more than six distinct views.
2. Reuse Governance, Work/Resource, Control, Organization, Identity, and
   provenance owners for agreements, queues, response, roles, and history.
3. Keep all safety material descriptive, source-bounded, candidate, and
   non-prescriptive; bind F90 to exact review and Product Owner decision.
