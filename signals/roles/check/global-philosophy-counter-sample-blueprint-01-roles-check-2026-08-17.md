## Synthesis

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 0 | P3 notes: 30

Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: lanes and packet floors are sampling safeguards, not civilizations,
coverage percentages, or evidence of product value.

Cross-role consensus: comparison must retain source language, translation loss,
internal disagreement, and community custody; recurrence alone cannot create a
Factorium owner.

## Amendments applied

1. The blueprint calls lanes expandable work partitions and permits multi-lane
   packets and splits.
2. The product decision states that packet floors prevent token representation
   but do not establish coverage or value.
3. GPC-08 requires specific community protocols; TCPS and AIATSIS are examples,
   not global authorization.
