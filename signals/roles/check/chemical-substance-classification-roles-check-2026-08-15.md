# Chemical Substance Classification Roles Check

## Artifact identification

Artifact type: canonical Factor Table anchor, Diagnostic Table view,
cross-reference repair, source review, concept-taxonomy role addition, and
proposed reference/book registration.

Domain signals: chemistry, classification, nomenclature, entity levels,
stoichiometry, mixtures, solutions, analytical reporting, search, and pedagogy.

## Role selection and findings

| Role | Finding | Severity | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compositional Semantics Steward | Entity, species, substance, and mixture could be treated as one compositional hierarchy. | major | Separated object/ensemble/material/constituent levels and their relations. |
