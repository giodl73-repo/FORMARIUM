# Formarium RMM Category-Coverage Panel

This review applies all 17 RMM writing roles and all 6 RMM editorial roles to
the current Formarium corpus. These are AI-applied role lenses derived from
public work and public intellectual positions. They are not statements,
endorsements, or participation by the named people.

## Question

Which recurring reader lookup questions are not yet owned by one of the 54
canonical Tables?

The panel was not asked to make Formarium an exhaustive encyclopedia. A
proposal qualified only when it supplied a clustered headword, a distinct
lookup contract, a nearest existing Table that did not already answer the
question, concrete senses or factors, and a plausible evidence path.

## Highest-confidence missing families

| Priority | Proposed canonical family | Missing lookup contract | Nearest existing coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Privacy, Confidentiality, Secrecy, Anonymity, and Disclosure** | What is protected when information is observed, linked, retained, or shared? | Access control governs allowed actions; it does not own exposure, identifiability, contextual expectation, or onward disclosure. |
| P1 | **Consent, Assent, Refusal, Withdrawal, and Revocation** | Did the affected person validly agree, and can that agreement change? | Permission and compliance do not establish informed, voluntary, scoped, or revocable agreement. |
| P1 | **Trust, Credibility, Reputation, Legitimacy, and Reliance** | Why may a person, source, institution, or system be relied upon? | Claim evidence and formal assurance can support trust but do not define the reliance relation or its repair after breach. |
| P1 | **Similarity, Difference, Analogy, Equivalence, and Match** | In what respect are two things the same, similar, analogous, or substitutable? | Comparative Quantity is numeric; Meaning and Reference is semantic. Neither owns the general comparison contract. |
| P1 | **Motive, Desire, Intention, Incentive, and Rationale** | Is behavior driven by an internal want, formed intent, external reward, or stated reason? | Choice and Control begin after goals or preferences exist; they do not own agentive motivation. |
| P1 | **Incentive, Alignment, Externality, Moral Hazard, and Adverse Selection** | Why do individually reasonable choices produce distorted collective outcomes? | Choice, Accountability, and Cost cover adjacent pieces but not payoff asymmetry, hidden information, or shifted consequences. |

## Strong second queue

| Priority | Proposed canonical family | Why it remains distinct |
|---|---|---|
| P2 | **Prediction, Forecast, Projection, Scenario, and Backtest** | Model and Simulation does not yet separate kinds of future claims or their retrospective tests. |
| P2 | **Threshold, Tipping Point, Cascade, Contagion, and Spillover** | State transitions and stock-flow models do not own threshold-triggered propagation across a network or population. |
| P2 | **Perception, Sensation, Appearance, Illusion, Hallucination, and Observation** | Signal, Evidence, and Measurement do not own first-person perceptual organization, misperception, or observer dependence. |
| P2 | **Adaptation, Fitness, Selection, Niche, and Exaptation** | Capability and environment do not describe selective persistence and fit across generations. |
| P2 | **Lineage, Kinship, Descent, Inheritance, and Succession** | Provenance and version history do not own parentage, branching descent, inherited traits or rights, and succession order. |
| P2 | **Distribution, Skew, Tail, Outlier, and Extremum** | Summary Statistics does not make distribution shape or extreme-value behavior a first-class lookup family. |

## Valuable but narrower additions

- **Tone, Mood, Atmosphere, Register, and Voice**
- **Metaphor, Symbol, Motif, Allegory, and Allusion**
- **Manifestation, Embodiment, Persona, Disguise, and Avatar**
- **Morphology, Form, Pattern, Metamorphosis, and Gestalt**
- **Ecology, Habitat, Niche, Web, and Interdependence**
- **Fault, Defect, Failure, Incident, and Outage**

These are real gaps, but the panel found either a narrower audience, greater
overlap with existing entries, or a less immediate need than the P1 and P2
families.

## Rejected false gaps

The following do not currently justify new canonical families:

- trade-off, priority, opportunity cost, and decision conflict;
- feedback loops and control;
- causation and counterfactual reasoning;
- ownership, provenance, and traceability;
- compatibility, substitution, and dependency;
- resilience without the broader nonlinear stress-response question;
- generic transformation, perspective, or viewpoint;
- broad subject labels such as history, biology, geology, astronomy, or myth.

Each is already materially owned, is better handled as a specialized view, or
lacks a recurring decomposition contract.

## Synthesis

The missing center is not another technical domain. It is the language people
use to decide whether they may **reveal, agree, rely, compare, intend, or
incentivize**, followed by the language they use to **predict, propagate,
perceive, adapt, and inherit**.

The panel recommends candidate-entry briefs before canonical expansion. The
first bounded slice should test five contrasting families:

1. Similarity, Difference, Analogy, Equivalence, and Match;
2. Privacy, Confidentiality, Secrecy, Anonymity, and Disclosure;
3. Trust, Credibility, Reputation, Legitimacy, and Reliance;
4. Perception, Sensation, Appearance, Illusion, Hallucination, and Observation;
5. Adaptation, Fitness, Selection, Niche, and Exaptation.

This set tests the core thesaurus contract, public-interest vocabulary, human
experience, and living systems without committing to the entire expansion
queue.

## Verdict

**FINDING, no blocker.** The current 54 Tables form a coherent technical
reference, but they do not yet provide balanced general-reference coverage.
Advance the five candidate briefs through source audit and role review before
promoting any of them to canonical Tables.
