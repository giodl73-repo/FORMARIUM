## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 12

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 29 | P3 notes: 7

Verdict: APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS

Top finding: factor role, decomposition mode, operator, dependence, and
completeness must remain distinct; otherwise the campaign merely gives an
arbitrary decomposition mathematical-looking names.

Cross-role consensus: one owner-first grammar, visible projection loss,
noncausal structural claims, explicit completeness, a recognizable reader
job, and ten-phase identity boundaries are mandatory.

## Amendments

1. Use one new modes/contracts anchor and nine task-specific views or
   existing-owner deepenings; do not create ten sibling headwords.
2. Require direction, domain, dependence, polarity, provenance, granularity,
   completeness, loss, and unresolved residual wherever applicable.
3. Bind the batch to a dedicated fixed-point review, exact assurance digests,
   neutral task coverage, and full canonical/book validation before F60 closes.
4. Require the Product Owner to issue a continue/merge/defer/stop decision at
   every ten-phase boundary based on reader value and portfolio cost.
