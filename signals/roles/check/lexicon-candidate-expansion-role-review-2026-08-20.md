# Lexicon Candidate Expansion Role Review

## Scope

This review covers the seventeen noncanonical candidate entries and matching
admission briefs added on 2026-08-20, plus the niche-boundary amendment to the
2026-08-19 adaptation pair, which the fixed-point pass resolved by removing the
evolutionary niche sense and renaming that pair to *Adaptation, Fitness,
Selection, and Exaptation*.

The requested thirteen families produced seventeen entries because four broad
proposals required separation:

- lineage split into descent/kinship and inheritance/succession;
- tone/register split into stance/attributed affect and language variation;
- persona/embodiment split into projected identity and abstract-to-concrete
  realization;
- morphology/form split into pattern and linguistic morphology, while broad
  form remained routed or explicitly unowned by domain.

These drafts remain outside canonical Tables, graph identity, catalogs, search,
publication counts, and reader-evidence claims.

## Review lenses

- agency, consent, motivation, and strategic incentives;
- prediction, propagation, distributions, descent, and transfer;
- literary, linguistic, figurative, identity, pattern, and realization terms;
- ecology, adaptation, dependability, incidents, and outages.

Each lens performed an independent source, boundary, naming, and
cross-reference review, followed by a rereview after repairs.

## Findings and closure

| Area | Severity | Affected artifacts | Finding | Closure condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consent | major | consent brief and draft | Consent was defined as permission; refusal used timing rather than object; GDPR withdrawal crossed local senses. | Separate the normative act from permission and distinguish refusal, participation withdrawal, and revocation by object. | Consent is distinct from permission; refusal and participation withdrawal are object-based; GDPR withdrawal maps explicitly to revocation. |

## Fixed-point verdict

**PASS.** No blocking or actionable findings remain after rereview. The
seventeen entries and their admission briefs are complete noncanonical
candidates.

Canonical admission remains separately gated by recurring reader need,
source/domain approval, graph and catalog validation, assurance bindings, and
publication review. Open residue such as urban morphology, musical or aesthetic
form, focalization, optimization extrema, cross-validation, and ecological
succession is recorded as unowned rather than silently assigned.
