# Measurement Quality Roles Check

## Phase 1 - Artifact identification

Artifact type: canonical Factor Table anchor, Diagnostic Table view, research
synthesis, probability-formula resolution, and proposed interchange/book
registration.

Domain signals: metrology, experimental design, measurement uncertainty,
instrument behavior, forecast verification, benchmarks, quality acceptance,
provenance, and practitioner diagnosis.

## Phase 2 - Role selection

| Role | Why selected |
|---|---|
| Factorization Method Steward | Tests whether related quality properties remain distinct without false independence. |
| Experimental Methodologist | Audits replicate conditions, references, shifts, exclusions, and falsifying tests. |
| Evidence & Claims Editor | Prevents quality labels from becoming unsupported certification claims. |
| Benchmark Numeracy Checker | Audits reference differences, dispersion, support, bins, intervals, and denominators. |
| Reference Lexicographer | Eight close senses and two calibration meanings require rapid separation. |
| Reference Architecture Editor | Connects measurement, uncertainty, sampling, control, and formula material. |
| Domain Source Reviewer | Formal metrology and forecast meanings require authoritative sources and limits. |
| Research Integrity & Provenance | References, raw indications, corrections, outcomes, and revisions require custody. |
| Equation & Units Auditor | Error, resolution, corrections, and uncertainty must retain quantity and unit contracts. |
| Reference Practitioner | The diagnostic must lead quickly from symptom to discriminating test and owner. |
