## Proposed value

Reader: a practitioner with a question that crosses several Factorium
concepts and cannot be answered by finding one entry.

Job: form a bounded typed closure; combine, exclude, interact, project, and
evaluate its concepts; see truncation and residuals; and receive a
reconstructable explanation with limitations.

Product change: no new semantic anchor by default. Audit the already-built
relation, composition, closure, projection, reconciliation, and evaluation
contracts; then expose one integrated route and one exact candidate Book One
spine.

Evidence now: canonical validation, adversarial fixtures, route/task/search
coverage, internal browser rehearsal, and deterministic identities. Evidence
later: invited-reader comprehension and usefulness. Internal simulation is
not reader evidence.
