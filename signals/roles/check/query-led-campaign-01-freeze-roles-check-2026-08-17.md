# Query-Led Campaign 01 Freeze Roles Check

## Artifact identification

Type: frozen internal query corpus, result contract, and execution plan.

Domain signals: measurement, physical science, software, systems, governance,
policy, safety, everyday reasoning, lookup, composition, and synthetic
evidence.

## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 6

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 12 | P3 notes: 6

Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: the result grammar and classification rules must be frozen with
the questions, before the first baseline route is inspected.

Cross-role consensus: candidate seeds and design flags organize the authored
challenge but confer no semantic identity, expected result, or content
entitlement.

## Amendments incorporated

1. Add frozen result contract `QLD-RC-01` with exact fields, codes, states,
   dispositions, measures, and claim prohibitions.
2. State that portfolio design flags are not expected baseline labels and
   preserve results that contradict them.
3. Keep high-stakes and named-member material generic, incomplete, externally
   bounded, and ineligible for unsupported canonical promotion.
