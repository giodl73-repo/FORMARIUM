# Formarium Five Candidate Briefs Role Check

## Scope

This review covers the pre-admission briefs for:

1. similarity and comparison;
2. privacy and disclosure;
3. trust and reliance;
4. perception and appearance;
5. adaptation, selection, and niche.

The briefs are source and boundary records. They do not create canonical
entries, graph identities, search records, or reader-evidence claims.

## Roles

- Product Owner
- Reference Lexicographer
- Concept and Taxonomy Boundary Editor
- Domain Source Reviewer
- Evidence and Claims Editor

## Findings and closure

| Brief | Finding | Closure |
|---|---|---|
| Similarity | `residual-difference` was a comparison remainder, not a distinct recurring sense, and overlapped model-fit language. | Removed it as a sense and source claim; retained residuals only as factors of difference or matching. |
| Perception | `perceptual-report` re-owned observation and provenance despite the brief's boundary. | Removed it as a sense and explicitly routed recorded reports to Claim and Evidence. |
| Adaptation | The fitness sense selected expected reproductive contribution too strongly while acknowledging competing definitions. | Required an explicit fitness definition and preserved realized-success, propensity, and model-specific accounts. |
| Privacy | No actionable finding. | Person-centered scope, jurisdictional limits, and access-control boundary pass. |
| Trust | No actionable finding. | Trust, trustworthiness, credibility, reputation, legitimacy, and reliance remain distinct. |
