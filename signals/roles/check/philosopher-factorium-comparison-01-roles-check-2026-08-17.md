Verdict: APPROVED

Top finding: recurrence alone does not justify a Table; the normative and
experience families must be split and all three leading families require
boundary design before authoring.

Cross-role consensus: taxonomy, architecture, mapping, evidence, and product
roles agree that the comparison should guide a small design queue, not create
philosopher headwords or claim philosophy coverage.
