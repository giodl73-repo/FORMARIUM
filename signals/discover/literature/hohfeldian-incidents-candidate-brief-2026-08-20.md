# Hohfeldian Incidents — Candidate Brief

## Decision question

Should Lexicon expose a source-relative Hohfeldian mapping without presenting it
as legal advice or universal legal doctrine?

## Bounded thesis

Yes, provisionally. The map distinguishes correlated normative positions that
current governance and access entries operationalize but do not analytically
separate.

## Proposed senses

| Sense ID | Governing question |
| --- | --- |
| `hohfeld-claim` | Which counterparty owes which correlative duty regarding the stated act or state? |
| `hohfeld-liberty` | Which act is the subject not under a correlative duty to forgo? |
| `hohfeld-power` | Which normative position can the subject alter through a recognized act? |
| `hohfeld-immunity` | Which attempted normative alteration by another lacks effect on the subject's position? |
| `hohfeld-duty` | Which conduct is owed to a counterparty under the stated mapping? |
| `hohfeld-no-claim` | Which counterparty lacks a claim that the subject act or refrain? |
| `hohfeld-liability` | Which position can another recognized act alter with respect to the subject? |
| `hohfeld-disability` | Which normative alteration can the subject not effect because the subject lacks the relevant recognized power? |

## Candidate contract

Every assertion requires parties, source, forum, time, act, claimed
correlative/opposite, and evidence. “Power” is a normative-alteration relation,
not a capacity sense.

## Existing-owner audit

`governance-obligation-compliance` owns obligations and compliance;
`access-permission-authorization-entitlement` owns governed access permission;
policy and decision records own applicability. None makes Hohfeld a
jurisdiction-neutral doctrine.

## Admission gates

Require source-relative examples, legal-domain review, and proof that analytic
labels do not duplicate access or obligation senses.

## Counterevidence and limits

Terminology and doctrine vary across jurisdictions and commentators disagree
about applications. The candidate makes no determination of enforceability,
remedy, or legal status.

## Disposition

Keep as a carefully guarded analytical candidate with collision-safe IDs.
