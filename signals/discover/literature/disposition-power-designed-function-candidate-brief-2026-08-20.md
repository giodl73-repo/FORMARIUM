# Disposition, Power, and Designed Function — Candidate Brief

## Decision question

Should Lexicon admit a limited vocabulary for bearer-grounded realizable
features and design-relative function without absorbing capacity, competency,
formal function, or legal capacity?

## Bounded thesis

Yes, provisionally. The family answers a question left open by operational and
organizational owners: what standing feature can manifest under conditions, and
when is an effect design-relative rather than merely useful?

## Proposed senses

| Sense ID | Governing question |
| --- | --- |
| `realizable-disposition` | Which bearer has what stable, realizable propensity under what manifestation conditions? |
| `designed-function` | Which activity or effect is attributed to an artifact or component under which design account? |

## Candidate contract

Record bearer, grounding basis, trigger, background, manifestation, evidence,
and time. For function require a stated design account and contribution; neither
utility nor successful performance alone is sufficient.

## Existing-owner audit

`operational-resource-capacity-demand` owns operational capacity;
`organization-role-authority` owns competency; formal/software functions remain
with mathematical and software records. Hohfeldian power and legal capacity are
excluded. Causal reasoning owns mechanisms and effects.

## Source matrix

| Source | Contribution | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [UFO overview](https://nemo.inf.ufes.br/en/ufo/) | Supplies bearer, trigger, and manifestation analysis. | Its intrinsic-moment commitments are not adopted as universal metaphysics. |

## Admission gates

The senses are disjoint by standing realizable feature versus design-relative
function attribution. Trigger, background, and manifestation remain factors of
the disposition. The pair needs examples across domains, fixed-point role
review, and no collision with canonical identifiers before admission.

## Counterevidence and limits

Some theories deny that dispositions need categorical grounds; design accounts
of function also compete with selected-effect accounts. This candidate preserves
that disagreement and makes no legal, clinical, or causal inference.

## Disposition

Keep as a noncanonical candidate. Merge engineering capability, operational
capacity, mathematical/software function, and competency into their present
owners.
