# GPC-08 Indigenous, Central/Southeast Asian, and Oceanic Literature Review

## Phase 1 — claims

1. Eighteen named packets can test Factorium's custody model, but cannot
   represent Indigenous, Central/Southeast Asian, or Oceanic philosophy.
2. Relation, reciprocity, land, authority, and good life recur, but their local
   owners, histories, languages, and normative force are not interchangeable.
3. Story, genealogy, council, chant, epic performance, proverb, ceremony,
   research method, political text, and academic article have different
   warrants; public description never licenses ceremonial extraction.
4. Colonial rule, missionization, state codification, militarization,
   migration, archive, translation, and research institutions shape what can
   be known and who may share it.
5. Customary law, sovereignty, land, gender, chiefly authority, spirituality,
   nation, decolonization, and community practice cannot become guidance.

INERTIA SCENARIO: Factorium notices repeated words for relation, reciprocity,
balance, land, respect, service, and consensus; merges them into universal
facets; treats publication as permission; and presents national or regional
summaries as if they owned living community knowledge.
