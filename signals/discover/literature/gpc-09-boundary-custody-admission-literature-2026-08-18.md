# GPC-09 Boundary and Custody Admission Literature Review

## Phase 1 — claims

1. Meaning, reference, interpretation, use, speaker intent, and semantic
   information are related but non-identical roles.
2. Epistemic attitude, evidence, justification or warrant, testimony,
   defeaters, authority, and confidence must remain independently visible.
3. Citation, access, ownership, consent, stewardship, and authority to share
   are not interchangeable.
4. Interpretation, translation, performance, and research custody require
   direction, mediation, alternatives, loss, restriction, and correction.
5. One semantic anchor and two evidence views are a bounded Factorium design;
   the literature does not establish universal categories or reader value.

INERTIA SCENARIO: Factorium keeps the existing owners unchanged, forcing a
reader to reconstruct these boundaries, or expands provenance until online
access, citation, and institutional custody are mistaken for permission.
