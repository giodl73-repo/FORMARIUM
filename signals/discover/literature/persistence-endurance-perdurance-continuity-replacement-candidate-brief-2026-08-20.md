# Persistence, Endurance, Perdurance, Continuity, and Replacement — Candidate Brief

## Decision question

Should Lexicon describe competing accounts of sameness through change without
duplicating canonical entity identity?

## Bounded thesis

Yes, provisionally. The candidate describes explanatory stances and defeasible
continuity evidence, whereas existing identity records assign and trace an
identity under operational rules.

## Proposed senses

| Sense ID | Governing question |
| --- | --- |
| `persistence-account` | Under which account is one continuing subject said to remain through a temporal interval? |
| `endurance-account` | Does the account treat the whole subject as present at each relevant time? |
| `perdurance-account` | Does the account treat the subject as temporally extended through temporal parts or stages? |
| `continuity-evidence` | Which tracked continuities support, without proving, a persistence claim? |
| `replacement-account` | Which part or role is replaced, under what continuity and boundary conditions? |

## Candidate contract

Record subject, interval, declared account, change, replacement scope,
continuity observations, and counterevidence. Never infer identity solely from
any one continuity dimension.

## Existing-owner audit

The `entity-identity` sense of `identity-naming-classification-versioning` owns
canonical identity; change-lineage evidence owns revision and provenance. This
candidate owns neither identifiers nor a decision procedure for sameness.

## Admission gates

Require examples where the account changes the explanation without changing
canonical identity records, plus fixed-point and collision review.

## Counterevidence and limits

There is no consensus on persistence. Continuity measures differ by domain and
replacement can be conventional; this is not a universal social or metaphysical
ontology.

## Disposition

Keep as noncanonical, theory-plural candidate; preserve rather than resolve
endurance/perdurance competition.
