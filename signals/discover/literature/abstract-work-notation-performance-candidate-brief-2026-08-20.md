# Abstract Work, Notational Specification, Performance Instance, Compliance Condition, and Autographic/Allographic Distinction — Candidate Brief

## Decision question

Should Lexicon admit a bounded work/notation/performance vocabulary while
rejecting aesthetic object, experience, interpretation, and a standalone medium
sense?

## Bounded thesis

Yes, provisionally. The five senses distinguish account-relative work,
notation, realization, compliance, and identity mode, without selecting a
universal ontology of art or cataloging.

## Proposed senses

| Sense ID | Governing question |
| --- | --- |
| `abstract-work-account` | Which abstract work is posited under which cataloging or ontological account? |
| `notational-specification` | Which notation or score fixes relevant features for compliant instances? |
| `performance-instance` | Which temporally bounded realization is presented as an instance or performance of the stated work/account? |
| `work-compliance-condition` | Which condition must an instance satisfy to count under the stated work/notation account? |
| `autographic-allographic-distinction` | Under which account does identity depend on history of production rather than compliance with notation? |

## Candidate contract

Record account, work, notation, occurrence, performance agent/time, compliance
conditions, production history, provenance, version, and medium as a factor.

## Existing-owner audit

`type-token-instantiation-realization-embodiment` owns the general
type/token/realization distinction; meaning/reference owns interpretation;
identity records own names and versions. This candidate owns neither aesthetic
value nor experience.

## Source matrix

| Source | Contribution | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SEP, Goodman](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/goodman/) | Explains autographic/allographic and notation-centered analysis. | It is a contested philosophical account, not a universal cataloging rule. |
| [IFLA LRM](https://repository.ifla.org/handle/20.500.14598/40) | Provides work/expression/manifestation/item cataloging comparison. | Its bibliographic model is not identical to Goodman's ontology. |
| [CIDOC CRM FRBRoo](https://cidoc-crm.org/frbroo) | Supplies cultural-heritage event and work modeling context. | It does not settle aesthetic or interpretive questions. |

## Admission gates

Require cross-media counterexamples, cataloging review, and proof that medium
does not qualify as a disjoint sense before any later expansion.

## Counterevidence and limits

The ontology of artworks, performances, and notation is disputed. This
candidate makes no claim about artistic value, aesthetic experience, or correct
interpretation.

## Disposition

Keep noncanonical with medium as a factor; reject aesthetic-object/experience
and interpretation ownership.
