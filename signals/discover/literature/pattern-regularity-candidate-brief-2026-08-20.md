# Pattern, Regularity, Motif, Template, and Pattern Formation

## Decision question

Does Lexicon need one owner for what counts as a repeated regularity across
instances, how it is recognized, described, named, reused, and explained, and
how it is separated from an artifact of sampling or of the observer?

## Scope decision

The originally proposed family was *Morphology, Form, Pattern, Metamorphosis,
and Gestalt*. Three of those words are removed and one is narrowed.

- `morphology` names at least six unrelated fields. Its shape-equivalence and
  set-theoretic image-operator senses belong to the canonical geometric entry;
  its linguistic sense is a coherent unowned contract and now has its own
  candidate; its urban sense remains unowned. It is not usable as a title word.
- `form` spans hylomorphic metaphysics, aesthetic formalism, musical form,
  grammatical word form, and a document to fill in. Its hylomorphic sense
  routes to the type and token candidate, its geometric sense to the canonical
  geometric entry, and its grammatical sense to the morpheme candidate; the
  aesthetic and musical senses remain unowned. It is not usable as a title word.
- `gestalt` is perceptual organization and is already a sense of the perception
  candidate. It is routed, not re-owned.
- `metamorphosis` is a transformation concept owned by the transformation root
  and the state and lifecycle entry. It is routed, not re-owned.

What survives is one bounded family about cross-instance recurrence, with
plurality criterial: a regularity requires a relation repeating across two or
more individuated instances. Single-figure invariance is a shape question. This
family is also the single owner of `motif`, which is cross-referenced from the
figuration candidate.

Only two closed false gaps are claimed here: `gestalt`, owned by perceptual
organization, and `metamorphosis`, owned by the transformation root together
with the state and lifecycle entry. No separate candidate should be opened for
either. Hylomorphic form and geometric morphology are routed to owners named
above rather than declared absent. Linguistic morphology, urban morphology, and
musical or aesthetic form were unowned rather than falsely closed: linguistic
morphology is admitted as the separate
[morpheme and word-formation
candidate](morphology-word-formation-candidate-brief-2026-08-20.md), and urban
morphology and musical or aesthetic form are recorded as open domain-specific
residue. No universal `form` sense is proposed to absorb that residue.

## Bounded thesis

Proceed only as a recurrence and reuse entry. Shape and transformation-group
equivalence, system composition and dependency, statistical estimation and
generalization, model and mechanism claims, perceptual organization, and state
transition retain their existing or candidate owners.

The thesis is falsified if a described recurrence can be recorded completely as
shape equivalence for one figure, or as composition of one whole, without
losing the instance set, the repetition relation, the recognition method, or
the artifact check; or if pattern, template, motif, and design pattern cannot
survive pairwise distinction.

## Proposed senses

| Sense | Governing question |
|---|---|
| `regularity` | Which relation repeats across two or more individuated instances, over which extent? |
| `pattern-description` | Which explicit rule, grammar, or specification generates or recognizes the instances? |
| `motif` | Which smallest recurring element persists across occurrences, and by which index or catalogue is it identified? |
| `template` | Which reusable prototype is instantiated with which variable slots and which fixed structure? |
| `design-pattern` | Which recurring problem in which context does this named solution resolve, and with what consequences? |
| `pattern-formation` | By which mechanism does a pattern arise from initially uniform or unstructured conditions? |

## Candidate contract

```text
pattern-regularity-use
  := subject, corpus, population, medium, or space
   x observation extent, resolution, sampling, and boundary
   x repeated element or unit and its individuation criterion
   x repetition relation of translation, reflection, rotation, scaling, sequence, or co-occurrence
   x describing rule, grammar, template, or specification
   x variable slots, invariants, and admissible variation
   x instance set, exceptions, and near-misses
   x recognition method, threshold, and observer or detector
   x generative mechanism or explanation, if claimed
   x naming, catalogue, index, or pattern-language membership
   x consequences, tradeoffs, and misapplication conditions
   x artifact risk from sampling, multiplicity, and confirmation
```

## Existing-owner audit

- [Geometric Object, Point, Curve, Path, Length, Shape, Angle, Coordinate
  System, Reference Frame, and Unit Circle](../../../tables/entries/geometric-reference-structure.md)
  owns `shape` as what is preserved under a declared transformation family and
  already names morphology inside that machinery. This is the largest
  collision: a pattern is a cross-instance recurrence relation, which shape
  equivalence for one figure cannot express.
- [System Composition, Architecture, Capability, Interface, and
  Dependency](../../../tables/entries/system-composition-dependency.md)
  owns the composition and dependency of one whole.
- [Decomposition Modes and Combination Contracts](../../../tables/entries/decomposition-modes-combination-contracts.md)
  warns that separate factors and familiar symbols do not establish a
  decomposition; the same caution grounds the artifact check here.
- [Population, Sample, Estimand, Estimate, and Generalization](../../../tables/entries/sampling-generalization.md)
  and [Mean, Median, Quantile, Variance, Standard Deviation, Standard Error,
  and Confidence Interval](../../../tables/entries/statistical-summary-interval.md)
  own the statistical validity of an inferred regularity; the
  pattern-versus-artifact guard routes there.
- [Model, Representation, and Simulation](../../../tables/entries/model-representation-simulation.md)
  owns model claims; a mechanism claim for pattern formation routes there.
- [State, Event, Transition, Process, and Lifecycle](../../../tables/entries/state-event-transition-process-lifecycle.md)
  and the [Transformation](../../../tables/roots/transformation.md) root own
  staged change, so metamorphosis routes there.
- [Perception, Sensation, Appearance, Illusion, and
  Hallucination](../candidates/perception-sensation-appearance-illusion-hallucination.md)
  owns `perceptual-organization`, so gestalt routes there.
- [Type, Token, Instantiation, Realization, and
  Embodiment](../candidates/type-token-instantiation-realization-embodiment.md)
  owns the general-to-particular relation. This family owns the reusable
  prototype and its declared slots; it does not own instantiation of a general
  by a particular, and the two candidates cross-reference reciprocally.
- [Morpheme, Inflection, Derivation, Compounding, and Word
  Formation](../candidates/morpheme-inflection-derivation-compounding-word-formation.md)
  owns linguistic morphology, which this family excludes by name.
- Design pattern catalogues belong in a mapping view under `tables/mappings/`,
  not as a canonical sense.

## Counterevidence and limits

- "Pattern" itself is polysemous: a repetition, a named design solution, a
  sewing or casting prototype, statistical pattern recognition, and a pattern
  language. The excluded senses must be named.
- A detected regularity can be a property of the sampling, of the detector, or
  of the observer's willingness to see structure; detection is not validation.
- A described pattern does not establish a mechanism, and a mechanism does not
  establish that any observed instance was produced by it.
- Motif indices are editorial and culturally situated; membership in an index
  is evidence of catalogue practice, not of a universal narrative unit.
- Design pattern catalogues are paradigm-bound and contested as reusable
  knowledge; naming a pattern does not validate applying it.
- Symmetry or invariance of a single figure is not recurrence; recording it as
  a pattern silently re-owns the canonical shape question, which is why
  cross-instance plurality is criterial.
- Shape equivalence, composition, perceptual organization, and staged change
  are adjacent and already owned; a recurrence claim must show what those
  owners cannot express.

## Admission gates

1. Exclude morphology, form, gestalt, and metamorphosis as senses and route
   them by name to the owners identified in the scope decision.
2. Exclude mathematical morphology, sewing and casting patterns, and Gestalt
   therapy by name, and record urban morphology and musical or aesthetic form
   as unowned residue rather than as closed gaps.
3. Require at least two individuated instances, an instance set, and a stated
   repetition relation for any pattern claim.
4. Keep description separate from mechanism, and mechanism claims routed to the
   model owner.
5. Keep motif, template, and design pattern pairwise distinct.
6. Require an artifact check that routes statistical validity to the sampling
   and estimation owners.
7. Keep design pattern catalogues in mapping views rather than as senses.
8. Show for every case that shape equivalence and system composition cannot
   answer the question.

## Disposition

**PROCEED TO CANDIDATE ENTRY DRAFT; DO NOT ADMIT CANONICALLY.** Cross-instance
recurrence is unclaimed and well sourced, but the boundary against canonical
shape is the hardest in this batch and must be settled by fixed-point role
review before canonical admission.
