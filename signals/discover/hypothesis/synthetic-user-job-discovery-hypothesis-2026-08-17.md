# Synthetic User Job Discovery Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

**Claim:** Factorium has a distinct post-definition job for adults who need to
distinguish, combine, bound, or evaluate several concepts; it should complement
ordinary dictionaries and thesauri more often than merely replace them.

**Falsification condition:** The technical/product mechanism is falsified for
this authored round if fewer than 8 of 25 frozen scenarios can produce a
traceable multi-owner or specialized-view route with an output materially
beyond definitions and synonym lists, or if more than 8 require invented
canonical concepts or unresolved destinations.

**Confidence:** 62/100.

**Hypothesis class:** Technical/product-mechanism hypothesis with a future
behavioral hypothesis explicitly out of scope.

## Prior

**Team belief before:** Factorium begins where a dictionary or thesaurus stops:
after naming a concept, it exposes governing distinctions, factors,
constraints, relations, failure signs, and bounded composition.

**Origin of the prior:** Product vision, internal composition rehearsals, and
author judgment. It is not customer or reader evidence.

| Evidence type | Threshold to shift belief |
|---|---|
| Frozen synthetic route portfolio | Fewer than 8/25 distinct post-definition routes weakens the product mechanism |
| Synthetic unresolved ownership | More than 8/25 routes requiring invented owners weakens reference readiness |
| Future R4E observations | Fewer than 3 of 5 readers independently identify a useful post-definition task strongly weakens the behavioral claim |
| Future Proof Set use | No observed self-selected application or return use blocks the adoption claim |

**Confirmation-bias check:** The team may dismiss simple definition lookups as
“not the real use case.” Five dictionary/encyclopedia controls are therefore
frozen and retained even if Factorium is excessive for them.

## Falsification tests

### Test 1 — Distinct route mechanism

- Method: deterministic search plus authored route audit over 25 frozen tasks.
- Measure: scenarios with at least two exact owners or one specialized view and
  a loss-declared output beyond definition/synonym retrieval.
- Falsification threshold: fewer than 8 of 25.
- Timeframe: this campaign.

### Test 2 — Ownership readiness

- Method: exact destination and canonical-owner validation against `sim-42`.
- Measure: scenarios requiring invented concepts, missing destinations, or an
  unresolved owner to express the proposed value.
- Falsification threshold: more than 8 of 25.
- Timeframe: this campaign.

### Test 3 — Real behavioral value

- Method: future R4E and Proof Set observation, not synthetic personas.
- Measure: unsolicited problem framing, independent task use, and return use.
- Falsification threshold: no repeated post-definition job across real readers
  or no voluntary return use.
- Timeframe: after real readers are available.

If Tests 1 and 2 do not falsify the mechanism, only the internal product
hypothesis survives. No statement about what people think, prefer, understand,
or will adopt is admitted until Test 3.

## Null hypothesis

Factorium provides no material reason to visit beyond a dictionary, thesaurus,
encyclopedia, handbook, web search, or workplace documentation; any apparent
advantage is created by author-selected tasks and routes.
