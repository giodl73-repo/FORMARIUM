# Factorium Consequential-Depth Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

**Claim:** After controlling for genuine thesaurus contrast, clean `sim-48`
exposes consequence-bearing decision support on overloaded concepts: it covers
the task's senses, names governing inputs, blocks an invalid transfer, states a
stopping condition, and gives a bounded next inspection.

**Falsification condition:** The claim is wrong if Factorium exposes at least
four of those five dimensions on fewer than five of six frozen packets, if it
strictly exceeds the Merriam-Webster Thesaurus by at least two dimensions on
fewer than four packets, if it needs more than one additional open on any
packet, or if it asserts any false equivalence.

**Confidence:** 65/100.

**Hypothesis class:** Technical. It predicts inspectable artifact output, not
reader preference, comprehension, usefulness, trust, or market demand.

## Prior

**Team belief before:** CAD-01 falsified the easy distinction: a good thesaurus
can supply real contrast. Factorium may still differ by carrying a distinction
through application boundaries and a next inspection.

**Origin:** The product thesis, authored Factor Table grammar, and the exact
CAD-01 parity cases for `force`, `power`, `value`, `model`, `system`, and
`evidence`.

| Evidence type | Threshold to shift belief |
|---|---|
| Consequence coverage | Factorium exposes at least 4/5 dimensions on at least 5/6 packets |
| Comparative depth | Factorium exceeds the thesaurus by at least two dimensions on at least 4/6 packets |
| Route cost | Factorium is never more than one result-open slower |
| Semantic custody | Zero false equivalences |

**Confirmation-bias check:** Factorium's schema makes governing fields easy to
count. A field earns credit only when the frozen visible output connects it to
the packet; headings, metadata volume, and implied expertise earn none.

## Falsification tests

### Test 1 — exact retained-capture replay

- Method: score the immutable Factorium and Merriam-Webster captures retained
  by CAD-01 against six predeclared consequence packets.
- Measure: five separate binary dimensions and result-open cost; no weighted
  aggregate.
- Falsification threshold: any conjunctive campaign threshold fails.
- Timeframe: immediate repository run.

### Test 2 — shuffled internal replay

- Method: re-score product labels and packet order concealed, reconcile every
  disagreement against exact retained text, and preserve both passes.
- Measure: disagreement register and final binary observations.
- Falsification threshold: unresolved disagreement or changed capture.
- Timeframe: same run.

### Test 3 — later paired-reader task

- Method: real readers use a thesaurus and Factorium on matched consequence
  questions.
- Measure: correct boundary choice, unsupported transfer rate, completion time,
  and confidence calibration.
- Falsification threshold: no preregistered improvement or worse unsupported
  transfer on the reader sample.
- Timeframe: later; no external readers are currently available.

Passing Tests 1 and 2 would reject “merely a schematized thesaurus” only for
the tested artifact outputs. It would not establish that people notice, value,
understand, prefer, or return to the difference.
