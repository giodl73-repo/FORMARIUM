# Route Choice Inertia Analysis

## Workaround map

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Workaround name | Search-and-stitch reference chain |
| How it works | Search the ordinary phrase; open a dictionary, thesaurus, encyclopedia, handbook, documentation, or forum; copy useful terms into another search; reconcile pages mentally or in notes; stop when the immediate task feels answerable |
| Who does it | Individual learners, practitioners, writers, analysts, managers, and hobbyists |
| Frequency | Unknown; no reader or usage evidence exists |
| Implicit cost | Unquantified; synthetic personas cannot supply time or monetary estimates |
| Hidden cost | Sense collapse, missing denominator or context, incompatible authorities, forgotten assumptions, unsupported inference, and no durable trace of why the pieces were combined |

## Switching costs

| Cost type | Estimate | Who bears it |
|---|---|---|

| Process change | Medium because the reader must adopt explicit sense, context, boundary, and stop checks | Reader |

| Total switching cost | MEDIUM as a product hypothesis, not a measured estimate | Reader |

Switching-cost verdict: **MEDIUM**. The site is mechanically accessible, but
the reference method asks for a new cognitive habit.

## Inertia threat

**HIGH.** There is no evidence that existing tool chains are failing badly
enough for real readers to seek a replacement. Their components are familiar,
fast, broad, and often authoritative within a domain.

## Conditions for inertia to lose

1. The question spans several meanings or sources and the existing chain makes
   a consequential distinction, assumption, or stopping boundary easy to miss.
2. Factorium exposes a useful first route from the reader's ordinary phrasing
   without requiring prior knowledge of canonical headwords.
3. Factorium makes it easy to stop or hand outward, so the cost of trying it is
   lower than manually reconciling several pages.

The closest condition today is the second: `sim-42` has broad search and
problem-led mechanics, but ordinary-language route choice remains unobserved.
