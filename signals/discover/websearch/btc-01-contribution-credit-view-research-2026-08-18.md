# BTC-01 Contribution and Credit View Research

Date: 2026-08-18
Method: established-source web review following the frozen BTC-01 baseline

## Design findings

1. NISO CRediT supplies a bounded example of representing contribution through
   explicit roles. Its fourteen roles describe contributions to scholarly
   outputs, and its own guidance says the taxonomy is not designed to determine
   authorship. Factorium should therefore record a source-declared contribution
   vocabulary without universalizing CRediT or converting roles into credit.
2. W3C PROV separates entities, activities, agents, generation, use,
   derivation, attribution, delegation, and revision. This supports an exact
   source/activity/agent chain while leaving truth and causal conclusions to
   separate evidence.
3. Library of Congress primary-source guidance treats sources as incomplete
   pieces requiring further evidence and interpretation. A historical credit
   row therefore needs omissions, contradictions, perspective, and unresolved
   status rather than one authoritative biography field.
4. The existing Factorium Causal Claim Evidence Table already separates actual-
   case attribution from responsibility, blame, liability, and policy. The new
   view must link to that owner instead of using `credit` as a synonym for
   causal attribution.

## Boundary

These sources support a reusable evidence-record design. They do not establish
one universal contribution taxonomy, decide authorship, assign deservedness,
settle historical priority, validate a biography, or show reader value.
