## Key findings

1. Lippert, Kühnen, and Wagenknecht proposed formography as "the detailed
   study of forms in (and of) practice" through ethnographic and STS inquiry:
   https://tu-dresden.de/gsw/phil/iso/technosozial/forschung/forms
2. No peer-reviewed publication using that exact field name was confirmed.
3. Novak and Cañas explicitly define concept maps through concepts, linking
   words, relationships, and hierarchy:
   https://cmap.ihmc.us/docs/origins.php
4. Ranganathan's faceted classification is required historical prior art:
   https://doaj.org/article/fce7935674da4458a765d043566510cc
5. ISO Topic Maps define scope as the context within which a statement is
   valid:
   https://www.isotopicmaps.org/sam/sam-model/2008-06-03/
6. Caroline Levine's *Forms* studies wholes, rhythms, hierarchies, and networks
   across culture, politics, and knowledge:
   https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691173436/forms
