# Disposition, Power, and Designed Function

Status: noncanonical candidate entry draft

[Admission brief](../literature/disposition-power-designed-function-candidate-brief-2026-08-20.md)

## Orientation

This entry distinguishes a realizable propensity in a bearer from a function
attributed by a design or selected-effect account. Trigger and manifestation
describe the propensity; they do not create another sense. The entry does not
make every resource, skill, executable, or role a disposition. The question is
what kind of realizable explanatory relation is at issue, not whether a system
can presently produce an outcome.

## Sense table

| Sense ID | Governing question | Core |

| `realizable-disposition` | Which bearer has what stable, realizable propensity under what manifestation conditions? | bearer-grounded propensity |
| `designed-function` | Which activity or effect is attributed to an artifact or component under which design account? | design-relative function |

## Root factorization

Factor bearer, grounding basis, trigger, enabling and blocking conditions,
manifestation, time, and evidence. For designed function also factor designer or
design account, intended contribution, system boundary, and failure criterion.
These are roles in an account, not additional senses.

## Decisive distinctions

A disposition can exist unmanifested; its manifestation is not another
disposition or sense. A designed function explains a component's contribution
under a design account, whereas
engineering capability, operational capacity, software or mathematical
function, and organizational competency retain their established owners. A
cause, probability, or observed regularity alone does not establish a
disposition or function.

## Dependencies and stopping boundary

Require an identified bearer and a bounded account of conditions. Route resource
limits to operational resource/capacity, work capability to organization/role,
and formal mappings or executable functions to their mathematical or software
owners. Do not introduce legal capacity, authority, entitlement, or a
`grounding-condition` sense: grounding is a factor.

## Selection procedure

First ask whether the claim concerns a standing realizable feature rather than
an event. If yes, ask whether a design account, rather than mere usefulness,
licenses function attribution. Record trigger and blocker evidence; otherwise
route to causal reasoning, evaluation, or the applicable domain entry.

## Reference Delta

Adds a bounded cross-domain vocabulary for realizable dispositions and
design-relative function. It treats manifestation conditions as factors and
does not alter operational, organizational, legal, mathematical, or software
records.

## Failure signs

Reject a draft that infers a disposition from one success, treats a function as
intrinsic without a design account, or turns enabling conditions into senses.
Reject claims of legal capacity or authority.
