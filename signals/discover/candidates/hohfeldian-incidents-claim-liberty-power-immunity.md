# Hohfeldian Claim, Liberty, Power, Immunity, Duty, No-Claim, Liability, and Disability

Status: noncanonical candidate entry draft

[Admission brief](../literature/hohfeldian-incidents-candidate-brief-2026-08-20.md)

## Orientation

This is an analytical mapping of correlatives and opposites associated with
Hohfeld. It is not legal advice, a statement of law, or a universal doctrine.
It helps distinguish positions that ordinary “right” language can collapse.

## Sense table

| Sense ID | Governing question | Core |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hohfeld-claim` | Which counterparty owes which correlative duty regarding the stated act or state? | claim-duty position |
| `hohfeld-liberty` | Which act is the subject not under a correlative duty to forgo? | liberty/no-claim position |
| `hohfeld-power` | Which normative position can the subject alter through a recognized act? | alteration power |
| `hohfeld-immunity` | Which attempted normative alteration by another lacks effect on the subject's position? | protection from alteration |
| `hohfeld-duty` | Which conduct is owed to a counterparty under the stated mapping? | correlative duty |
| `hohfeld-no-claim` | Which counterparty lacks a claim that the subject act or refrain? | correlative no-claim |
| `hohfeld-liability` | Which position can another recognized act alter with respect to the subject? | exposure to alteration |
| `hohfeld-disability` | Which normative alteration can the subject not effect because the subject lacks the relevant recognized power? | actor's lack of alteration power |

## Root factorization

Factor subject, counterparty, act or state, source, forum, jurisdiction, time,
recognized act, correlative, opposite, evidence, and remedy context. These
guards prevent the mapping from becoming a free-standing legal conclusion.

## Decisive distinctions

Claim correlates with duty, liberty with no-claim, power with liability, and
immunity with disability. Claim is opposite to no-claim, liberty to duty, power
to disability, and immunity to liability. A liberty is not permission; a power
is not physical ability or operational capacity; an immunity is not a broad
exemption. These are analytic relations whose legal effect depends on source
and forum.

## Dependencies and stopping boundary

Require a stated governing source and applicable forum. Route policy,
authorization, entitlement, obligation, agreement, enforcement, remedy, and
legal interpretation to their existing owners or qualified advice. Do not infer
rights, validity, or outcomes.

## Selection procedure

Identify the alleged relation, then test its correlative and opposite while
retaining source, parties, and time. If a source does not establish the
relation, record uncertainty or route to a policy or legal authority; do not
complete the square by assumption.

## Reference Delta

Adds collision-safe labels for an analytical grid that can clarify, but never
replace, source-bound governance records.

## Failure signs

Failure includes calling every permission a liberty, every capability a power,
or treating Hohfeld's scheme as controlling doctrine in every jurisdiction.
