# Appraisal, Emotion Episode, and Phenomenal Feeling

Status: noncanonical candidate entry draft

[Admission brief](../literature/appraisal-emotion-episode-feeling-candidate-brief-2026-08-20.md)

## Orientation

This candidate separates an evaluative relation, an episode-level emotion
account, and subjective phenomenal feeling. It does not universalize emotion
categories or own affect as an umbrella, enduring mood, causal mechanism, or
clinical diagnosis.

## Sense table

| Sense ID | Governing question | Core |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `affective-appraisal` | How is a situation evaluated relative to concerns, goals, norms, or coping possibilities? | concern-relative evaluation |
| `emotion-episode` | Which bounded episode links an eliciting situation, appraisal, action tendency, expression, and reported experience? | episode account |
| `phenomenal-feeling` | What subjective felt character is reported or otherwise evidenced in the episode? | reported experience |

## Root factorization

Factor subject, situation, concern, appraisal dimensions, temporal extent,
physiology, expression, action tendency, report, culture, context, and
evidence. Affect and mood are context and routing factors, not senses here;
generic psychological mood has no current owner.

## Decisive distinctions

Appraisal is an evaluative relation and need not be consciously felt. An emotion
episode is a bounded explanatory pattern, not merely a word label. Phenomenal
feeling is first-person or carefully evidenced experience, not an inference
from facial expression. Mood is typically diffuse and enduring. Attributed mood
of an expression, work, performance, or place routes to the `felt-mood` sense
of `stance-tone-voice-mood-atmosphere`; a reported clinical mood can be a
`clinical-symptom`. Generic psychological mood remains explicitly unowned.

## Dependencies and stopping boundary

Require source, subject, context, and evidence type. Route generic affect and
perception, motives, causal claims, evaluations, attributed mood, and clinical
symptoms to their current owners. Do not route generic psychological mood to a
narrower owner or make claims about culture-universal basic emotions.

## Selection procedure

First identify whether the source describes evaluation, an episode, or felt
experience. Then record the evidence and competing theory; use no category
label as proof of a shared physiology or expression.

## Reference Delta

Adds a disciplined bridge between evaluative and experiential descriptions
without absorbing mood or a general affect ontology.

## Failure signs

Failure includes inferring feeling from behavior alone, treating appraisal as a
settled causal mechanism, or presenting one cultural emotion vocabulary as
universal.
