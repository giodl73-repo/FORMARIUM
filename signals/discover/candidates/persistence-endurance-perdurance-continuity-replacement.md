# Persistence, Endurance, Perdurance, Continuity, and Replacement

Status: noncanonical candidate entry draft

[Admission brief](../literature/persistence-endurance-perdurance-continuity-replacement-candidate-brief-2026-08-20.md)

## Orientation

This entry records rival ways of accounting for apparent sameness through
change, continuity evidence, and replacement. It does not decide the canonical
identity of an entity and preserves endurantist and perdurantist disagreement.

## Sense table

| Sense ID | Governing question | Core |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `persistence-account` | Under which account is one continuing subject said to remain through a temporal interval? | time-spanning account |
| `endurance-account` | Does the account treat the whole subject as present at each relevant time? | wholly-present theory |
| `perdurance-account` | Does the account treat the subject as temporally extended through temporal parts or stages? | temporal-parts theory |
| `continuity-evidence` | Which tracked continuities support, without proving, a persistence claim? | defeasible linkage evidence |
| `replacement-account` | Which part or role is replaced, under what continuity and boundary conditions? | substitution through change |

## Root factorization

Factor subject, time interval, account, temporal relation, material or
organizational continuity, relevant changes, replacement scope, authority, and
evidence. Identity is a dependency and outcome to be evaluated elsewhere, not a
sense here.

## Decisive distinctions

Persistence is the umbrella explanatory question. Endurance and perdurance are
competing accounts, not jointly true data fields. Continuity can support either
account but does not entail identity. Replacement concerns bounded substitution;
it is not automatically loss of identity, nor proof that a successor is the
same entity.

## Dependencies and stopping boundary

Require temporal indexing and a declared account. Route identifier assignment,
versioning, lineage, and canonical entity identity to
`identity-naming-classification-versioning`. Do not settle metaphysical
controversies or add a universal identity test.

## Selection procedure

State the change and interval; identify whether the source asserts endurance,
perdurance, neutral persistence, evidence of continuity, or replacement. Keep
competing analyses side by side and mark missing evidence rather than forcing a
verdict.

## Reference Delta

Adds account-relative persistence vocabulary around, but not instead of,
canonical entity identity and change lineage.

## Failure signs

Failure signs include treating continuity as identity proof, presenting one
theory as settled, or converting every repair and version change into
replacement.
